## What an authorized dealer is

Every dealer listed below has completed the VDS certification program, signed the current dealer agreement, and maintains active insurance and licensing in their state of operation. Authorized dealers can sell, install, support, and service VDS mobile units under the manufacturer warranty.

Dealers shown here are listed by their primary business address. Many cover regions broader than their home state — verify coverage with the individual dealer or use the territory filter below.

## States without an active dealer

If your state does not have a dealer listed, VDS will route your inquiry to the direct sales team. Direct customers get the same products, the same SOC, and the same support — the difference is that there is no regional dealer in the middle. Direct contracts are signed with VDS in Pikesville.

## How to choose between two dealers in your region

When two dealers serve your region:

- **For complex multi-site deployments** — choose the dealer with the highest tier (Strategic Partner > Tier 3 > Tier 2 > Tier 1), reflecting deployment volume experience
- **For single-site or small-volume deployments** — choose by responsiveness and local proximity
- **For specialty verticals** (e.g., energy infrastructure, modular construction) — choose the dealer whose specialty filter matches
- **If unsure** — contact VDS directly and we will route based on territory, capability match, and current pipeline load
