## Why financing matters in this category

A mobile surveillance unit is a multi-thousand-dollar piece of equipment paired with an ongoing monitoring service. Customers buy it for projects that may run 6 months, 18 months, or 5 years. Capital expense, operating expense, project-billable, and renewable revenue treatment all matter to the way customers structure the purchase. VDS supports four primary financing structures, each appropriate to a different customer situation.

## Option 1: Standard service plan (no separate financing)

The default VDS offering is a service plan: a monthly fee covering hardware use, monitoring, software, support, and unit replacement. No separate capital purchase, no separate financing.

- Sky Guard MSU: $2,200/month
- Swift Deploy: $1,500/month
- Boundary Guard Pro: $1,800/month

Terms are typically 12 months minimum, month-to-month thereafter. No down payment. No early-termination penalty after the 12-month commitment. Most customers stay on this structure for the life of the deployment because it is the simplest accounting treatment — pure OpEx, no asset on the balance sheet, no depreciation schedule.

## Option 2: Multi-year service agreement with locked pricing

For customers planning 3+ year deployments, the multi-year agreement locks pricing for the contract term and includes built-in service and replacement guarantees.

- **3-year term** — pricing locked, modest discount versus month-to-month
- **5-year term** — pricing locked, larger discount, dedicated account manager

The discount structure is not published on the public site. Specific numbers are in the dealer pricing reference and on the customer quote. Talk to sales or your dealer for current rates.

The multi-year agreement is most common in:
- Energy and infrastructure deployments with long project timelines
- Public-sector-adjacent customers with multi-year budget cycles
- Property management firms with multi-site fleets

## Option 3: Operating lease through a VDS finance partner

For customers who want to treat the equipment as a leased capital asset, VDS works with established commercial leasing partners to offer 36, 48, and 60-month operating leases. The lease covers the hardware; monitoring and software run on a separate monthly service contract.

Benefits:
- Off-balance-sheet treatment for the equipment under most accounting frameworks
- Predictable monthly payment for budgeting
- End-of-term options: renew, return, or purchase at fair market value
- Tax treatment: monthly lease payment typically deductible as operating expense (verify with your CPA)

Approval is handled by the finance partner, not by VDS. Credit decisions typically come back within 5 business days of application. Documentation is standard commercial lease paperwork.

This option is most common with:
- Construction firms that prefer leased equipment on jobsites
- Modular construction and rental firms integrating units into their own customer offerings
- Mid-market companies with established commercial banking relationships

## Option 4: Project-based contracts

For customers on a single project — particularly construction GCs with a defined project end date — VDS offers project-based contracts that align deployment cost to project duration.

- Minimum project duration: 3 months
- Pricing per project month, with discounts at 6, 12, and 18-month durations
- Single mobilization and demobilization fee
- Project completion triggers contract close — no auto-renewal

This is the structure used in the hero case study (Mid-Atlantic GC, 12-month project, $340,800 saved against prior-year baseline).

## Dealer-supported financing

VDS dealers may offer additional financing structures specific to their business model — for example, a regional integrator might bundle VDS units into a broader site-security package with their own financing terms. If you are working through a dealer, ask the dealer about their financing options first; if their structure does not fit, VDS direct financing options remain available.

## What we do not finance

- **Initial site assessment and design fees**, where applicable — these are billed up front
- **Custom hardware modifications** beyond standard configurations — billed up front
- **Pass-through third-party services** (cellular data overage, custom integrations) — billed at cost on the standard monthly invoice
