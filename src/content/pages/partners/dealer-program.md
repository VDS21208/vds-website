## Margin structure

VDS uses a published, tiered margin structure based on annual deployment volume. Tier reviews happen quarterly. You move up when you hit volume; you do not get downgraded mid-year for being below pace.

**Tier 1 (12–35 units/year):** Standard dealer margin on hardware MSRP, standard monitoring share on monthly recurring revenue, deal registration eligible.

**Tier 2 (36–99 units/year):** Increased hardware margin, increased monitoring share, priority deal registration, quarterly MDF.

**Tier 3 (100+ units/year):** Top-tier margin, top-tier monitoring share, dedicated account team, custom MDF programs, eligibility for Strategic Partner conversion.

Specific margin percentages are published in the dealer program guide. We do not list them on the public website because pricing is part of the dealer agreement. The guide is available on application.

## Deal registration

VDS operates a deal registration system. When you bring a qualified opportunity to VDS — name, scope, timeline — you have first right of refusal on that deal for 90 days, renewable on documented progress. Registered deals are protected from channel conflict; we will not sell direct into a registered opportunity, and we will not route the lead to another dealer.

Disputes are handled by the channel manager and escalated to the Director of Sales if needed. We have published the dispute process in the dealer agreement.

## What VDS provides to dealers

- **Lead routing.** Inbound leads in your active territory get routed to you. Average inbound volume varies by territory; channel manager will share territory-specific data on application.
- **Sales support.** Channel manager available for discovery calls, scoping, and pricing. Engineering support for complex deployments.
- **Technical training.** 2-day Pikesville certification covering hardware installation, VMS configuration, and SOC handoff. Refresher training annually. Travel reimbursed for Tier 2 and Tier 3 dealers.
- **Marketing development funds (MDF).** Quarterly accrual at Tier 2 and above, usable on co-branded campaigns, trade show attendance, and lead generation. Documentation required.
- **Co-branded marketing assets.** Spec sheets, customer-facing decks, ROI calculators, and email templates with your branding alongside VDS.
- **Direct line to the SOC.** Dealers have a priority line for customer escalations. You do not wait in the standard support queue.

## What VDS expects from dealers

- Meet your tier's annual volume commitment
- Complete certification before installing or scoping VDS units
- Use VDS-supplied marketing materials accurately (no rewrites that create misleading claims)
- Pass through manufacturer warranty terms to end customers
- Maintain general liability and professional liability insurance at published minimums
- Sign and adhere to the dealer agreement, including non-circumvention provisions on registered deals

## How territory works

VDS does not grant exclusive territories. We do operate a "first dealer of record" model: when a customer in a geography has been won by a dealer, that customer is the dealer's account for renewal and expansion. New customers in the same geography are routed by inbound interest, dealer pipeline strength, and program tier.

Strategic Partners may have agreed-upon exclusivity in specific verticals (e.g., a national modular construction firm) — negotiated separately from the standard dealer program.
