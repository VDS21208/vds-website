## Before you apply, check fit

The dealer program is built for established businesses with existing customer relationships in security, construction services, or adjacent industries. We are not the right partner for first-time entrepreneurs without an installed base, lead-gen affiliates, or firms outside our target verticals.

**Strong fit looks like:**
- Security integrator selling cameras, access control, or alarm systems to commercial customers
- Construction services firm offering jobsite security, fencing, or trailer rentals
- Specialty rental firm in the energy, infrastructure, or events space
- Existing fleet of 100+ commercial customers in our segments
- Annual capacity for 12+ unit deployments
- Insurance, licensing, and operating history in good standing

**Not a fit (yet):**
- Lead-generation firms with no install or service capability
- Pre-revenue startups without a customer base
- Affiliates seeking referral-only compensation (see referral program separately)
- Firms outside the US (VDS sells in the United States only as of 2026)

## What the application asks

The form below collects:

1. **Company information** — legal name, DBA, address, year founded, employee count
2. **Business background** — segments served, primary customer types, geographic territory, annual revenue range
3. **Capabilities** — install capability, field service team, monitoring relationships if any
4. **Volume estimate** — projected first-year deployment count, target customer profile
5. **Compliance posture** — insurance carriers, licenses held, NDAA-relevant procurement (if any)
6. **Contacts** — primary owner/exec, channel sponsor, technical lead, billing contact
7. **Why VDS** — brief statement on why VDS fits your portfolio

The application takes about 20 minutes. We do not require attachments at this stage; we will request insurance certificates and licensing documentation if the application moves forward.

## What happens after submission

- **Day 1:** Confirmation email with channel team contact
- **Day 2–5:** Internal review and territory check
- **Day 5:** Decision email. Approved applications get a 30-minute intro call scheduled. Declined applications get the reason in writing.
- **Day 6–10:** Intro call, dealer agreement preview, NDA exchange if requested
- **Day 11–20:** Agreement signed
- **Day 21–30:** Certification scheduled, lead routing activated

## Frequently asked before applying

Most applicants ask three questions before submitting. Answers below; full FAQ on the program page.

**1. What is the upfront cost?** No franchise fee. No territory fee. Certification travel is dealer-paid at Tier 1; reimbursed at Tier 2 and above.

**2. How exclusive is my territory?** Not exclusive in the franchise sense. See dealer program page for the "first dealer of record" model.

**3. Can I see margin before applying?** Margin percentages are in the dealer program guide, available under NDA after intro call.
