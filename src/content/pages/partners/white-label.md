## What white label means at VDS

White labeling at VDS is not a sticker on a chassis. It is a manufacturing and operations integration that lets a high-volume partner sell VDS technology under their own brand without exposing VDS as the upstream provider — while still benefiting from VDS engineering, VMS, and SOC operations.

**On the hardware side:** Chassis paint, vinyl wrap, and weatherproof badging in your colors and logo. Custom signage and contact information. Optional custom mounting hardware for specific use cases. We do not white-label internal components; the engineering remains standard VDS and remains under our warranty and support.

**On the software side:** VMS branding (logo, color palette, customer-facing labels, mobile app icon). Custom domain (yourcompany.cloud.example) with SSL. Optional custom user invitation emails and customer notifications under your brand.

**On the operations side:** Custom SOC operator script that addresses your customer in your brand voice, custom call-out language during talk-down events, custom escalation procedures aligned to your customer agreements.

**On the contract side:** Your customer signs a contract with you, not with VDS. VDS is the unnamed subcontractor under your master agreement.

## What does not get white-labeled

- **Underlying engineering documentation.** Component data sheets, certifications, NDAA attestations remain VDS-branded. Customers who ask for this documentation see VDS authorship.
- **SOC 2 and security audit reports.** These are issued to VDS as the underlying processor.
- **AWS hosting.** The platform is on AWS under VDS's account. Custom AWS account hosting is available at the strategic-partner level with separate pricing.
- **Regulatory compliance attestations.** NDAA, FCC, and other regulatory documents reference VDS as the manufacturer of record.

For customers who ask "who actually makes this," your sales staff will be trained on the standard disclosure language. We do not require you to lie about the upstream relationship; the program is about brand presentation, not concealment.

## Who this is for

The white-label program is built for:

- **National security integrators** deploying mobile units across multiple regions
- **Modular construction and trailer rental firms** integrating surveillance into a broader rental offering
- **Specialty service firms** in energy, infrastructure, or events with established brand equity
- **Private label resellers** with existing customer relationships in our target verticals

Minimum annual volume: 100 units. Below that, the standard dealer program with co-branding options is the better fit.

## Commercial structure

- **Custom unit pricing** tied to annual volume commitment, separate from standard dealer pricing
- **Custom MRR share** on monitoring services
- **Tooling and setup fees** for one-time chassis customization, VMS branding setup, and SOC script development — typically amortized over the volume commitment
- **Annual minimum** committed in writing; below-commitment performance triggers pricing review at renewal

Specific pricing is negotiated. Expect the conversation to involve volume forecasts, geography, customer profile, and your existing operations.

## Onboarding timeline

White-label onboarding takes 60–90 days from contract signature, depending on customization scope:

- **Days 1–14:** Brand asset collection, design review, VMS customization spec
- **Days 15–30:** Chassis design proof, VMS staging environment setup
- **Days 31–60:** First production units, SOC operator script development and certification
- **Days 61–90:** First customer deployment under your brand

## What we expect from white-label partners

- A real brand and a real customer base — this is not a vehicle for arbitrage or quick reselling
- A capable sales and support organization that can stand behind a customer-facing brand
- Adherence to VDS quality and operational standards in customer communications
- A multi-year volume commitment
