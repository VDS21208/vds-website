## Sales assets

**VDS company overview deck** (PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides). 18 slides covering the company, the integrated stack, the SOC, the case data, and the pricing tiers. Co-branded version available with your logo on the title slide and section dividers.

**Product spec sheets** — Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro. Each one is a 2-page PDF with photography, specifications, deployment requirements, and the standard service plan. Co-branded versions available.

**Vertical solution briefs** — construction, perimeter, lot and yard, energy and infrastructure, public sector adjacent. Each one is a 4-page PDF mapping the platform to vertical-specific use cases.

**Battle cards** — internal-use comparison cards against the most-encountered competitors in field deployments. Updated quarterly. These are dealer-only; do not share with customers.

## Pricing and ROI tools

**ROI calculator** (Excel and web-based). Inputs: site count, current loss baseline, current guard cost, current vandalism baseline. Outputs: projected 12-month savings, payback period, year-over-year reduction. The hero case study ($340,800 saved on the Mid-Atlantic GC project) was modeled from this calculator with actual customer inputs.

**Pricing reference** — current MSRP, tier-based dealer pricing, monthly monitoring rates, contract terms, available financing options. Updated quarterly. Dealer-only.

**Quote template** — Word document with VDS standard terms and your branding overlay. For dealers who quote outside the VDS CPQ system.

## Marketing assets

**Logo and brand assets** — VDS logos in SVG, PNG, and EPS. Co-marketing logos with VDS + dealer combinations. Brand guideline summary.

**Photography library** — product shots, deployment shots, SOC shots. Web-ready and print-ready resolutions. Licensed for dealer use in marketing within the dealer agreement.

**Email templates** — six approved templates for cold outreach, follow-up after a demo, post-deployment check-in, renewal, and case-study reference outreach. Co-branded versions auto-generated from the portal.

**Social media kit** — LinkedIn post templates, image cards, and short-form video clips for organic social. Updated monthly.

**Trade show kit** — booth graphics, pull-up banner artwork, handout one-pagers, and badge scanner integration. Co-branded design available with 10-business-day turnaround.

## Technical and operational resources

**Installation guides** — current revision installation procedures for each unit. PDF and video.

**Service and maintenance manuals** — quarterly inspection procedures, fault diagnosis flowcharts, parts ordering process.

**VMS configuration guides** — how to provision a new customer tenant, set up users with RBAC, integrate third-party cameras, configure alerting and SOC handoff.

**Certification curriculum** — pre-read material for the 2-day Pikesville certification. Required reading before in-person training.

## Marketing development funds (MDF) toolkit

For Tier 2 and Tier 3 dealers with MDF eligibility:

- MDF request form and approval workflow
- Approved campaign templates (digital, print, event)
- MDF reporting and reconciliation requirements
- Co-marketing approval process and design review SLAs

## How to access

Active dealers receive portal credentials at certification completion. If you have lost access or need additional team members added, contact your channel manager directly or email **partners@visiondetectionsystems.com** with subject "Portal access — [Dealer Name]."

If you are not yet a dealer, the resources above are not publicly available. The public site does have product information at the platform and product pages — but the sales toolkit, pricing, and battle cards are dealer-only by policy.
