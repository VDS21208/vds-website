## The challenge: an audit posture during construction, then forever after

A data center is the most audit-bound private asset in commercial real estate. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 control families both require physical security evidence — perimeter monitoring, access logging, incident response, and retention. The interior security program is typically extensive: man traps, biometrics, cage-level access logs. The exterior — fence line, transformer yard, employee parking, and the active construction phase — is the harder problem.

Construction phase is the highest-risk window. The site is large, the equipment laydown is valuable (copper, transformers, switchgear, generators), the building is not yet energized, and the long-term fixed surveillance system isn't online. Most operators contract a guard program for this phase. The cost is high and the evidence record is thin.

## SOC 2 and ISO 27001 evidence posture

- **CC6.4 physical access:** continuous monitored perimeter with verified dispatch
- **CC7.2 system operations:** 24/7 monitored, <60-second escalation, full incident documentation
- **A.11.1.4 ISO physical security:** documented perimeter monitoring with retention
- **A.12.4 logging and monitoring:** immutable 12-month audit log, RBAC, MFA
- **Retention:** 30/60/90-day cloud, extendable for legal hold
- **Encryption:** TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest
- **Data residency:** US-only AWS infrastructure
- **Supply chain:** NDAA Section 889 compliant across the entire device fleet

Our auditor-facing documentation package maps each deployed control to the specific SOC 2 / ISO 27001 control families and provides the evidence format the audit team expects.
