## The challenge in healthcare and adjacent hospitality

Hospital parking decks see assault, vehicle theft, and staff-targeted incidents at rates that don't appear on the inpatient dashboard. ER approach lanes attract loitering, vehicle dumping, and behavioral crises that arrive before triage. Behavioral health facilities need perimeter awareness without turning the patient-facing environment into a surveillance state. Adjacent hotels — the ones contracted for traveling nurses or out-of-town families — share the same parking exposure.

VDS does not do clinical-interior surveillance. We do not install cameras in patient rooms, corridors, treatment areas, waiting rooms, or anywhere care is delivered. The product is exterior boundary only: parking lots, perimeters, ER vehicle approaches, loading docks, and adjacent hospitality lots that share the same risk profile.

## How VDS protects healthcare and adjacent hospitality sites

Each unit is sited on the exterior with sightlines reviewed against the facility's clinical footprint. Privacy zones mask building entrances where patient identity could be inferred, and retention defaults to the shortest setting the facility's risk team will accept — typically 30 days for parking lot footage of patient arrivals.

AI VisionStream runs on AWS with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Access logs are exportable for the privacy officer. Footage requests follow the facility's release-of-information process, not ours — we do not push video to third parties without written facility approval.

When motion crosses an active zone after hours, the US-based SOC verifies on live video within 60 seconds. False-alarm rate runs at roughly 3%. Verified events route to facility security per the escalation tree the facility specifies — internal radio, contracted patrol, or local PD.

Typical use cases:
- **Hospital and clinic parking decks and surface lots** — assault prevention, vehicle theft, vandalism
- **ER approach lanes** — loitering, vehicle dumping, after-hours arrivals
- **Behavioral health perimeters** — elopement awareness on the exterior fence line
- **Loading docks and pharmacy delivery zones** — vendor verification, controlled-substance delivery windows
- **Adjacent hospitality lots** — extended-stay hotels and hospital-contracted lodging serving the same staff and patient base
