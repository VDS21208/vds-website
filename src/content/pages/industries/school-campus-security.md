## The challenge in campus security

K-12 facilities directors are managing a security spend that is trending up year-over-year heading into the 2026 budget cycle, with most of the increase landing on exterior coverage — parking lots, athletic complexes, bus depots, and construction zones — rather than classroom interiors. Higher-ed campus security directors face a different mix: stadium parking on game days, perimeter parking lots with after-hours risk, and rolling construction projects that exceed the existing camera footprint.

Hardwired exterior cameras require trenching across athletic fields, conduit through structural concrete, and an IT integration that lags the threat by 9–18 months. VDS deploys in one site visit, redeploys when the calendar changes, and demobilizes when a project closes.

## How VDS protects campus sites

Each deployment starts with a sightline walk reviewed against the district or university's privacy posture. Privacy zones mask classroom windows, locker-room exteriors, and any sightline where individual student identification serves no security purpose. Cameras are sited for vehicle, vehicle-occupant, and after-hours pedestrian activity — not for monitoring students during the school day.

AI VisionStream runs on AWS with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. The US-based SOC verifies events on live video within 60 seconds and follows the district's escalation tree — school resource officer, district security, or local PD. False-alarm rate runs at roughly 3% across deployed sites.

K-12 use cases:
- **Parking lots and bus depots** — vehicle theft, vandalism, after-hours intrusion
- **Athletic complexes and concession stands** — vandalism, copper theft, equipment shed protection
- **Construction zones on campus** — additions, roof replacements, HVAC plant work
- **Weekend and summer break coverage** — when the building is empty and the existing camera system is the only thing watching

Higher-ed use cases:
- **Perimeter and overflow parking lots** — vehicle break-ins on commuter lots
- **Stadium and event parking** — game-day surge coverage with rapid demobilization
- **Construction sites** — residence hall builds, lab additions, athletic facility projects
- **Remote campus assets** — agricultural research stations, observatories, satellite sites

## Compliance and procurement

VDS hardware is NDAA Section 889 compliant — required for most federal-funded education spend, including ESSER and Title IV. FERPA-adjacent framing applies to any incidental student identification in footage: access is logged, retention is policy-driven, and release follows the district's records process. Title IX-relevant footage requests are handled through the institution's compliance office, not VDS.

Available through Sourcewell, GSA, COSTARS, BuyBoard, and most state cooperative purchasing contracts. Month-to-month after initial term — useful for districts that don't want to commit reserve dollars to a multi-year capital lease.
