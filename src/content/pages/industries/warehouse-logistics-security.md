## The Warehouse & Logistics Security Problem

<p>Cargo theft costs the US logistics industry over $15 billion annually — and loading docks are the primary point of entry for both external theft and internal shrinkage operations. Warehouses with multiple bays, large outdoor staging areas, and high-value inventory require surveillance covering the full operation, not just the main entrance.</p>

<p>The most costly theft patterns in logistics involve coordinated groups — often including insider information — targeting specific trailers or dock windows. By the time missing inventory is discovered during a count, footage may already be overwritten and the trail cold. <a href="https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/violent-crime/cac" rel="nofollow">FBI cargo theft data</a> shows that facilities without active monitoring experience theft incidents at 3x the rate of continuously monitored operations.</p>

<p>Standard solutions fail because fixed cameras create blind spots, guards cost $18,000–$36,000 monthly for 24/7 coverage, and basic CCTV systems generate too many false alarms to maintain effective response protocols. Most warehouse security incidents occur during shift changes and after-hours periods when coverage gaps are largest.</p>
