## The Retail & Shopping Center Security Problem

<p>Organized retail crime (ORC) has evolved into coordinated operations targeting everything from pharmacy shelves to loading dock deliveries. Traditional store security — LP officers and basic CCTV — addresses incidents after they happen. VDS shifts that equation by creating a visible, active deterrence layer at parking lots, loading areas, and perimeter access points that ORC groups specifically avoid.</p>

<p>Retail property managers face dual pressure: rising crime rates and rising security labor costs. For national retail chains and shopping center operators, the inability to standardize response across a portfolio — with consistent documentation, reporting, and evidence quality — creates compounding liability exposure with every incident. <a href="https://nrf.com/research/national-retail-security-survey-2024" rel="nofollow">The National Retail Federation reports that ORC incidents increased 26.5% in 2023</a>, with average losses per incident reaching $1.1 million for organized theft rings.</p>

<p>Standard security approaches fail because they're reactive. Basic CCTV captures footage for review after theft occurs. Security guards patrol fixed routes that sophisticated ORC groups easily circumvent. Neither solution provides the real-time detection and verified response needed to stop organized crime before it escalates. The result: repeated targeting, escalating losses, and frustrated LP teams managing incidents instead of preventing them.</p>



## How VDS Solves It

<p>VDS solves both simultaneously — replacing expensive guard positions with AI-monitored surveillance that costs less per month than a single part-time LP officer's wages, while providing 24/7 coverage that guards can't match.
