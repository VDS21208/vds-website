## The economics carriers already know

Construction theft and vandalism losses crossed $1B annually in the US in 2024 by NICB tracking — and that's the reported, claimed figure, not the full operational loss. Builder's risk, commercial property, inland marine, and fleet lines all carry the exposure. Auto dealership open-lot coverage is its own conversation.

Most carriers' risk engineering teams already have a position on "site monitoring credit" — what they don't always have is a vendor that produces the documentation their underwriters will actually accept at portfolio scale.

VDS works with carriers and brokers to fill that gap.

## What carriers and brokers get from a VDS partnership

### Documented site monitoring evidence

Every VDS deployment produces a verifiable monitoring footprint: deployment date, trailer GPS location, SOC monitoring confirmation, AI VisionStream analytics record, alert log, audio talk-down event count, LE dispatch records. This is the documentation underwriters need to approve a premium credit and that claims adjusters use during loss investigation.

### A vendor your risk engineering team can field-validate

Risk engineers can run a deployment pilot at a representative insured site — same 30-day structure as our standard pilot — and validate that the operational performance matches the underwriting assumption. Field-validated, not assumed.

### NDAA Section 889 compliance for federal-adjacent insureds

Carriers writing federal contractors, defense suppliers, or grant-funded infrastructure projects need vendors whose hardware clears Section 889. VDS attests to that compliance. AWS US-only data residency closes the loop for carriers with data sovereignty requirements.

### Single-vendor coverage across the 30+ state footprint

Insureds operate across state lines. A carrier program that names VDS gets one vendor, one MSA structure, one SOC, and consistent monitoring evidence regardless of which state any given insured site sits in.

## The buyer profiles we work with

### Carrier risk engineering teams

National accounts and construction practice risk engineers at carriers including (we work with carriers comparable to) Travelers, Zurich, Liberty Mutual, AIG, Chubb, Sompo, Berkley, and others. The conversation starts with whether your team has a documented monitoring credit on the line you write, and whether VDS can be specified or recommended within that program.

### Wholesale and retail brokers with portfolio exposure

Brokers placing builder's risk for a GC with 40 active projects, or commercial property for a dealer group with 18 lots, or fleet for an interstate logistics operator. VDS becomes the recommended monitoring vendor inside the broker's stewardship program — the insured gets a credit, the broker delivers value, the carrier gets risk reduction documented in the file.

### Captive insurers and risk retention groups

Construction and trade-association captives often have more flexibility to structure monitoring requirements directly into coverage terms. VDS works with captive risk managers to scope program economics.

## What a carrier program partnership looks like operationally

We do not pay referral fees, kickbacks, or commissions. Carrier program partnerships are structured around:

- Documented endorsement or recommendation pathway from carrier or broker to insured
- Standardized deployment package and pricing for program-eligible insureds
- Risk engineering field validation at representative sites
- Monthly portfolio reporting to the carrier or broker on deployed sites, monitoring events, and loss-avoidance documentation where available

Insureds remain the contracting customer of VDS. The carrier or broker drives recommendation, not procurement.
