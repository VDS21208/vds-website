## The Auto Dealership Security Problem

<p>Auto dealerships face a convergence of theft threats: vehicle theft, catalytic converter removal (up 400%+ since 2019), key box theft, and organized groups targeting service lot vehicles overnight. Dealerships with 200–500 vehicles in outdoor lots represent multi-million dollar inventory exposure that a single overnight guard cannot meaningfully protect.</p>

<p>Catalytic converter theft rings specifically target dealerships — multiple vehicles can be hit in a single night by organized groups. A single night of unreported theft can cost $50,000–$200,000 in converter replacements, vehicle damage, and insurance claims. The <a href="https://www.nicb.org/news/news-releases/catalytic-converter-thefts-skyrocketed-over-1000-2021" rel="nofollow">National Insurance Crime Bureau</a> reports that dealership lots are prime targets because of their predictable inventory and limited overnight security presence.</p>

<p>Standard solutions fail because traditional CCTV provides footage after the fact, not real-time intervention. Security guards patrol massive lots with hundreds of blind spots, making it impossible to monitor every vehicle simultaneously. Most dealerships discover theft in the morning, when the evidence trail is cold and the damage is done.</p>
