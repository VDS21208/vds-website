## The problem with substation security

Substations are off-grid by nature — that's their point. They sit at the end of an access road, behind a chain-link fence, and they contain tens of millions of dollars in transformers, switchgear, and copper grounding. Most are unmanned. Most have minimal lighting. Most run on a perimeter intrusion detection system that triggers alarms but doesn't tell you what you're looking at.

Copper theft and vandalism are the dominant loss vectors. NERC CIP-014 requires physical security plans for designated critical substations with documented threat assessments, third-party verification, and ongoing program management. After the 2013 Metcalf incident, the bar moved permanently.

The challenge: most surveillance vendors can't deploy without trenching and a utility tap. At a remote substation, that means building infrastructure for the infrastructure.
