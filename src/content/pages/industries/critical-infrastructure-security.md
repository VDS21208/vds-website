## The challenge: federal frameworks meet remote geography

The CISA 2024 advisory cycle flagged a sharp rise in physical attacks on substations and water treatment facilities — gunfire damage at the Moore County substation, the Las Vegas water utility breach, repeated incidents across the Pacific Northwest grid. Operators are squeezed between two realities: federal frameworks demand specific evidence and access controls, and the assets themselves sit in remote locations with no power, no fiber, and no fence.

NERC CIP-014 requires physical security plans for high-impact assets. TSA Security Directives apply to pipelines and rail. Water utilities operate under AWIA assessments. Telecom carriers answer to CISA. Every framework asks for the same thing: who was there, when, what did they do, and where is the evidence.

## Compliance posture

NDAA Section 889 compliance is baseline — every camera, recorder, and edge device in the fleet. AWS Cloud Partner status means our infrastructure inherits AWS's FedRAMP-aligned controls. SOC 2 Type II controls govern the operations side. For NERC CIP-014 covered assets, our delivery package includes the physical security plan support documentation: deployment maps, monitoring procedures, escalation matrix, incident reporting workflow.

For TSA-regulated pipeline operators under Security Directive Pipeline-2021-02, our evidence packaging maps to the 24-hour incident reporting requirement out of the box.
