## The challenge in hotel security

Hotel GMs and director-of-security roles inherit a property where guest expectations are absolute, insurance carriers ask sharp questions about parking-lot incidents, and the camera system was probably installed during the last renovation. Vehicle break-ins in the back lot show up on TripAdvisor before they show up on the incident log. Valet zones generate disputed-damage claims that go nowhere without timestamped video. Vendor delivery bays at 4 a.m. are a known shrinkage vector that no front-desk associate is watching.

VDS deploys to the exterior — parking lots, valet drop, pool decks after hours, and vendor delivery zones. We do not put cameras in guest hallways, guestroom floors, or anywhere a guest has a reasonable expectation of privacy. The product is parking and perimeter.

## How VDS protects hotel sites

Each unit is sited where guest, vehicle, and vendor traffic intersect: the back lot, the valet stand, the porte-cochere, the pool deck after hours, the loading dock. Sightlines are walked before deployment with privacy zones blocking guestroom windows and any building face where guests are identifiable through a window.

AI VisionStream is camera-agnostic over ONVIF/RTSP — if the property already has interior lobby cameras under a brand standard, those feeds route into the same dashboard. LPR runs at 95%+ accuracy in under 3 seconds for valet vehicle tracking and known-trespass vehicle alerts.

When motion crosses an active zone (back lot after midnight, pool deck post-closing, loading dock outside delivery windows), the SOC verifies on live video within 60 seconds and follows the GM's escalation tree — front desk, night manager, contracted patrol, or local PD. False-alarm rate runs at roughly 3%.

Typical use cases:
- **Guest parking lots** — vehicle break-ins, catalytic converter theft, vehicle theft
- **Valet zones** — pre/post-hand-off damage documentation, key-drop area monitoring
- **After-hours pool decks** — trespass, liability incidents, vandalism
- **Vendor delivery and loading docks** — early-morning shrinkage, vendor verification
- **Porte-cochere and front drive** — guest arrival incidents, drive-off documentation
