## The challenge: cash, target, and regulator — all at once

Cannabis operators carry three problems no other retail category carries at the same intensity. The federal banking situation means cash on premises is the norm, not the exception. The product is high-value, lightweight, and resaleable — making dispensaries among the most-targeted retail formats in the country. And state regulators in every legal jurisdiction prescribe specific video retention, access control, and audit requirements as a license condition.

Inside the dispensary, interior coverage is typically handled by a state-aligned interior CCTV system. The gap is the parking lot, the perimeter, the after-hours zone, and the cultivation facility exterior — exactly where the armed robbery, the smash-and-grab, and the cultivation break-in happen.
