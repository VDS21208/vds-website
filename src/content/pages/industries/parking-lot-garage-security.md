## The Parking Lot & Garage Security Problem

<p>Parking lot crime accounts for over 10% of all US property crime. Vehicle theft, catalytic converter removal, package smash-and-grabs, and assault in parking facilities create significant liability exposure — and traditional guard patrols covering large surface lots or multi-level garages are expensive and riddled with coverage gaps.</p>

<p>Catalytic converter theft has increased over 400% since 2019 and is now one of the most common parking lot crimes according to the <a href="https://www.nicb.org/news/news-releases/nicb-reports-catalytic-converter-theft-increased-more-1000-percent-2021" rel="nofollow">National Insurance Crime Bureau</a>. These crimes happen fast — thieves can remove a catalytic converter in under two minutes with basic tools. Standard security cameras capture footage after the fact, but provide no real-time deterrence or intervention capability.</p>

<p>Traditional security approaches fail parking lot operators. Roving guards cover massive square footage ineffectively, creating predictable patrol gaps that criminals exploit. Fixed camera systems require expensive infrastructure investment, often lack adequate lighting for night identification, and generate alerts that go unmonitored for hours. Property managers need verified response capabilities that actually prevent crimes in progress, not just document losses after they occur.</p>
