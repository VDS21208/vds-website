## The challenge: large yards, federal frameworks, and constant motion

A bus depot covers 5-20 acres. A rail yard covers 50-200. A port terminal can run thousands. Every one of these sites moves equipment day and night and exposes its highest-value assets — locomotives, fuel, copper, vehicles, and intermodal containers — to perimeter risk that fixed cameras can't fully cover.

TSA-regulated passenger rail and transit agencies operate under specific Security Directive frameworks. Port operators answer to DHS and the MTSA. Bus transit operators carry both federal grant compliance and FTA reporting expectations. Every framework asks for documented surveillance, incident response, and evidence retention. Most agencies are running on a combination of fixed cameras at gates and a guard rotation in the yard — neither of which produces a continuous record across the acreage.
