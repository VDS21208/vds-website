## The challenge in government facility security

Government facilities directors are buying surveillance under three pressures at once: NDAA Section 889 prohibits any covered telecom or video equipment from Hikvision, Dahua, Hytera, Huawei, or ZTE, which knocks out a significant share of the commodity camera market. Budget cycles run on the fiscal year, but the threats — copper theft from motor pools, illegal dumping at transfer stations, vandalism at public parks — don't wait for the next CIP. And procurement has to clear cooperative-purchasing rules without a sole-source justification.

VDS is NDAA Section 889 compliant end-to-end, deployable in one site visit, and available on Sourcewell, GSA, COSTARS, BuyBoard, and most state cooperative contracts. Month-to-month operational expense, no capital request, no IT integration timeline.

## How VDS protects government facility sites

Each deployment starts with a sightline walk reviewed against the agency's public-records and privacy posture. Privacy zones mask adjacent private property, residential windows, and any view that creates needless public-records exposure. AI VisionStream runs on AWS GovCloud-adjacent infrastructure with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Access is logged. Footage release follows the agency's public-records process.

The US-based SOC verifies events on live video within 60 seconds and escalates per the facility's tree — facility maintenance staff, agency security, or local PD. False-alarm rate runs at roughly 3%. LPR captures plates at 95%+ accuracy in under 3 seconds, useful for tracking repeat-offender vehicles at transfer stations and motor pools.

Typical use cases:
- **Motor pools and fleet yards** — vehicle theft, fuel theft, catalytic converter theft, after-hours intrusion
- **Public works yards and salt sheds** — equipment theft, copper theft, fuel-tank monitoring
- **Parks and recreation facilities** — after-hours vandalism, illegal dumping, restroom destruction
- **Transfer stations and recycling centers** — illegal dumping after hours, scrap-metal theft, gate-rush incidents
- **Evidence storage exteriors** — perimeter awareness on impound lots and evidence yards (exterior only)
- **Public works construction and capital projects** — water plant upgrades, road project staging areas, sewer expansions
