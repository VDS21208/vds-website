## The Construction Security Problem

<p>Construction sites are the most targeted property type in America. The average jobsite theft costs $150,000–$300,000 accounting for equipment, timeline delays, insurance impact, and subcontractor disruption. The FBI estimates over $1 billion in annual US construction theft — and less than 25% of stolen equipment is ever recovered.</p>

<p>Traditional security approaches — overnight guards and alarm systems — have documented gaps that experienced theft rings exploit. Guards follow predictable patrol routines. Alarms generate so many false triggers that law enforcement deprioritizes response. And by the time a theft is discovered the next morning, the equipment is across state lines.</p>

<p>The fundamental problem is response time. Standard CCTV systems record incidents but don't prevent them. Motion-triggered alarms create false positives that overwhelm security teams. By the time human verification occurs, thieves have already loaded equipment and departed. Construction sites need real-time detection with immediate human verification — not next-day footage review.</p>



## How VDS Solves It

<p>VDS mobile surveillance trailers change the response dynamic entirely. AI VisionStream detects unauthorized entry in real time. SOC operators verify and issue audio challenges within 60 seconds. Police are dispatched with live video — not a next-day police report. Most theft attempts stop at the audio challenge stage, before a single piece of equipment is touched.</p>

<p>The complete workflow: AI VisionStream analyzes video feeds and flags human activity in restricted areas or after-hours movement around equipment. SOC operators receive the alert with live video context within 15 seconds. They verify the threat, issue an audio deterrent through the trailer's PA system, and coordinate police response with real-time video evidence. This verified response model eliminates false alarms while ensuring legitimate incidents receive immediate escalation.</p>
