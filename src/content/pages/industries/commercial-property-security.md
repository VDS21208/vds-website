## The challenge: the gap between construction and stabilization

A Class A office tower or a 300-unit multi-family asset goes through three distinct security phases — and most owners run a different system for each.

**Construction phase:** active job site, materials and tool theft, after-hours intrusion. Loss is documented; insurance carriers expect monitored coverage.

**TCO to lease-up:** the building is finished but the parking garage card system isn't fully populated, the lobby has rotating finish trades, and surface lots are open while the leasing office runs tours. This is the highest-risk window and the one with the least continuous coverage.

**Stabilized operations:** the building has its fixed CCTV but surface parking, loading docks, and rear access roads are still exposed. Multi-family adds resident parking exposure that runs forever.
