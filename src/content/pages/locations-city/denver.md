## Local compliance note

**NDAA Section 889.** Any federal-prime or sub-prime work in Colorado triggers 889. VDS units are NDAA 889 compliant; we publish a written attestation and refresh it annually.

**Cannabis customers.** Colorado MED licensing covers cultivation and retail premises. Our deployments to dispensary parking lots and cultivation perimeters are private-property surveillance; we do not integrate with state seed-to-sale tracking and we do not feed any state agency.

**Colorado wiretap statute (C.R.S. § 18-9-303).** Colorado is a one-party-consent state for audio, but our units capture video only by default unless the customer has a documented business reason and signage.

**High-altitude solar.** Above 7,500 feet, solar yield varies seasonally. We size the array conservatively and offer grid or generator tie-in for sites that need guaranteed uptime through extended overcast.
