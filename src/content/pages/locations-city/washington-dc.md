## Mobile surveillance in Washington, DC

The DC metro runs on federal procurement rules. NDAA Section 889 — which prohibits federal agencies and their contractors from using covered Chinese-manufactured surveillance equipment — is the floor, not the ceiling. Customers in this market want a written attestation, a SAM.gov record, and a GSA Schedule contract number before the first site walk. VDS has all three.

We've deployed across federal facility upgrades, GSA-managed construction, DC infrastructure projects (rail, bus depots), and the broader NoVA/DC/suburban-MD private construction corridor. Our Pikesville, MD headquarters is approximately 50 miles north of downtown DC — close enough that DC-metro deployments draw on the same staging capacity as Baltimore.

## Industries we protect in the DC metro

- **Federal facility construction and upgrades.** GSA-managed projects, agency campuses, secure perimeters.
- **Infrastructure construction.** WMATA stations and bus facility upgrades, DC DOT projects, utility yards.
- **Private commercial and mixed-use construction.** Tysons, Reston, Crystal City/National Landing, NoMa, Capitol Riverfront.
- **Data centers.** NoVA — the largest data center cluster in the world. Construction-phase coverage, primarily.
- **Auto and parking.** Northern Virginia dealer corridors, federal-employee long-term lots.

## Local compliance note

**NDAA Section 889.** Federal agencies and their contractors cannot use covered surveillance equipment under Section 889. VDS units are NDAA 889 compliant; we publish a written attestation and refresh it annually. Our cameras, radios, and recording hardware do not include components from the named covered manufacturers.

**GSA Schedule.** We hold an active GSA Schedule contract for surveillance services. Federal customers can procure directly off the schedule without a separate competitive process for orders under threshold.

**SAM.gov.** Registered and current.

**DC DOT permits.** Right-of-way placement in the District requires a DC DOT permit; we file on the customer's behalf. Add 5–7 business days for issuance.
