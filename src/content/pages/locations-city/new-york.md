## Mobile surveillance in New York

The New York metro is the largest construction market in the United States — over $86 billion in active project value across the five boroughs and the surrounding counties. It's also the densest enforcement and compliance environment in the country: POST Act disclosure rules, NYC DOT right-of-way permits, MTA construction protocols, and the lingering reputational baggage of every surveillance vendor that ever sold to a city agency without disclosing it.

To be direct: **VDS is private-property surveillance. We are not connected to NYPD. We do not feed any city or state agency, and we do not operate facial recognition.** That distinction matters in this market, and we put it in writing in every NYC contract.

## Industries we protect in NYC metro

- **Construction.** NYC is the highest-volume construction market we serve. Mid-rise and high-rise residential, mixed-use, hospitality, and infrastructure.
- **Port and logistics.** Port Newark/Elizabeth-adjacent operations on the Jersey side, plus Brooklyn and Bronx logistics yards.
- **Hospitality and event security.** Hotel construction, large-venue staging.
- **Retail and organized retail crime (ORC).** Flagship retail in Manhattan and high-frequency loss locations across the boroughs.
- **Auto and parking.** Bronx and Queens dealer corridors, long-term lot storage.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk), and Westchester from a staging hub in northern New Jersey. Lead time: 24–48 hours for most NYC-metro deployments. Manhattan placements with NYC DOT ROW permits add 3–5 business days for permit issuance.

We do not currently operate in Connecticut except for active-customer redeployments.

## Local compliance note

**POST Act (NYC Local Law 65 of 2020).** POST Act applies to NYPD surveillance technology, not private-property surveillance, but we structure all NYC contracts to make the line explicit: no city or state agency has standing access to VDS footage. Customers receive footage they own and control; law enforcement access requires a subpoena or warrant served on the customer.

**No facial recognition.** Our cloud VMS does not run facial recognition. We've audited our pipeline specifically against NYC's vendor-disclosure standards.
