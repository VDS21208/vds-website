## Mobile surveillance in Houston

Houston is the largest energy-industrial metro in the country and the perimeter risk profile reflects it. Permian Basin operations and the offshore back-end run out of regional yards across the metro. The Ship Channel and Port of Houston move container, bulk, and petrochemical cargo at volumes that make cargo theft a daily problem. Construction in the Energy Corridor, the Texas Medical Center expansion, and the suburban housing belt up and out of the metro is continuous.

VDS dispatches Houston-bound units on flatbeds from a Southwest staging hub. Standard lead time from contract sign to power-on is 72-96 hours. Hurricane-season planning is a standard part of every Houston deployment.

## Industries we protect in the Houston metro

- **Oil and gas.** Permian-adjacent staging yards, separator and tank-battery sites, frac sand storage, drilling and completions equipment laydown.
- **Petrochemical and refining.** Ship Channel laydown yards, turnaround staging, contractor parking inside the fenceline.
- **Port and logistics.** Port of Houston container and breakbulk terminals, cargo theft hot zones, intermodal yards.
- **Commercial construction.** Energy Corridor, Med Center expansion, Galleria mixed-use, suburban housing.
- **Utility infrastructure.** CenterPoint Energy substation perimeters, transmission staging.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Brazoria, Galveston, and Liberty counties from a Southwest staging hub via dedicated flatbed shipping. Standard lead time is 72-96 hours from quote-signed to power-on. We've also dispatched west to Austin and San Antonio on the same staging schedule.

## Local compliance note

**Hurricane redeployment.** Every Houston-metro contract includes a hurricane-redeploy clause. We pull units off-site ahead of a named-storm warning and redeploy after the all-clear with no additional mobilization fee. The customer specifies the trigger threshold in writing.

**CenterPoint Energy substations.** Utility substation work requires NERC CIP-aware access procedures. We coordinate access with the customer's CIP compliance team before mobilization.
