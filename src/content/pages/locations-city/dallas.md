## Mobile surveillance in Dallas

The Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex is the largest construction market in Texas — over $50 billion in active construction starts, driven by data center build-out in Plano and Richardson, the ongoing residential boom in the northern suburbs, downtown Dallas mixed-use, and the Fort Worth manufacturing corridor. Dallas is also the headquarters market for several major Permian Basin oil and gas operators, even though the actual oilfield assets sit 350 miles west.

VDS deploys to the DFW metro from a Texas staging hub. Lead time: 2–4 business days for most deployments.

- **Construction.** DFW is the highest-volume TX construction market. Mid-rise residential, mixed-use, industrial.

- **Logistics.** AllianceTexas, DFW Airport cargo, and the I-35 corridor.

## Local compliance note

**Texas wiretap law.** Texas is one-party consent for audio. We deploy video only by default and recommend video-only even where one-party consent is technically permissible.

**Texas DOT and city permits.** Right-of-way placement requires the relevant city's ROW permit (Dallas, Plano, Frisco, Irving, Arlington, Fort Worth, etc.) and we file on the customer's behalf. Typical issuance: 3–7 business days depending on the jurisdiction.

**Data center site security.** Several DFW-area data center operators have internal vendor security requirements that exceed standard NDAA 889 — background checks for deployment crew, badge integration requirements, and SOC integration with the operator's own NOC. VDS supports all of these on request.

**NDAA Section 889.** Increasingly a standard requirement on DFW data center contracts even where federal funds are not involved. We're compliant; written attestation available.
