## Mobile surveillance in Richmond

Richmond is the Virginia state government center and the headquarters market for Virginia state procurement. The Department of General Services (DGS), the state capitol complex, the Virginia State Police, and the Department of Corrections all sit in or near downtown Richmond. The private market mix is construction (downtown mid-rise and Scott's Addition redevelopment), the VCU and VCU Health campus, and logistics tied to the Port of Virginia in the Hampton Roads region a couple of hours southeast.

VDS deploys to the Richmond metro from our Pikesville, MD headquarters. Lead time: next business day for most deployments.

## Industries we protect in the Richmond metro

- **State government facilities.** Capitol complex perimeter, DGS-managed properties, state police facilities, DOC adjacent.
- **Construction.** Downtown high-rise, Scott's Addition, Manchester redevelopment, suburban mixed-use.
- **University and campus.** VCU and VCU Health construction-phase coverage.
- **Port of Virginia adjacent.** Inland intermodal and logistics yards in the Richmond corridor that feed the Port of Virginia at Norfolk.
- **Auto and parking.** Midlothian Turnpike and Broad Street dealer clusters.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover the City of Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Hanover, and Goochland counties from the Pikesville yard, plus extended coverage into the Tri-Cities (Petersburg, Hopewell, Colonial Heights). Lead time: 24–48 hours from contract sign to power-on for most Richmond-metro deployments. State facility deployments add lead time for credentialing and any required DGS facility review.

A Virginia state agency running a multi-building campus upgrade selected VDS after a competitive RFP. ## Local compliance note

**Virginia state procurement.** VDS is registered with eVA, the Virginia state procurement portal. We can be procured under existing statewide term contracts for surveillance services or via direct purchase under DGS-DPS thresholds. The state procurement packet includes our W-9, COI, NDAA 889 attestation, and prior performance references on state contracts.

**NDAA Section 889.** Required for Virginia state contracts that flow federal funds, and increasingly required as a baseline by DGS even where federal funds are not involved. VDS is fully compliant; written attestation available.

**Virginia wiretap law.** Virginia is one-party consent for audio. Our units capture video only by default and we recommend video-only deployments even where one-party consent is technically permissible.
