## Mobile surveillance in Miami

The Miami metro is one of the highest-growth construction markets in the country and one of the most weather-exposed. The Brickell, Wynwood, and Edgewater corridors are in the middle of a multi-year high-rise residential and mixed-use boom. PortMiami is the busiest cruise port in the world and a growing cargo gateway. Miami Beach hospitality, large-venue event security, and the South Florida auto dealer market round out the mix.

Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. Every VDS contract in Florida includes documented redeployment protocols: when does the unit get pulled, who pulls it, where does it go for storage, and how fast does it return after the storm clears. We don't leave mobile units exposed in the path of a hurricane.

## Local compliance note

**Florida wiretap law.** Florida is two-party consent for audio (Fla. Stat. § 934.03). Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.

**Miami-Dade construction permits.** Miami-Dade DERM and the City of Miami building department coordinate jurisdictionally for construction-zone placement. We don't pull building permits, but we coordinate with the GC's permit holder on placement.

**PortMiami access.** TWIC credentials and MTSA-compliant access required on the secure side. Our deployment crew is current on the credentialing.

**Florida Building Code wind load.** South Florida units are wind-rated for the conditions; we still pull units ahead of any storm tracking near landfall regardless of rating. Wind rating is not a substitute for storm protocol.
