## Mobile surveillance in Los Angeles

The LA metro is the second-largest construction market in the country and the largest port complex in the United States by container volume (Ports of Los Angeles + Long Beach combined). It's also the highest-scrutiny privacy environment on the West Coast. California's Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the more recent CPRA, plus SB 1186 on automated license plate reader (ALPR) operations, shape what a credible private-property surveillance vendor can and can't do here.

To be direct on one specific point: **VDS does not operate community ALPR networks.** We are not Flock. We do not share license plate data across a customer network. We do not feed any government agency by default. Customer footage is customer-owned and customer-controlled.

## Industries we protect in the LA metro

- **Construction.** Downtown LA, Hollywood, Century City, the Westside, and the San Gabriel Valley corridor.
- **Ports and logistics.** Ports of LA and Long Beach adjacent operations, Inland Empire warehousing.
- **Film and event production.** On-location set perimeter and event venue staging.
- **Cannabis.** Licensed dispensaries and cultivation facilities under California state license.
- **Auto dealers.** The largest dealer cluster in California — Glendale, Cerritos, Santa Monica, and the South Bay.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover Los Angeles, Orange, San Bernardino, Riverside, and Ventura counties from an LA-area staging hub. Lead time: 2–4 business days from contract sign to power-on for most LA-metro deployments. Port deployments add lead time for TWIC coordination and terminal-specific access.

## Local compliance note

**California CCPA / CPRA.** Customer footage is customer-owned. We are a service provider under CCPA terms. We do not sell or share personal information. Audit-trail logging and retention terms are explicit in every California contract.

**SB 1186 (ALPR operations).** VDS does not operate community ALPR networks. If a customer requires plate reading on their own private property for their own internal use (e.g., a dealer lot tracking its own inventory movement), that's a feature configuration on a specific deployment, not a network. We don't share plate data across customers and we don't feed government agencies by default.

**No facial recognition.** Our cloud VMS does not run facial recognition.

**California wiretap law.** California is two-party consent for audio (Cal. Penal Code § 632). Video only by default; audio disabled unless customer documents all-party consent.
