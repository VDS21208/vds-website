## Mobile surveillance in Jacksonville

Jacksonville's perimeter market sits on three pillars. The Port of Jacksonville (JAXPORT) moves vehicle, container, and bulk cargo at a scale that makes the metro a known cargo-theft target — vehicle theft from RoRo and dealer staging is a continuous issue. Naval Station Mayport and NAS Jacksonville bring federal procurement rules into nearby commercial work; even private contractors that touch a Navy contract often need NDAA Section 889 attestation from their surveillance vendor. And the metro auto-dealer cluster — Atlantic and Beach Boulevards, Roosevelt, and the Southside — is one of the densest in the Southeast.

VDS dispatches Jacksonville-bound units on flatbeds from a Southeast staging hub. Standard lead time from contract sign to power-on is 60-84 hours. Hurricane-season planning is built into every contract.

- **Auto dealers.** Atlantic Boulevard, Beach Boulevard, Roosevelt, Southside. New and used vehicle storage, long-term hold lots.

- **Parking and storage.** Long-term airport lots, vehicle-storage lots inland of the port.

## Local compliance note

**NDAA Section 889.** Contractors with sub-prime work tied to Mayport, NAS Jacksonville, or Blount Island Command need 889 attestation. VDS units are NDAA 889 compliant; we publish a written attestation and refresh it annually.

**Hurricane redeployment.** Every Jacksonville-metro contract includes a hurricane-redeploy clause. We pull units off-site ahead of a named-storm warning and redeploy after the all-clear with no additional mobilization fee.

**Florida wiretap statute (Fla. Stat. § 934.03).** Florida is a two-party-consent state for audio. Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.
