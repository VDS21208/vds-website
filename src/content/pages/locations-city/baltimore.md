## Mobile surveillance in Baltimore

Baltimore is our home market. VDS has been headquartered in Pikesville since 2017, and our staging yard is 15 minutes from the Inner Harbor, 20 minutes from the Port of Baltimore, and 25 minutes from BWI. We've run more deployments in the Baltimore metro than in any other city — and most of our same-day deployment capacity in the country routes through this hub.

The Baltimore market mix is industrial-heavy: Port of Baltimore container and roll-on/roll-off terminals, BWI cargo and construction, Inner Harbor mixed-use development, Johns Hopkins medical and East Baltimore campus expansion, and the Tradepoint Atlantic redevelopment at Sparrows Point. That's before counting the auto dealer corridor on Pulaski Highway and the parking-lot density of downtown.

## Local compliance note

Baltimore City has no specific ordinance restricting private mobile surveillance on private property, but several construction zones along the Inner Harbor and around Johns Hopkins are inside Baltimore City Right-of-Way permit jurisdiction. If a unit sits on a sidewalk or shoulder, we pull the ROW permit and post signage per Baltimore DOT rules.

Maryland's wiretap statute (Md. Cts. & Jud. Proc. § 10-402) is two-party-consent for audio. Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.

The Port of Baltimore requires TWIC-credentialed escorts and MTSA-compliant access on the secure side of terminal fencing. We've run jobs at Seagirt and Dundalk Marine Terminal and our deployment crew is current on the credentialing.
