## Mobile surveillance in Philadelphia

Philadelphia is a 2-hour drive from the VDS Pikesville headquarters. Next-business-day deployment is standard across the metro, and we keep staged units rotating between Philly and Baltimore depending on demand. The Philly market is a balanced mix: heavy construction in Center City and the Navy Yard, port logistics on the Delaware River, refinery and chemical operations along the I-95 industrial corridor, and four major university campuses (Penn, Drexel, Temple, plus Villanova in the suburbs) that drive both campus security and student-housing construction.

## Industries we protect in the Philadelphia metro

- **Construction.** Center City high-rise, Navy Yard redevelopment, North Broad and University City mid-rise.
- **Port and industrial.** Port of Philadelphia container and break-bulk terminals, PhilaPort facilities, refinery perimeter.
- **University and campus.** Penn, Drexel, Temple — construction-phase coverage and event security.
- **Auto and parking.** Roosevelt Boulevard corridor, suburban dealer clusters.
- **Municipal and transit.** SEPTA construction, PennDOT yards, Philadelphia DOT facilities.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover Philadelphia County, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, and Montgomery counties on the PA side, plus Camden, Gloucester, and Burlington counties in South Jersey. Lead time: next business day from contract sign to power-on for most Philly-metro deployments. Center City placements with PPA or DOT permits add 3–5 business days.

## Local compliance note

**PA COSTARS.** VDS is eligible under the Pennsylvania COSTARS cooperative purchasing program. Municipal, school district, and county customers in PA can procure VDS services through the COSTARS contract without a separate competitive bid for orders within program thresholds.

**Philadelphia surveillance disclosure.** Philadelphia City Council has been actively considering surveillance disclosure ordinances similar to NYC's POST Act. Today there is no enacted private-property restriction, but we structure Philly municipal-adjacent contracts with the same private-property-only framing we use in NYC. Audit-trail and footage-ownership terms are explicit in every contract.

**Pennsylvania wiretap law.** PA is two-party consent for audio (18 Pa.C.S. § 5703–5704). Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.

**Philadelphia Parking Authority.** Some Center City deployments interact with PPA-managed curbside zones. We coordinate with the PPA on placement and confirm any required permits before deployment.
