## Local compliance note

**NDAA Section 889.** Federal-adjacent semiconductor work — including TSMC supply chain build-out tied to CHIPS Act funding — increasingly requires 889 attestation from on-site vendors. VDS units are NDAA 889 compliant; we publish a written attestation and refresh it annually.

**Arizona wiretap statute (A.R.S. § 13-3005).** Arizona is a one-party-consent state for audio, but our units capture video only by default unless the customer has a documented business reason and signage.

**Copper theft enforcement.** Arizona DPS and Phoenix PD coordinate on construction-zone copper-theft cases. Our SOC escalation pattern — verified perimeter event, video evidence package, license plate capture — aligns with what their investigators ask for.
