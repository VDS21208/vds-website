## Mobile surveillance in Chicago

Chicago is the largest inland rail hub in the United States — every Class I freight railroad meets here — and one of the highest-volume retail and ORC enforcement markets in the country. The Mag Mile retail corridor, the CTA and Metra transit construction programs, the data center build-out tied to CME Group and the financial market infrastructure, and the broader Chicagoland industrial and logistics market drive most of our deployment volume here.

Chicagoland is also the most aggressive biometric privacy environment in the US. The Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) has produced more private-litigation risk for surveillance vendors than any other state statute. To be direct: **VDS does not collect, store, or process biometric information. We do not run facial recognition. We do not run iris, voice, gait, or any other biometric matching.** This is a configuration default, not a feature we toggle off on request.

## Industries we protect in the Chicago metro

- **Construction.** Downtown high-rise, West Loop, Fulton Market, suburban mixed-use.
- **Transit and infrastructure.** CTA station and yard construction, Metra facility upgrades, IDOT projects.
- **Logistics and rail.** Inland intermodal yards, distribution-center perimeter, last-mile facilities.
- **Retail and ORC.** Mag Mile, downtown flagship retail, and flash-mob-targeted locations.
- **Data centers.** Northern Chicagoland data center construction corridor.

## Coverage and lead time

We cover Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake (IL), McHenry, and Will counties from a Chicagoland staging hub. Lead time: 2–4 business days from contract sign to power-on for most Chicago-metro deployments. Mag Mile and downtown placements with Chicago DOT ROW permits add 5–7 business days for permit issuance.

## Local compliance note

**Illinois BIPA.** No biometric data is collected, stored, or processed in any VDS deployment. No facial recognition, no iris, no voice print, no gait analysis. This is a stack-level configuration default — the capability is not installed.

**Chicago surveillance disclosure.** Chicago has no enacted POST Act equivalent today, but we structure private-property contracts with the same private-property-only framing we use in NYC. Customer-owned footage, audit-trail logging, and explicit law-enforcement-access-by-subpoena terms in every contract.

**Illinois wiretap law.** Illinois is two-party consent for audio (720 ILCS 5/14-2). Video only by default; audio disabled unless customer documents all-party consent.

**Chicago DOT permits.** Right-of-way placement in the City of Chicago requires a CDOT permit. We file on the customer's behalf. Typical issuance: 5–7 business days.

**Rail and intermodal access.** Class I railroad intermodal yard access requires railroad-specific credentialing. Our crew is current on the major operators serving the Chicagoland hub.
