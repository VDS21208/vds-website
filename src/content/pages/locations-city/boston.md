Boston is a three-market metro stacked on top of itself: institutional construction (Harvard, MIT, BU, Northeastern, Tufts, BC), high-rise commercial in the Seaport and Back Bay, and the Kendall Square biotech build-out that has put more cranes over Cambridge than at any time in the city's history. Add the Allston Yards megaproject and the slow grind of MBTA infrastructure work, and the result is a metro where every general contractor has a perimeter problem and most of them are still buying it from a guard-only vendor.

VDS deploys to Boston from our Pikesville, MD headquarters. Drive time is approximately 8 hours; we ship most Boston-bound units on a flatbed dispatched the day the contract is signed. Standard deployment window is 24-72 hours from contract sign to power-on.

## Local compliance note

**Massachusetts wiretap statute (M.G.L. c. 272 § 99).** Massachusetts is a two-party-consent state for audio recording. Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.

**Massachusetts Open Meeting Law and public records.** Any state or municipal contract may bring the deployment into the orbit of M.G.L. c. 30A (OML) and c. 66 (public records). We structure public-sector contracts so footage retention, access requests, and chain-of-custody align with the customer's records-retention schedule.
