## Mobile surveillance in Las Vegas

Las Vegas runs on three perimeter-relevant cycles. Construction has been continuous on the Strip and downtown for the better part of a decade — new arenas, expanded casino footprints, mixed-use towers off the Strip, and the suburban housing pipeline through Henderson and the northwest. The events calendar is functionally year-round: trade shows at the LVCC and Mandalay Bay Convention Center, concerts at the major venues, and the Formula 1 buildout cycle each fall. And the parking and garage footprint — both on-property and the casino-employee and trade-show overflow lots — is a continuous theft and trespass target.

**One important boundary up front.** VDS is not gaming-property-licensed. Gaming-floor and interior-casino surveillance is regulated by the Nevada Gaming Control Board and falls outside our scope. We cover construction, exterior perimeter, parking, and event-staging — the spaces around the gaming property, not the gaming surveillance itself.

VDS dispatches Vegas-bound units on flatbeds from a Southwest staging hub. Standard lead time from contract sign to power-on is 72-96 hours.

## Local compliance note

**Gaming jurisdiction boundary.** Interior gaming-property surveillance is regulated by the Nevada Gaming Control Board and requires gaming licensing we do not hold. VDS covers construction, exterior, parking, and event-staging only. We document the scope boundary explicitly in every Las Vegas contract.

**Nevada wiretap statute (NRS 200.620).** Nevada is a two-party-consent state for audio. Our units capture video only by default; audio is disabled unless the customer has documented all-party consent.

**Event and trade-show staging.** Convention authority and venue-operator security plans typically require coordination with on-site security before perimeter deployment. We integrate with the customer's event security lead during mobilization and align SOC escalation paths to their dispatch protocol.

**Heat and dust.** Panel mounting and seals are engineered for desert-summer wind and dust loads. We swap intake filters on a tightened schedule through the hot months.
