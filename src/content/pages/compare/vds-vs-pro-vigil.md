## Why this comparison matters

Pro-Vigil is one of the older names in managed remote video monitoring. They've built a real business on the "virtual guard" model — live operators watching customer-owned (or third-party-installed) cameras and intervening when something happens. They publish a 97% deterrence claim and a 24-second response time.

The structural challenge with the Pro-Vigil model is that the platform underneath the monitoring is not their own. Cameras come from various installers. Network connectivity depends on the site. Repair and uptime depend on whoever installed the gear. When a camera goes down, the customer is in the middle of a vendor triangle.

VDS owns the full stack — the trailer, the cameras inside it, the LTE radio, the VMS, the analytics, and the SOC operators. When something breaks, there's one number to call and one team to fix it. This page lays out where Pro-Vigil's model wins, where the integrated approach wins, and what real customers report in public reviews.

## Where Pro-Vigil wins

**Long-established virtual guard operation.** Pro-Vigil has been running live remote monitoring for two decades. The operational playbook for talk-downs and dispatch is mature.

**Works on existing camera estates.** If you've already paid for fixed cameras and just need a monitoring layer on top, Pro-Vigil's service-on-other-people's-hardware model fits. We can do that too via ONVIF ingest, but Pro-Vigil's commercial motion is built around it.

**Brand familiarity in commercial real estate.** Property managers know the name. If your procurement office wants a name they recognize for the monitoring line item, Pro-Vigil clears that bar.

## Where VDS wins

**One throat to choke when something breaks.** Public BBB complaints flag repair delays on Pro-Vigil deployments — including a documented case of a non-working camera taking over a week to repair because the installer and Pro-Vigil were each waiting on the other. With VDS, there is no installer middleman. Our truck, our trailer, our techs, our 48-hour onsite SLA.

**Hardware refreshed inside the rental term.** Glassdoor reviews from Pro-Vigil employees flag outdated technology in the field. With a service-layer model, you're stuck with whatever hardware the installer put in two or five years ago. VDS rotates hardware on a documented refresh cycle at no incremental cost.

**Documented escalation pipeline.** Pro-Vigil publishes a 24-second response time. We don't dispute it — but we want to be precise about what's being measured. Our published metric is <60 seconds from analytic trigger to operator action with live LE dispatch initiated. Our 97% number is false-alarm reduction (the percentage of analytic alerts our operators suppress before they ever reach the customer), not deterrence — a measurable operational metric, not a vendor-friendly outcome claim.

**SOC operator stability.** We're a Maryland operation with documented SOC operator tenure. Pro-Vigil's Glassdoor reviews indicate high turnover, which matters because consistent operators learn your specific sites, your access protocols, and your local PD relationships.

**Mobile and outdoor coverage.** Pro-Vigil doesn't sell solar-autonomous trailers. If your site has no power or moves quarterly, that's a non-starter for the Pro-Vigil model.

## Cost and TCO

Pro-Vigil monthly monitoring runs roughly $300-$800 per site for service only — but that excludes camera hardware, installation, network, and ongoing repair, which the customer pays separately. Real all-in cost lands $1,200-$2,500/mo depending on installer markup.

VDS Sky Guard at $2,200/mo includes the trailer, the cameras, the LTE, the VMS, the SOC, the audio talk-down, the LE dispatch workflow, the audit log, and the hardware refresh. No installer line item, no repair surprises.

## Switching from Pro-Vigil

1. **End-of-month inventory.** Identify which sites are under Pro-Vigil monitoring and which third-party installer owns the camera hardware.
2. **Replacement or ingest decision.** Where the cameras are old or limited, we deploy a VDS unit. Where the cameras are good, our VMS ingests them via ONVIF and the SOC takes over monitoring.
3. **Single MSA cutover.** One Master Service Agreement with VDS replaces the Pro-Vigil monitoring contract and the installer's maintenance contract.
4. **Quarterly review.** Documented operator notes, incident summaries, hardware health.
