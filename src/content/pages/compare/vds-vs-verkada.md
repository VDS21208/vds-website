## Why this comparison matters

Verkada is the most polished enterprise cloud video product in the market. The mobile app is genuinely excellent. The unified access-plus-video story closes deals against Lenel and Genetec every week. We are not going to pretend otherwise.

We are also going to point out that Verkada was breached in March 2021 (150,000+ cameras, intruder accessed live feeds at psychiatric hospitals, women's health clinics, Tesla factories, and jails), paid $2.95M to the FTC in August 2024 (the largest CAN-SPAM penalty ever assessed), and was cited by the FTC for posting fake employee-written reviews. Those facts are public and they matter when you're picking the company that holds your video.

This page covers where Verkada genuinely wins, where VDS wins, and the architectural differences that matter for risk-sensitive buyers.

## Where Verkada wins

**Best UX in the category, period.** The mobile app, the web console, the floor-plan view, the search experience — all genuinely well-designed. If your security director's primary daily tool is the mobile app on her phone, Verkada is hard to beat on user experience.

**Unified access and video.** Verkada Access (door controllers, badge readers) talks to Verkada Video in one console. If you're standing up a new corporate HQ and want one vendor for both, that's a real advantage and we don't match it natively.

**Indoor product line breadth.** Domes, fisheyes, multi-sensors, mini-domes, license-plate cameras, environmental sensors, alarm panels — Verkada has more SKUs than we do. For an indoor enterprise rollout, that catalog matters.

## Where VDS wins

**Camera-agnostic VMS.** Our platform accepts any ONVIF-compliant camera. Axis, Hanwha, Bosch, Avigilon, your existing fleet — we ingest the stream and run our analytics on top. Verkada's platform only runs Verkada cameras. If you already own $400K of cameras, that matters.

**Outdoor and mobile.** Verkada has no solar-autonomous outdoor trailer. We do — Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, and Boundary Guard Pro, all assembled in Pikesville MD. If your security perimeter includes a construction site, a parking field, a yard, a remote substation, or a job site without power, we cover it and Verkada doesn't.

**24/7 in-house SOC.** Verkada is a software-plus-camera company. Monitoring is on you or your guard provider. VDS includes a Pikesville, Maryland-staffed SOC with <60-second escalation, 97% false-alarm reduction, audio talk-down on every trailer, and LE dispatch with live video pushed to the responding officer.

**Pricing transparency.** Verkada's cloud licenses run $199-$1,799 per camera per year, tiered opaquely. Customers regularly report sticker shock at renewal. VDS rolls everything — hardware, VMS, storage, SOC — into one monthly per unit. No per-camera license escalator at renewal.

**No breach or FTC settlement on the record.** The 2021 Verkada breach (Bloomberg, March 2021) exposed live feeds from psychiatric hospitals, women's health clinics, Tesla factories, and jails. The 2024 FTC settlement was $2.95M — the largest CAN-SPAM penalty in FTC history — and the case file specifically cited Verkada for posting fake reviews written by employees. We have zero comparable incidents on file.

- **Verkada:** ~$15,000 hardware avg + ~$799/camera/yr license × 24 × 3 = $57,528 license + $15,000 = **$72,528** (hardware-low estimate; real-world deployments often run higher with multi-tier licensing).
- **VDS equivalent outdoor coverage:** 2 Sky Guard MSU at $2,200/mo × 36 = **$158,400** if you actually need mobile/outdoor — but if it's purely indoor enterprise, Verkada is the right tool. This is not always our deal.

We will tell you when Verkada is the right answer. For pure indoor corporate, it often is. For anything with an outdoor or mobile component, VDS wins on TCO and operational fit.
