## Where Deploy Surveillance wins

**Simplicity for smaller deals.** If you need one or two trailers on a single site, do not need a platform to manage 30 cameras across 15 sites, and want a straightforward rental contract, a smaller vendor like Deploy Surveillance can be the right fit. Less surface area means less software to learn.

**Lower coordination overhead.** Smaller operators sometimes move faster on a one-off custom request because there is less internal process.

**Direct phone line to the owner.** With smaller vendors you often talk to the principal directly. That has value for some buyers.

## Where VDS wins

**Scale signal.** 500+ deployments across 30+ states is a different field-data set than a smaller operator can offer. When something unusual happens at 2:47 AM at a remote site, the SOC has likely seen the pattern before.

**Real AI, not a marketing label.** AI VisionStream is camera-agnostic via ONVIF/RTSP, hits 95%+ LPR accuracy in under 3 seconds, and feeds the SOC the filtered signal that powers the 97% false-alarm reduction number. This is 7 years of model and ops iteration.

**24/7 in-house US SOC.** Not a third-party referral. VDS operators dispatch local law enforcement, run audio talk-down through the unit speaker, and escalate to a named site contact in under 60 seconds.

**NDAA Section 889 in writing.** Required for federal contracts, federally-funded projects, and most large GC master agreements. We hand you the bill of materials.

**Published pricing.** Sky Guard MSU from $2,200/mo. Swift Deploy from $1,500/mo. Boundary Guard Pro from $1,800/mo. Sky Guard purchase from $38,000. No quote-walls.

**Hero outcome data.** $28M Mid-Atlantic GC — 73% theft reduction, $0 insurance claims, $340,800 saved in 12 months. Numbers are the comparison.

We have ported customers off other mobile surveillance vendors before. The migration usually looks like:

1. **Week 1** — VDS engineer reviews your site map, current footage retention, and existing camera assets. If you have ONVIF/RTSP cameras already, AI VisionStream can ingest them.

3. **Week 3** — Live. Old vendor demobilized. Historical footage exported to your storage if required.

No early-termination penalty applies on the VDS side; check your existing contract for the other side.
