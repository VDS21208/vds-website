## Where GoView wins

**Lowest-end pricing if you have a narrow scope.** If your budget is tight and you need a basic camera trailer for a short window with minimal software requirements, a smaller rent vendor like GoView may come in below VDS Swift Deploy on price alone.

**Short-cycle rentals.** Smaller vendors sometimes accept very short rental terms that larger operators do not optimize for.

**Direct owner conversation.** With smaller operators you talk to the principal. Some buyers prefer that for a one-off deployment.

## Where VDS wins

**Real AI platform.** AI VisionStream is a 7-year-mature platform that does the work — camera-agnostic ingest, 95%+ LPR at sub-3-second match, person/vehicle/intrusion detection, and the inference layer that feeds the SOC. Smaller vendors often advertise AI without the model ops behind it.

**24/7 in-house US SOC.** VDS operators sit in a US facility, dispatch local law enforcement, run audio talk-down, and escalate to your named site contact in under 60 seconds. 97% false-alarm reduction is the operational number.

**NDAA Section 889 compliance.** Federal-eligible. Required by most large GC master agreements. Provided in writing.

**US-only AWS data residency.** All footage in US AWS regions only. TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest. RBAC, MFA, 12-month immutable audit log. If your security committee asks where the data lives, the answer is documented.

**Scale and references.** 500+ deployments across 30+ states. Hero case study with measurable dollar outcomes.

**Published pricing.** Swift Deploy from $1,500/mo. Boundary Guard Pro from $1,800/mo. Sky Guard from $2,200/mo. Sky Guard purchase from $38,000.
