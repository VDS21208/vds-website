## Why this comparison matters

The .ai TLD is a marketing signal. It is not a product signal. AI in mobile surveillance is judged on three things: how accurate the inference is in the field, how the SOC uses that inference to reduce false alarms, and how the data is governed. This page compares VDS and BIGView on those operational dimensions, not on web design.

## Where BIGView wins

**Startup product velocity.** Newer entrants can ship interface changes and new AI features faster than 7-year-old platforms. If your buying preference is product velocity over field-tested operations, that has weight.

**Modern visual branding.** The .ai domain and modern web design appeal to buyers who index on look-and-feel during the first call.

**Fresh model architectures.** Newer entrants are sometimes built on more recent open-source models. Whether that matters in production depends on training data and ops discipline more than on architecture.

## Where VDS wins

**7 years of field data.** AI VisionStream has been running in production across 500+ deployments in 30+ states since 2017. The models have seen weather, edge lighting, false-positive patterns, and adversarial scenes that newer entrants will not see for years. Field data is the moat.

**SOC integration is where AI actually matters.** Raw inference is cheap. What matters is whether a 24/7 operator can act on it in under 60 seconds, filter 97% of false positives before they reach your phone, run audio talk-down, and dispatch local law enforcement with the right ticket detail. That integration takes years.

**Documented data governance.** US AWS regions only. TLS 1.3. AES-256. RBAC. MFA. 12-month immutable audit log. NDAA Section 889 in writing. These are the things insurance underwriters and federal buyers ask for. We answer them in writing on day one.

**Published pricing.** Swift Deploy from $1,500/mo. Boundary Guard Pro from $1,800/mo. Sky Guard from $2,200/mo. Sky Guard purchase from $38,000. No quote-wall on the platform.

**Customer outcome data.** $28M Mid-Atlantic GC project — 73% theft reduction, $0 insurance claims, $340,800 saved in 12 months. That is the conversation we want the comparison to land on.
