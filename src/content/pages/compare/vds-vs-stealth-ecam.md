## Why this comparison matters

In 2024-2025, Stealth Monitoring, ECAMSECURE, and GardaWorld merged into what is now ECAM. The combined entity monitors 140,000+ cameras daily and is the largest live remote monitoring service in North America. By any volume measure, ECAM is the biggest player in remote video monitoring.

The strategic question for buyers is whether scale equals fit. ECAM is a service company without first-party platform IP — they monitor cameras others install on platforms others built. The GardaWorld parentage adds a guard-services upsell motion that some customers want and others actively want to avoid. And the brand churn (Stealth → ECAMSECURE → ECAM in 18 months) creates real operational friction for procurement, signage, and customer-facing communications.

VDS is a smaller, more focused operation. We own the hardware, the VMS, the analytics, and the SOC under one brand we've operated since 2017. This page is for buyers deciding between scale and integration.

## Where Stealth / ECAM wins

**Scale of monitoring volume.** 140,000+ cameras monitored daily means deep operational pattern recognition. If you want a vendor that's seen every type of perimeter event a thousand times before, ECAM has the volume.

**Case study library across the merged entities.** Stealth, ECAMSECURE, and GardaWorld each brought multi-vertical references. The combined library is genuinely broad — retail, multi-family, construction, education, healthcare.

**Bundled guard services if you want them.** GardaWorld is one of the largest physical guard companies in the world. If you want a single vendor for both remote monitoring and physical patrols, the bundled offering is real.

## Where VDS wins

**Operational stability since 2017.** VDS has been one company under one brand since 2017. ECAM has had three brand identities in 18 months. That matters for site signage (which has to be updated each time), incident reports, customer-facing communications, and procurement file maintenance. Our customers don't have to update anything when their security vendor renames itself.

**First-party platform IP.** ECAM is a service layered on third-party cameras and third-party platforms. When a platform vendor changes pricing or sunsets a feature, ECAM and the customer are along for the ride. We own our platform end-to-end. Pricing and feature roadmap are decisions we make for our customers, not decisions made for us.

**No guard-services upsell pressure.** GardaWorld's commercial model points naturally toward "let's add physical guards." That's a legitimate service line, but customers regularly tell us they want the technology layer specifically because they're trying to reduce guard spend. With VDS, the conversation about guards never happens because we don't sell them.

**Mobile and solar hardware.** ECAM monitors fixed installations. No solar-autonomous trailers. If your need is mobile or off-grid, the entire ECAM proposition doesn't fit.

**Documented false-alarm metric.** We publish 97% false-alarm reduction as an auditable operational number — the percentage of analytic triggers our operators filter before they reach the customer. ECAM publishes response times but not false-alarm-suppression rates, which is the more operationally meaningful metric for security directors evaluating alert fatigue.

## Cost and TCO

ECAM monitoring runs $400-$1,200 per site per month for service only. Hardware and installation are separate, typically through an authorized integrator network with their own markups. All-in monthlies often land $1,400-$2,800/mo per site for fixed coverage, plus capex on the camera installation.

VDS Sky Guard at $2,200/mo includes the trailer, every camera on it, the LTE, the VMS, the SOC, the audio talk-down, and the LE dispatch workflow. No installer line item.

## Switching from Stealth / ECAM

1. **Identify the multi-vendor stack.** ECAM monitoring contract, separate installer maintenance contract, separate platform license. Map all three.
2. **Replace the stack with one VDS contract.** Sky Guard for mobile/perimeter coverage, ONVIF ingest where you want to keep existing fixed cameras.
3. **Brand-update the signage.** If your site signage references Stealth or ECAMSECURE, update to VDS — and don't expect to do it again soon, because we've kept one brand since 2017.
4. **Single quarterly review.** One contact, one report, one number to call.
