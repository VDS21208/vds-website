## Where Deep Sentinel wins

**Homes.** A single-family home does not need a solar-autonomous trailer. Deep Sentinel's fixed AI cameras with live agent monitoring are well-priced and well-designed for that buyer.

**Single storefronts and small businesses.** A coffee shop, a small office, a single-bay auto shop. The Deep Sentinel hub plus 2-4 cameras and a monthly plan is the right shape and the right budget.

**DIY install and short install timelines.** Deep Sentinel ships, you mount cameras, you go live in hours. No site survey, no trailer transport.

**Sub-$500/mo budget.** If your monthly security spend is under $500 across the whole site, Deep Sentinel is a real fit. VDS is not optimized for that band.

**Interactive AI talk-down at the consumer tier.** Deep Sentinel's interactive agents are a thoughtful product for residential use.

## Where VDS wins

**Sites without power.** Solar-autonomous units mean construction sites, undeveloped land, utility substations, and remote yards get full coverage without trenching or a generator.

**Sites that move.** Construction projects relocate the active work zone every few weeks. Lay-down yards shift. Mobile units redeploy in hours. Fixed cameras do not.

**Multi-acre and perimeter coverage.** A 20-acre lay-down yard, a 40-acre solar farm, or a quarter-mile substation perimeter is not a fixed-camera job. Mobile units with elevated masts, pan-tilt-zoom cameras, and AI-driven detection cover that footprint with fewer assets.

**Commercial governance.** NDAA Section 889 in writing. US-only AWS data residency. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. 12-month immutable audit log. RBAC and MFA. These are the answers a commercial security committee, an insurance underwriter, or a federal-flow-down GC needs.

**Operator-driven SOC playbooks.** VDS' in-house US SOC builds a per-site escalation tree, named contacts, dispatch boundaries, and audio talk-down scripts. The 97% false-alarm reduction comes from that operator discipline, not just from the AI.

**Customer outcomes at commercial scale.** Hero case: $28M Mid-Atlantic GC. 73% theft reduction. $0 insurance claims. $340,800 saved in 12 months. That is a different math problem from a residential security plan.
