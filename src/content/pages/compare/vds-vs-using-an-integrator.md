## Why this comparison matters

Security 101 is the most-recognized national US integrator. They resell Verkada, Avigilon, Genetec, and others, and assemble those products into a system for the buyer. VDS is a manufacturer — we design and build mobile surveillance hardware (Sky Guard, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro), run the AI VisionStream platform, and operate a 24/7 US SOC under one roof.

Neither model is automatically better. The right model depends on what you are buying.

|---|---|---|

## Where an integrator like Security 101 wins

**Heterogeneous environments.** A corporate campus with badge access control, intercoms, fixed surveillance from two brands, intrusion panels, and a parking gate system needs an integrator. Stitching those products together is the integrator's job and they are good at it.

**One-throat-to-choke across 10+ vendors.** If your security director wants one PO, one trouble ticket address, and one annual review covering everything, the integrator is the model.

**Existing relationship.** If Security 101 already installed your access control and your fixed surveillance, adding the mobile surveillance trailer through them is sometimes simpler than starting a parallel relationship.

**Geographic install density.** National integrators have install techs in markets where a single manufacturer may not.

## Where buying direct from VDS wins

**Mobile surveillance is a dedicated program.** If you are spending $50,000+ per year on mobile surveillance trailers across multiple sites, you are running a dedicated program. Buying it through an integrator adds margin without adding value.

**Manufacturer SOC and warranty.** When a unit fails at 3 AM, you want the manufacturer's SOC and the manufacturer's field service. Going through an integrator means the integrator opens a ticket with VDS — extra step, no benefit.

**Transparent pricing.** Published rates: Swift Deploy from $1,500/mo, Boundary Guard Pro from $1,800/mo, Sky Guard from $2,200/mo, Sky Guard purchase from $38,000. An integrator quote layers margin on top of MSRP. You are paying for the relationship.

**NDAA Section 889 traceability.** When the GC's federal flow-down audit lands on your desk, you need a component bill of materials. Direct from manufacturer is one document. Through an integrator is two paper trails and a phone call.

**Direct engineering line.** Site-specific deployment questions get answered by the engineer who designed the unit, not by a sales rep who resells the unit.

## A note on integrator partners

VDS works with security integrators through our /partners program. If you are an integrator reading this — we are not trying to cut you out. We are trying to be honest with end-buyers about when a manufacturer-direct purchase is the better answer. Where the integrator relationship adds real value (heterogeneous environments, multi-vendor stitching, geographic install reach), we partner. Where it adds only margin, we encourage direct.

## Decision framework

**Buy through an integrator if:**
- You are buying mobile surveillance as one piece of a broader multi-vendor security system.
- You have an existing integrator relationship and the marginal install volume is small.
- You want one PO covering access control, fixed cameras, intrusion, and mobile.

- Mobile surveillance is a dedicated program with its own budget line.
