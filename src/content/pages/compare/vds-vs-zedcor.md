## Why this comparison matters

Zedcor and VDS are often shortlisted together by North American buyers — energy operators, national GCs, and retail chains with footprints on both sides of the border. The two companies serve different geographies and operate under different regulatory regimes. The right answer is rarely "pick one" — it is usually "pick the right one for each country, or run them together."

## Where Zedcor wins

**Canadian regulatory fit.** A US vendor cannot offer Canadian data residency or PIPEDA-aligned audit posture out of the box. Zedcor was built for it.

**Oil and gas references.** Decade-plus of Alberta and Saskatchewan upstream and midstream deployments. If your project is a wellpad, lease site, or pipeline yard in western Canada, Zedcor has more references in the file than VDS does.

**Public-company governance.** TSX-V listing means audited financials, public quarterly disclosure, and a board structure that some procurement teams require for vendor approval.

**Canadian SOC operator nuance.** Local language coverage, Canadian law enforcement dispatch relationships, and provincial reporting familiarity.

## Where VDS wins

**NDAA Section 889 compliance.** Required for any US federal contract, federally-funded project, or large GC jobsite where the GC's master agreement flows down 889. Canadian operators are not built around this requirement.

**US AWS-only data residency.** All AI VisionStream footage is encrypted at rest with AES-256 in US AWS regions only. TLS 1.3 in transit. Immutable 12-month audit log. RBAC and MFA enforced.

**Published pricing.** Sky Guard MSU from $2,200/mo, Swift Deploy from $1,500/mo, Boundary Guard Pro from $1,800/mo. Sky Guard purchase from $38,000. Zedcor pricing is by quote.

**7-year AI platform maturity.** AI VisionStream is camera-agnostic (ONVIF/RTSP), 95%+ LPR accuracy in under 3 seconds, 30/60/90-day cloud retention options, and feeds the SOC's 97% false-alarm reduction.

**Hero case study with US numbers.** $28M Mid-Atlantic GC — 73% theft reduction, $0 insurance claims, $340,800 saved in 12 months.
