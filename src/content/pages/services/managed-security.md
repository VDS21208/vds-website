Capex is dead weight on a project budget. Multi-vendor opex is a finger-pointing exercise. Managed services from a vertically integrated provider is the only model that makes the math and the accountability work.

## What's included in the monthly fee

- Hardware (trailers, fixed cameras, mast units) — owned by VDS, deployed at your site
- 24/7 US SOC monitoring with full event response and LE dispatch
- Quarterly preventive maintenance on every camera
- Emergency field service inside SLA
- Redeployment as your site changes
- Monthly reporting: incident logs, dispatch records, false-alarm rates, camera uptime, recommendations
- Customer portal access for live video, recorded video, incident history, and event export
- VMS license and cloud storage at your contracted retention
- Cellular data and connectivity
- All firmware and software updates

One line item on the PO. One invoice per month. One escalation path.

## What's not included

- Capex on hardware (intentionally — you don't own it)
- Police response costs (handled by your jurisdiction)
- Insurance premiums (though our case study shows them dropping)
- Damage from intentional vandalism by authorized personnel (covered for unauthorized)

## The case study, in plain numbers

A $28M Mid-Atlantic mixed-use construction project, 14 months in scope.

Before VDS (guard service):
- $32,400/month for two 24/7 armed guards
- 14 reported theft incidents in the prior 12-month equivalent
- $186,000 in filed insurance claims for theft and vandalism
- Two guard-related incident reports requiring legal review

After 12 months on VDS Managed:
- $4,000/month
- 4 reported theft incidents (73% reduction)
- $0 insurance claims filed
- Zero legal-review incident reports

Net annual savings: $340,800. Theft losses prevented: estimated $140,000 (insurer's own actuarial model). Total economic benefit: ~$480,000 in year one.

## How the pricing works

Three components, blended into one monthly number:

1. **Hardware unit fee** — per trailer or per fixed-camera channel, per month. Includes the unit, install/redeploy, and PM.
2. **Monitoring fee** — per camera channel, per month. Covers SOC, incident reports, and LE coordination.
3. **Platform fee** — flat monthly. Covers VMS, cloud storage, customer portal, integrations.

Typical small site (1 trailer, 6 cameras): $2,800–$4,500/month all-in.
Typical mid-size construction (3 trailers, 18 cameras): $7,500–$11,000/month.
Typical enterprise multi-site (50+ cameras across locations): negotiated, volume tiers below standard pricing.

Term is month-to-month with a 90-day cancellation notice. Annual contracts get a 10% discount.

## Reporting that goes to your CFO, not just your security manager

Monthly report includes:
- Incident summary with severity classification
- Dispatch records and LE response codes
- False-alarm rate (with quarter-over-quarter trend)
- Camera uptime and PM completion
- Cost-avoidance estimate against prior security model
- Insurance posture notes
- Recommendations for next quarter

The cost-avoidance line is the one your CFO cares about. We publish it because we have the numbers to back it.
