A trailer doesn't need a permit, a transformer, or a network drop. That's why we built one.

## What "72 hours" actually means

The clock starts at contract signature. Inside that window: deployment engineer reviews the site survey, the trailer is staged from one of our regional depots, freight is dispatched, our field tech meets the trailer at the gate, and the unit is online and talking to the SOC before the third sunset. We've hit this window on 96% of deployments since 2022. The misses were customer access issues — locked gates, missing site contacts.

## What's on the trailer

- **400W–800W solar array** sized for the latitude and tree canopy. Battery bank carries 5–7 days of overcast operation.
- **4–8 cameras** depending on perimeter geometry. PTZ + fixed combinations. 4K resolution. Thermal optional.
- **AI analytics on-board** for person/vehicle/loitering detection. Reduces uplink bandwidth and SOC false-alarm load.
- **Dual-carrier cellular** with automatic failover. Optional Starlink for remote sites.
- **Two-way audio** for SOC talk-down. 100dB+ SPL — audible across a 500-ft yard.
- **Strobe and floodlight** triggered manually by SOC or automatically by analytics.
- **Tamper sensors** on every access point. GPS lock. Theft recovery built in.

Assembled in Pikesville, MD. NDAA Section 889 compliant. Documentation ships with the unit.

## Setup in under 20 minutes

The trailer arrives on a standard goose-neck. Drop it, level it, raise the mast (electric), point the solar array south. The unit boots, registers with our cloud, and the SOC sees the feed inside ten minutes of mast-up. No ladders. No specialty tools. One person.

We've trained customer site supers to stage their own units in 18 minutes. Most clients let our field tech handle the first deployment and then self-service from there.

## How it pays for itself

A two-guard 24/7 contract runs $28,000–$35,000/month in most metros. Two VDS trailers with SOC monitoring runs $3,500–$5,500/month. The Mid-Atlantic case study cut a $32,400 monthly bill to $4,000 and reduced theft 73% in the first year. The hardware pays itself back inside the first quarter on most projects.
