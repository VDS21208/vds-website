A trailer that sits in a yard is not security. Getting steel onto the dirt — on the right pad, at the right elevation, with the right view angles, levered up, leveled, and connected — is half the job. VDS runs deployment logistics as a single managed workflow from quote to operational.

## Phase 1: Site survey

Every deployment opens with a survey. Three formats depending on the engagement:

- **Remote desk survey** — satellite imagery, customer-provided site photos, address geocoding for cellular signal estimate. Same day, no charge. Sufficient for ~60% of deployments.
- **Drive-by survey** — regional tech visits the site for 30-60 minutes, captures RF measurements on all four carriers, photographs sight lines and ingress, confirms pad surface. 2-5 business days, no charge for serious-buyer engagements.
- **Full on-site survey** — 2-4 hour engagement with a senior solutions engineer. Coverage mapping per camera, mast height analysis, neighbor / nuisance considerations, lighting analysis for IR vs. visible. Quoted per site for complex deployments.

Deliverable: a site plan PDF with proposed camera positions, view fields, mast height, expected RF, and pad requirements.

## Phase 2: Pre-deployment

- Trailer prep at the regional staging facility — battery balance check, firmware update to current release, camera focus verification, SIM activation, AI rules pre-loaded
- Customer access coordination — gate codes, escort requirements, badging, insurance certificates
- Permits where required — most municipalities do not require permits for a trailer; some HOAs and a handful of city codes do. We handle the filing.

## Phase 3: Transport

Our fleet runs DOT-compliant CDL drivers across owned tractors and a vetted contract network. Transport insurance is $1M per load. Most metro markets are reached from a regional yard within an 8-hour drive window.

Typical timelines:

- **Same-day** — 60-mile radius from a regional yard, request received before 10am local
- **Next-day** — 250-mile radius, request received before 5pm local
- **72-hour** — anywhere in the continental US not already covered by the above

Hawaii, Alaska, and Puerto Rico are quoted separately; ocean freight adds 7-21 days.

## Phase 4: On-site install

Standard install runs 90-180 minutes per trailer:

1. Position and level — hydraulic outriggers, bubble level, 2-degree tolerance
2. Mast deployment — 12-30 ft typical, wind-rated to 75 mph deployed
3. Camera aim and focus — verified against the site plan via on-platform live view
4. Cellular antenna alignment — peaked for best RSRP on primary and secondary carriers
5. Solar panel orientation — magnetic south, tilt-adjusted for latitude
6. Configuration push — AI rules, retention policy, user permissions, alert routing
7. SOC handoff — operator confirms inbound stream, AI events firing, audio talk-down test
8. Customer walkthrough — site contact gets the portal login and a live demo on their site

A typical 4-camera install with a 2-person crew completes in under 2.5 hours.

## Phase 5: Redeploy

The most common operational request: move the trailer. New phase of a construction project, new event venue, seasonal retail, post-storm response.

Redeployment is treated as a fresh install — survey (usually remote for repeat customers), transport, level, reconfigure. Same crew network. 24-72 hour standard turnaround within a state, 5-7 days cross-country.

For multi-site customers with frequent rotations, we provision a regional swap inventory — a spare trailer staged within a few hours of the highest-rotation sites. Redeploys against the swap inventory take hours, not days.

## Coverage footprint

Active deployments today across all of the lower 48 except for Vermont and Wyoming. Eight regional yards positioned to keep transport drives under 8 hours for ~92% of US population centers. Field-tech network covers every market we deploy into.

## What is not included by default

- Concrete pad construction (most sites use the existing ground; gravel and packed dirt are fine)
- Power tie-in (trailers are solar-autonomous; AC power is optional for high-density camera builds)
- Long-term lease of customer-owned land — that is between the customer and the property holder
