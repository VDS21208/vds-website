Most security failures aren't camera failures. They're install failures, mounting failures, and "no one cleaned the lens in two years" failures. We do the boring work that keeps the rest of it working.

## Permanent install

Mast-mount, building-mount, pole-mount, parking-deck mount. Concrete coring, conduit runs, weatherproofing, lightning arrest, and grounding to code. We pull permits where required and coordinate with your GC if you're on an active project.

What we install:
- VDS-branded fixed cameras and PTZ
- Third-party cameras you specified (Axis, Hanwha, Bosch, Hikvision-replacement programs, Verkada migrations)
- Mast extensions and parapet mounts
- Solar-only power kits for buildings without exterior conduit
- Cellular backhaul kits where running fiber isn't practical

## Retrofit on existing camera infrastructure

If you already have 40, 200, or 2,000 cameras, the install service that matters is the one that doesn't ask you to throw them away. Our VMS speaks ONVIF and RTSP — we connect to your existing infrastructure, add analytics and SOC monitoring, and only replace cameras when the math says replace.

Typical retrofit:
- Inventory audit of existing cameras, NVRs, and network
- ONVIF/RTSP connectivity test, firmware update on supported models
- Replace truly dead cameras (usually 5–15% of fleet)
- Cloud VMS cutover with side-by-side parallel operation
- SOC onboarding once analytics are tuned

Average retrofit cost is 12–18% of the cost of a full rip-and-replace.

## Preventive maintenance

Quarterly site visits on every camera under contract:
- Lens cleaning (the single highest-impact maintenance item)
- Housing seal inspection
- Mount torque check
- Cable continuity and PoE budget verification
- Firmware updates
- IR illuminator test
- Battery and solar health on mobile units
- DVR/NVR health check (where applicable)

Reports filed in the customer portal. Annual deep-service includes mast inspection, lightning arrest test, and full housing reseal.

## Redeployment

Construction sites change phases. Lay-down yards relocate. Substation outages end. When a perimeter shifts, the trailers and fixed-mast units move with it. Redeployment is included in managed contracts. For owned hardware, it's a flat-fee service call per unit.

## Emergency service

24/7 dispatch for client-down events. SOC sees a camera offline before you do — and dispatches our field tech with the customer's authorization. Average on-site response in metro markets: under 6 hours. Rural sites quoted per the field tech coverage map.

## Who does the work

W-2 field technicians, not 1099 contractors. Background-checked. OSHA-30 minimum. Tower-climber certified where height work is required. Insured under VDS general liability and workers' comp — your project doesn't carry the risk.

We subcontract only for trenching, electrical above 600V, and licensed crane work. Every subcontractor goes through our vendor compliance check.
