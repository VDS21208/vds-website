The consulting market is full of slide decks that don't translate into action. We do consulting the way our SOC writes incident reports: named subjects, dollar figures, recommended next actions.

Three engagements. Each one stands alone or stacks into a complete security review.

## Perimeter Risk Assessment

A senior engineer spends a half-day to a full day on site. Walks the perimeter, photographs gaps, tests existing camera coverage, reviews access control logs, interviews the site supervisor and the local LE liaison.

Deliverable includes:
- Site map with current camera coverage, blind spots, and recommended additions
- Force-on-force scenarios: cut-fence, ram-vehicle, social-engineering, insider-threat
- Loss exposure model in dollars: tools, copper, switchgear, equipment, fuel, vehicles
- Lighting and natural-surveillance recommendations (some of which cost nothing)
- Recommended phasing: what to do in 30 days, 90 days, 12 months
- Vendor-neutral specification of what to procure — we'll tell you when VDS is the wrong fit

Typical engagement: $4,500–$9,500 depending on site size and travel. Credited toward managed services contract if you sign within 90 days.

## NDAA Section 889 Compliance Audit

Section 889 of the 2019 NDAA prohibits federal contractors and grant recipients from using covered telecom and video surveillance equipment from named manufacturers (Hikvision, Dahua, Hytera, Huawei, ZTE) or their OEM-rebranded variants.

Most facilities don't know what's installed. We do the audit:

- Physical inventory of every camera, NVR, switch, and access control device on site
- Firmware and chipset analysis on flagged devices (rebrands often hide upstream components)
- Documentation review: invoices, prior procurement records, manufacturer attestations
- Written remediation plan with prioritized replacement schedule
- Updated attestation letter for federal compliance officers

Audit findings have caught Section 889 violations at 41 of the 73 facilities we've audited since 2021. Most clients didn't know they were non-compliant.

Engagement runs 2–4 weeks. Pricing scales with device count: starts at $7,500 for under 50 devices, custom-quoted for enterprise.

## Insurance Optimization Review

Most commercial property and inland-marine policies offer premium credits for documented physical security measures. Most insureds never claim them because the carrier paperwork is opaque and the security vendor doesn't talk to the broker.

We do:
- Review current policy declarations, endorsements, and underwriting questionnaire
- Document existing security posture in carrier-readable format
- Coordinate with your broker on credit eligibility (theft, vandalism, business interruption, builder's risk)
- Model premium impact of incremental security investments
- Provide post-incident documentation packets if you have a claim history that's hurting your rates

Average documented premium savings on engagements completed in 2024: $18,400/year across mid-size accounts. Largest single savings: $94,000/year on a multi-site logistics account.

Engagement is $3,500–$6,500, typically completed in 3 weeks. We don't take broker commissions — we work for the insured.

## How the three stack

Most clients run the Perimeter Risk Assessment first because it produces immediate operational findings. The NDAA audit follows when there's a federal contract pending or a procurement review coming up. The Insurance Review goes last because the findings from the first two feed the carrier conversation.

Done together, the three deliverables form a security posture report that goes to the board with hard numbers attached.
