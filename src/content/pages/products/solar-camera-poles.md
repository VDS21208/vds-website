## When a trailer is the wrong tool.

Trailers are designed to move. If a site is staying put for two years or more, the wheels and registration are dead weight. Fixed solar poles trade redeployability for a lower lifetime cost, a smaller footprint, and a permanent install that survives a hard winter without service calls.

## What's on the pole

- 4K or HD camera options
- Optional PTZ
- AI analytics on edge plus cloud
- Optional LPR
- Solar array sized to site latitude and shading conditions
- Battery reserve sized for local weather conditions
- Cellular backhaul standard; hardline available

## Where they earn their keep

Permanent yards. Long-term construction parks. Solar farms. Substations. Storage facilities. Municipal lots. Schools and campuses. Any site where the trailer registration would expire before the project ends.

## How they connect to the platform

Same AI VisionStream account as your trailers. Same SOC. Same audit log. Mix fixed and mobile across a portfolio without managing two vendors.

## Pricing

Configured per site. Most fixed pole deployments price lower per year than equivalent trailer leases when the engagement runs 24+ months.
