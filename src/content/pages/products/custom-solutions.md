## Why this page exists

Most of what we sell ships out of our Pikesville plant the same way every time. But every week we get a request that doesn't fit a SKU — a federal contractor with a specific NDAA flow-down, an integrator wanting a private-labelled VMS, a multi-site retail customer who wants a custom alert routing playbook, a transit agency adding gunshot detection to a trailer.

This page exists because saying "no" to those requests would mean leaving real solutions on the table. We say yes.

## What we customize

### Custom hardware configurations

Stock is a baseline, not a ceiling. We build trailers and wall-mounts to spec.

- **Non-standard sensor packages** — thermal LWIR, ground radar, gunshot detection, environmental sensors, dual LPR fronts.
- **Mast and mount variations** — taller mast, side-mount platforms, ruggedized housings for marine or arctic environments.
- **Power systems** — extended battery reserve, hybrid solar+grid, generator interlocks for hostile-environment deployments.
- **Federal/defense-grade** options — full NDAA Section 889 build, TAA-compliant cellular modules, hardened encryption modules.

### Custom AI analytics

The AI VisionStream platform ships with ten production analytics. We add eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth ones for customers who need them.

- **Custom detection models** trained on your data — equipment-class detection, uniform compliance, vehicle-make classification.
- **Custom alert routing** — region-specific escalation, time-of-day rule sets, integration with your dispatch or ITSM tools.
- **Custom dashboards** — KPI views, multi-tenant rollups, compliance reporting exports.
- **API and webhook delivery** of detection events into your stack.

### White-label and private-tenant deployments

For dealers, integrators, and large enterprise:

- **White-label AI VisionStream** with your brand, your domain, your colors.
- **Private SOC playbook** — your escalation contacts, your dispatch SOPs, your incident report format.
- **Custom SLAs** — guaranteed response times, dedicated SOC seats during peak hours.

### Bundled multi-unit deployments

When you need a fleet — not a unit — we design the whole deployment.

- **Site planning** with placement diagrams, sight-line analysis, and coverage maps.
- **Logistics** — staging, delivery, commissioning, decommissioning at end of contract.
- **Dedicated account engineering** for the duration of the contract.
- **Quarterly business reviews** with incident reports, false-alarm metrics, ROI tracking.

### Dealer and specifier configurations

If you're an integrator, dealer, fed/SLED specifier, or A&E firm:

- **Spec-sheet-ready configurations** with full BOMs, drawings, and submittals.
- **NDAA Section 889 attestation** documentation provided up front, not after the PO.
- **Channel pricing** and registered deal protection for committed partners.
- **Co-marketing** — case studies, joint webinars, dedicated SE support.

## How a custom engagement actually works

1. **Discovery call.** 30 minutes, no slide deck. You describe the site, the threat model, the constraints. We ask hard questions.
2. **Proposed approach.** Within a week. Hardware BOM, software scope, timeline, dependencies — not a brochure.
3. **Statement of work.** Once approach is approved, formal SOW with milestones and acceptance criteria.
4. **Build and deploy.** We build at our Pikesville plant and commission on site. Engineering stays attached through go-live.
5. **Operate.** SOC, VMS, and quarterly reviews continue as long as you need them.

## Who this is for

- Federal contractors and prime contractors with NDAA flow-down requirements.
- State and local agencies with specific procurement language.
- National enterprises with multi-site portfolios and custom dispatch needs.
- Integrators and dealers building branded offerings on top of our stack.
- A&E firms specifying mobile surveillance into RFPs and submittals.
