## Built for crews who do not have 90 minutes to babysit a deployment.

Most "rapid-deploy" trailers in our category require two people, leveling jacks, mast guy-wires, and a separate cellular commissioning step. Swift Deploy was redesigned in 2023 around a single-operator workflow. Drop legs, raise mast, power on. The cellular link self-registers. AI VisionStream sees the unit before the operator gets back in the truck.

That redesign is why this is the most-deployed unit in our fleet across 30+ states.

## What's on the mast

**HD PTZ camera, 30x optical zoom.** Reads a face at 300ft and a plate at 200ft. Pan-tilt-zoom controllable from any device on AI VisionStream.

**Dual-spectrum imaging.** Full-color night vision without infrared illuminators that announce the unit's presence. Pairs visible and low-light sensors so footage stays usable from dusk through dawn.

**AI analytics.** On-edge person and vehicle detection. Cloud classifiers filter out wildlife, weather, and vegetation movement so operators only see real events.

**Optional LPR.** 95%+ accuracy, sub-3-second plate alerts, immutable log.

**Strobe and PA.** Active deterrence with SOC-triggered live voice challenge.

## Power and form factor

- **Mast.** 20ft, single-operator manual lift

## The deployment claim, broken down

"Under 20 minutes, one person, no tools" is the spec. Here is what that looks like in field time:

- 0:00 — arrive on site, position trailer
- 2:00 — drop stabilizer legs
- 5:00 — raise mast
- 8:00 — power on, cellular handshake
- 12:00 — first live frame in AI VisionStream
- 18:00 — operator confirms scene, adjusts PTZ presets

That is the median across our last 200 deployments. We have field data.

## When to choose Swift Deploy over Sky Guard MSU

Pick Swift Deploy when your sites are well-lit at night, your perimeter is under 300ft, and your primary threat is human intrusion captured by a PTZ camera. Pick Sky Guard MSU when you need thermal, radar, and active deterrence on the same unit, or when sites lose visibility to weather or terrain.
