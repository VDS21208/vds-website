## Why thermal and radar belong on the same trailer.

Cameras lose people in shadow, weather, and dense vegetation. Thermal LWIR sees heat through all three. Radar sees motion through all three plus fog and rain. Run them together and you have detection that does not care what time it is or what the weather is doing. Run only one and you have a blind spot.

Sky Guard MSU is the only mobile unit in our catalog that ships with both, plus a 4K multi-sensor camera, LPR, and active deterrence — strobe and PA — on a single 22ft mast.

## Detection stack

**Thermal LWIR.** Detects body heat at long range in zero visible light. Trained AI classifiers on AI VisionStream filter wildlife and weather from human intrusion.

**Ground radar — 500ft.** Detects motion through fog, smoke, and heavy rain. Pairs with thermal for two-modality verification before an alert ever reaches an operator.

**4K multi-sensor camera.** Wide situational coverage plus tight PTZ-class detail on the same mast. Daylight evidence quality, full-color night vision via dual-spectrum optics.

**LPR.** 95%+ accuracy, sub-3-second alerts, immutable plate log feeding the same audit trail as your video.

**Active deterrence.** Multi-stage strobe and PA. SOC operators issue live voice challenges before law enforcement is dispatched. 97% of intrusions resolve at the audio challenge step — measured across our active fleet.

## Power and environmental

- **Solar.** 600W panel array, sized for full operation at 38N latitude with three consecutive overcast days
- **Battery.** 48+ hour reserve at full draw including thermal, radar, PTZ, illumination, and cellular
- **Enclosure.** IP66 — dust-tight, hose-down rated
- **Operating range.** -20F to 120F. Tested in Minnesota winters and Arizona summers
- **Mast.** 22ft, manual or hydraulic lift options
- **Backhaul.** Dual-SIM cellular standard. Starlink, point-to-point microwave, and hardline options on request
