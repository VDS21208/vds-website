## What it is

A self-contained mobile security trailer that turns any site into a monitored perimeter inside of a working day. Solar-powered. Cellular-connected. Built to run unattended, in the field, in any weather.

The unit pairs **three 4MP cameras for full 360° coverage** with a **PTZ HD camera** that delivers 25x zoom and full pan/tilt control. A 22 ft heavy-duty mast lifts everything above truck height for unobstructed sight lines.

## How it works

**No power. No internet. No on-site infrastructure.**

- **Solar with battery reserve.** High-efficiency panels and battery backup run the unit for 5–7 days in zero-sun conditions. Cloudy weather is a non-event.
- **4G cellular link.** Live video, alerts, and remote command flow over cellular. No site wiring, no IT department needed.
- **Hardware deterrence.** Police-style strobe lights and a 120 dB speaker are built in. SOC operators can issue live audio challenges from anywhere in the world the moment a threat is verified.
- **Tamper-resistant.** Heavy-duty frame, removable hitch, deployable outriggers, and GPS tracking. The unit reports its position continuously.
- **30 days of footage on board.** Plus continuous cloud sync to AI VisionStream. Pull clips by event, by time, or by camera.

## Where it fits

The Mobile Surveillance Unit is the right call when you need to put surveillance in front of a problem in days, not months — and walk it away when the problem moves. It is the workhorse of our deployed fleet across 30+ states.

- **Construction sites** where the boundary changes every week and copper, tools, and equipment go missing on weekends.
- **Parking lots and retail** with after-hours liability and ORC exposure.
- **Critical infrastructure and utility** sites where hardline runs aren't feasible.
- **Public safety** — short-term deployments at hot spots, special events, and recovery operations.
- **Auto dealerships** monitoring vehicle inventory across a sprawling lot at night.

## Why customers choose it over fixed installs

A fixed install on a pole or building requires power, conduit, network, permits, and a return visit when the site changes. A Mobile Surveillance Unit does not.

You roll it in. You roll it out. The same unit covers six different sites in a year. The lease pays for itself the first time you avoid a theft, an incident, or a fixed-install bid.
