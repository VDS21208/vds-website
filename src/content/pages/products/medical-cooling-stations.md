## Heat illness is a citation and a lost day.

OSHA's heat injury and illness rules are getting stricter every cycle. Sites without compliant shade, cooling, and rest areas get cited and lose crew time. A delivered welfare unit solves both problems before the inspector arrives.

## Configurations

- **Cooling stations.** HVAC-equipped enclosed shade, seating, water dispensing, electrical service
- **Medical stations.** First-aid integration, cot space, sink, HVAC, secure storage for medications and AEDs
- **Combo units.** Cooling plus medical in a single chassis for sites that need both
- **Solar option.** Solar-powered HVAC for sites without grid power

## Where they earn their keep

Construction in heat-stressed regions. Outdoor industrial sites. Event venues. Disaster response staging. Agricultural operations. Any site running an OSHA-watched workforce in temperature extremes.
