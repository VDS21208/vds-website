## What it is

A trailer-mounted solar lighting tower designed to illuminate large work areas, perimeters, and event spaces — without fuel, without noise, and without daily refills. Automated mast operation gets the lights up in minutes.

## Where it earns its place

- **Construction sites** with night crews or high-risk after-hours exposure.
- **Public-safety perimeters** — hot-spot policing, recovery operations, large-event detail.
- **Critical infrastructure** maintenance windows.
- **Disaster response** and FEMA-style staging areas where diesel logistics are a burden.

## Why customers run it next to our surveillance trailers

A visible-light deterrent and a surveillance camera complete each other. Light tells the intruder "you are seen." The camera, the SOC, and the audio challenge tell them "and you will be responded to." Together they shut down opportunistic intrusion before it becomes an event.

## Features

- **Solar-powered** with battery reserve for multi-day off-grid runs.
- **Automated mast** with wide-area illumination angles.
- **Fuel-free** — zero fuel logistics, zero emissions, zero generator noise.
- **Tamper-resistant** trailer design and GPS tracking.
- **Compatible** with existing VDS surveillance deployments for unified site coverage.
