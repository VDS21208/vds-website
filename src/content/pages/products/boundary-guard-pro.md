## Most trailers solve a point. This one solves a line.

A PTZ trailer is excellent at covering one location and useless at covering a 600ft fence line. Operators have to pan to a problem after motion is already in progress. By the time the camera is on the intruder, the intruder is at the fence.

Boundary Guard Pro is built around overlapping fixed sensors plus PTZ for verification. The whole fence is in frame the whole time. Motion triggers the right sensor; PTZ snaps to the event for high-detail capture and SOC verification.

## How the coverage works

- **Multi-sensor array** covers 500+ linear feet without gaps
- **Overlapping fields** mean intrusion at any point along the line triggers verified detection within seconds
- **PTZ verification** auto-cues to the alerted sensor
- **AI analytics** filter wildlife, weather, and vehicle pass-bys

## Power and form factor

## Where it earns its keep

Long construction fence lines. Material yards. Solar farm perimeters. Utility substations. Quarries. Logistics yards. Anywhere "we need to watch the whole fence, not just the gate" is the requirement.

A typical 1,000ft perimeter runs two Boundary Guard Pro units for full coverage. A 2,000ft perimeter runs three units staggered. We will scope it.
