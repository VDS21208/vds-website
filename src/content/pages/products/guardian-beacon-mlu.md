## What it is

A compact, all-in-one solar lighting unit for task and perimeter coverage where a full trailer-mounted tower won't physically fit. Think gates, alleys, secondary access points, internal lot coverage, supplementary fence-line lighting.

## Features

- **All-in-one form factor** — light, panel, and battery in one footprint.
- **Multi-day battery reserve** with full daylight recharge.
- **Silent and fuel-free** — no generator, no diesel logistics.
- **Quick deploy** — one person, minutes.
- **Stackable in a fleet** — string multiple units along a perimeter.

## Where it earns its place

- **Tight access points** where a trailer can't site.
- **Supplementary perimeter coverage** alongside Sentry Tower or surveillance trailers.
- **Construction subgrades** and interior yard sections.
- **Events** with restricted vehicle access.
