## Lumens first. Surveillance optional but useful.

Light trailers are an obvious tool for night work. We make ours optionally smart — add a camera package and the trailer that lights the area also watches it, feeds AI VisionStream, and ties into your SOC subscription.

## Configurations

- **Solar LED.** Quiet, no fuel, multi-day battery reserve
- **Diesel LED.** Runtime-priority configurations for extended deployments off solar
- **Hybrid.** Solar primary, diesel backup for high-runtime sites
- **Camera-integrated.** Add a PTZ or fixed camera and you have a one-chassis lighting and surveillance unit

## Where they earn their keep

Construction night shifts. Roadwork. Event lighting. Disaster response. Temporary parking lot lighting. Any site that needs lumens after dark plus a record of who was there.
