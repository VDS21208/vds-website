## Trailers do not fail. Mounts, batteries, and signage do.

Seven years of deployments tell us where field failures actually come from. The camera works. The cloud works. The unit gets clipped by a forklift, the battery cycles past 1,500 charges, or somebody steals the signage. This catalog is the parts that fix those problems before they become a service call.

## Catalog

**Anti-tamper kits.** Tamper-evident enclosures, lock upgrades, GPS tracking for mobile units, vibration alerting.

**Additional batteries.** Battery bank upgrades for sites with shading, high cellular draw, or extreme cold cycles.

**LPR add-on.** Retrofit LPR onto Swift Deploy and Solar Pole units. 95%+ accuracy, sub-3-second alerts.

**Signage.** Warning, surveillance, and trespass signage — printed, posted, and rated for outdoor multi-year exposure.

**K-rail and barrier mounts.** Mount cameras and lights to concrete K-rail without trenching or core drilling.

**Cellular and Starlink upgrades.** Backhaul redundancy and Starlink kits for remote sites.

**Spare parts.** Camera modules, mast hardware, solar panels, charge controllers, replacement panels. Same-week shipping from Pikesville, MD.

## How accessories integrate

Every add-on is built for the same AI VisionStream and SOC pipeline as the base unit. LPR retrofitted to a Swift Deploy reports to the same audit log as a Sky Guard's factory LPR. Anti-tamper triggers route through the same SOC operators.
