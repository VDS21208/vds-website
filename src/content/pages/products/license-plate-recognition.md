## What this product is

A two-part LPR solution. Use either one or both.

**1. Dedicated LPR solar camera kit (hardware).** A purpose-built solar-powered camera designed specifically for license-plate capture — correct optics, correct mounting angle, correct shutter behavior for plates at speed. Drop it at a gate, driveway, or access road. 4G cellular, IP67 rugged, multi-day battery reserve. The same field-proven enclosure as our [Solar-Powered Surveillance Kit](/products/solar-powered-surveillance-kit), tuned for LPR.

**2. LPR AI analytic (software).** The Automatic License Plate Recognition (ALPR) analytic in AI VisionStream. ONVIF/RTSP compliant — runs against any compatible camera, including cameras you already own. Capture, OCR, watchlist matching, alerting, and historical search — all in one analytic.

You can run either, or both. Most customers start with one of each and expand.

## How it works

LPR uses purpose-built cameras and computer vision to capture plate images, OCR them in milliseconds, and match against inclusion or exclusion lists. The result flows into AI VisionStream as a structured event with the captured image, plate text, timestamp, and camera location.

From there:

- **Alert** your SOC, dispatch, or building access system within 3 seconds.
- **Search** historical records by plate, partial plate, time window, or location.
- **Integrate** with parking, access control, and incident response systems via API.

## Common deployments

- **Auto dealerships** — log every plate in and out of inventory lots, alert on after-hours activity.
- **Construction sites** — vehicle access logging, contractor verification, vehicle theft prevention.
- **Retail and shopping centers** — repeat offender alerting, organized retail crime tracking.
- **Multi-tenant residential** — resident vs. non-resident, unauthorized parking enforcement.
- **Public safety** — hot list alerts integrated with dispatch.

## Accuracy and performance

- **>95% read accuracy** in real-world conditions with proper camera placement.
- **<3 second alert latency** end-to-end.
- **24/7 operation** in any lighting condition with IR-capable variants.
- **Multi-lane and multi-angle** support for entry/exit configurations.

## What it integrates with

- **AI VisionStream** — the analytic ships in the platform. No separate dashboard.
- **24/7 US SOC** — operators can be paged on watchlist hits with live video.
- **Your stack** — REST API and webhooks deliver events into dispatch, ITSM, building access, parking, and incident systems.
