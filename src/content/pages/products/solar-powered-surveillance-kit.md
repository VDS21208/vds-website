## What it is

A standalone solar-powered camera system that deploys anywhere you can mount it. No trenching, no conduit, no IT integration. You unbox, mount, and connect to AI VisionStream.

Think of it as the **single-point** version of our trailer: same VMS, same SOC, same AI analytics — focused on watching one specific asset.

## Key features

- **Up to 9 days of battery life** even in low-sunlight conditions.
- **AI-powered analytics** — onboard classification of human and vehicle activity, with smart alerts that filter out animals, weather, and lighting changes.
- **IP67 waterproof** rugged design tested up to Beaufort level 12 (hurricane-force) winds.
- **Wireless 4G connectivity** — ideal for remote locations with no wired infrastructure.
- **Intelligent energy-saving mode** for extended operation through long off-grid stretches.

## Ideal applications

- **Construction sites** — pole-mounted at the gate or trailer office.
- **Remote areas** — well sites, comms towers, agricultural perimeters.
- **Parking lots** — single-asset coverage to supplement a trailer or wall-mount fleet.
- **Farms & ranches** — barn, gate, fuel tank, livestock crossing.
- **Critical infrastructure** — substations, pump stations, perimeter fence points.

## How it pairs with the rest of the stack

- **AI VisionStream cloud VMS** — same dashboard as every other VDS camera.
- **24/7 US SOC monitoring** — optional, billed per camera.
- **Camera-agnostic platform** — kits can sit alongside existing fixed cameras and trailers in one operator view.
