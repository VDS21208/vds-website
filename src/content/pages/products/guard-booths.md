## Built to last past the project.

Most prefab guard booths on the market are wood-framed boxes with a window. They warp in heat, leak in rain, and need replacement inside three years. Ours are heavy-gauge steel frames with insulated panels, HVAC, electrical service ready, and bullet-resistant glazing and walls available where the threat profile requires it.

## Configurations

- **Standard.** Steel frame, insulated walls, HVAC, electrical service, full-height door, large viewing windows
- **Bullet-resistant.** UL-rated glazing and wall panels — Level I through Level IV depending on threat profile
- **Sizes.** Single occupant up to multi-occupant with restroom integration
- **Custom.** Logo, paint, signage, and interior layouts on request

## Manned, unmanned, or hybrid

Put a guard inside, mount VDS hardware on the roof, or both. The booth becomes the access control point — vehicle screening, ID verification, package inspection — while AI VisionStream watches the perimeter and the SOC handles overnight coverage.
