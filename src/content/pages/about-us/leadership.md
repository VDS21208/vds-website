## How leadership operates

VDS is privately held and founder-operated. There is no board of outside investors directing quarterly priorities. The executive team meets weekly. Operations reviews happen daily at the SOC. Engineering reviews happen bi-weekly. Customer escalations above $50,000 in contract value route to a co-founder within 4 business hours.

The organizational depth below the founders includes:

- **VP of Operations** — owns SOC staffing, training, and quality metrics.
- **Director of Engineering** — owns hardware revisions, firmware releases, and VMS feature roadmap.
- **Director of Sales** — owns enterprise pipeline and the dealer program.
- **Director of Customer Success** — owns post-deployment account management and renewals.
- **Head of Manufacturing** — owns the Pikesville assembly line and supplier relationships.

Total VDS headcount is in the published 25–50 range as of 2026. We are not optimizing for headcount growth. We are optimizing for revenue per deployment and customer retention. Our 12-month logo retention is 94% across all contract tiers.

## What we do not do

We do not outsource the SOC. We do not white-label monitoring through a third-party central station. We do not sell customer data to insurance carriers or analytics platforms. We do not accept advertising on the VMS interface. We do not run "growth experiments" on live customer accounts.

Those constraints cost us margin. They are non-negotiable for reasons stated on the Trust Center page.
