## Why VDS content has named authors

Most security-vendor content is written by marketing copywriters working off an interview transcript and a content brief. We do it differently because the audience for VDS content — security directors, risk managers, construction project executives, federal procurement officers — can tell within two paragraphs whether the author has done the work.

Every published article on visiondetectionsystems.com carries a named author from the operating team. Bios are factual: role, tenure, specific operational experience, professional credentials where relevant. We do not list pet names, favorite books, or college mascots. The audience is here for technical and operational credibility, not personality.

If you're a journalist, analyst, or industry researcher looking for a named source quote, the contact options below route directly to the named author or their support team.

## Editorial standards

VDS content follows a defined editorial process:

- **Named author accountability** — every published article carries a single named author responsible for technical accuracy
- **Internal technical review** — engineering or operations review before publish for any article making operational claims
- **Citation discipline** — operational claims cite source data (FBI UCR, NRF NRSS, NICB, customer-authorized field data); statistical claims link the underlying source
- **Correction policy** — errors are corrected in-place with a dated correction notice; substantive corrections are noted at the article footer

We do not run articles ghostwritten by contract agencies. We do not publish syndicated content under VDS bylines. If an article appears under a VDS author name, that author wrote it or directly edited it.

## Subject matter expert availability

Journalists, analysts, and customer security teams can request direct conversation with any named author for background, on-record quotes, or technical deep-dives. Routing is via the press kit page for media or the contact channel below for non-media inquiries.

Standard response time: two business days for non-time-sensitive requests, same-business-day for media on deadline.
