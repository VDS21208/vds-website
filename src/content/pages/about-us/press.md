## Boilerplate

**100-word version:**
Vision Detection Systems (VDS) is America's integrated mobile security infrastructure company. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Pikesville, Maryland, VDS designs and manufactures solar-autonomous mobile surveillance units, operates a camera-agnostic cloud video management system, and staffs a 24/7 US-based Security Operations Center. The company has deployed over 500 units across more than 30 states for construction, perimeter, lot, and remote infrastructure security. VDS is NDAA Section 889 compliant and an AWS Cloud Partner.

## On-record executive quotes

For story-specific quotes from David Weingot or Joe Kruk, contact the press desk with the story topic, publication, deadline, and 2–3 questions. Standard quote turnaround is 24 hours for daily-news cycles and 3 business days for feature pieces. We do not pay for placement and do not offer exclusives in exchange for coverage.

## Customer interviews

Customer testimonials and case-study interviews require customer approval. We can introduce credentialed press to specific customers for named case studies. Customer-supplied logos and quotes used in the case study library are pre-cleared for press reuse.

## What we do not provide

- We do not provide product loaners for hands-on press review at this time
- We do not provide non-public financial data
- We do not respond to anonymized "industry source" inquiries without attribution
- We do not pay for editorial placement, sponsored content, or analyst coverage
