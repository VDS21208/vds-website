This is not a feature comparison page. These are the eight structural decisions we made differently than the rest of the industry — and the proof points for each.

## 1. Full-stack ownership

We manufacture the hardware, operate the cloud VMS, and staff the SOC. One company, three layers, one accountable owner.

**Proof:** Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro, solar camera poles, guard booths, cooling stations, and light trailers are assembled by VDS in Pikesville, MD. AI VisionStream runs on AWS under our control. The SOC operates from our facility, staffed by VDS employees.

## 2. In-house US Security Operations Center

The SOC is not contracted. It is not offshored. It is VDS staff, in the US, monitoring your sites.

**Proof:** <60 second escalation time. 97% false alarm reduction through human verification before dispatch. Operators are trained on your specific site rules — entry points, expected vehicles, after-hours protocols.

## 3. Your data is yours

We do not sell footage. We do not train third-party models on your video. We do not move data out of the US.

**Proof:** TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, US-only data residency, 12-month audit log retention. AWS Cloud Partner under contractual data-handling controls. Your data export is available on demand in standard formats.

## 4. Genuine NDAA Section 889 compliance

Not "NDAA-compatible." Not "NDAA-ready." Genuine 889 compliance, traceable down to the component.

**Proof:** Bill-of-materials traceability for every unit assembled. No Hikvision, Dahua, Huawei, ZTE, Hytera, or Dahua-subsidiary components in our supply chain. Documentation available on request for federal, utility, and critical-infrastructure customers.

## 5. Assembled in Maryland

Pikesville, MD assembly plant. Quality control, programming, and integration done in-house.

**Proof:** Every unit is built, configured, tested, and inspected at our facility before deployment. Replacement parts ship from the same facility — typically same-day or next-day for active service contracts. See /about-us/our-plant for the full walkthrough.

## 6. Manufacturer, not reseller

We do not mark up someone else's hardware. We design and build our own.

**Proof:** Sky Guard MSU and Swift Deploy chassis, mast assemblies, solar integration, control electronics, and firmware are VDS-built. Cameras and solar panels are sourced from named, NDAA-compliant vendors and integrated by VDS engineers. The warranty is ours — not a passthrough.

## 7. Transparent, all-inclusive pricing

One monthly number. Hardware, VMS, SOC monitoring, cellular data, and support are bundled. No hidden per-camera VMS fees, no per-event SOC charges, no surprise data overages.

**Proof:** Published price ranges by product line. Quotes itemize every line. Contract terms are spelled out — no auto-escalator clauses, no per-incident surcharges. If your site needs change, we re-quote in writing before any change takes effect.

## 8. Seven years of field data

We have been deploying since 2017. 500+ units across 30+ states. That is 7 full seasons of weather, theft attempts, false alarm patterns, and edge-case learning.

**Proof:** Our AI detection thresholds, SOC escalation rules, and hardware spec revisions are calibrated against real field data — not lab tests. We have logged what fails in the desert, what fails in -20F, what triggers wildlife false alarms in the southeast, and what break-in patterns look like on construction sites at 3 AM. Customers benefit from every site that came before theirs.

## The summary

If you only need one of these eight, plenty of vendors will sell you one. If you need the whole stack — accountable, US-owned, compliant, and priced predictably — there is a short list. We are on it.
