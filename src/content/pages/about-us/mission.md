## Why VDS exists

In 2017, the founders walked job sites where the security plan was a guard in a folding chair, a camera nobody was watching, and a sign that said "Premises Under Surveillance." When something happened — copper theft, equipment vandalism, an after-hours break-in — the response time averaged 18 to 40 minutes. By then the trailer was empty.

That gap between "we have cameras" and "someone is actually doing something about this right now" is the problem we set out to fix.

## The three gaps

We started VDS because site security in the field has three structural failures, and most vendors only address one.

**1. Cost.** Permanent fiber-trenched, hardwired surveillance costs $40K–$120K per acre and takes weeks to install. For a 90-day construction project or a seasonal yard, that math never works. Sites stay uncovered, and losses get written into the budget as "shrinkage."

**2. Blind spots.** Fixed cameras see a fence line. They do not see the lot, the staging area, the back row of equipment, or the access road. Guards see one quadrant at a time. Most sites are 60–80% un-monitored at any given moment.

**3. Response time.** Even when something is captured on camera, the average time from event to dispatched response is 18+ minutes industry-wide. Footage is great for insurance. It is not security.

## What we built

VDS is built as a manufacturer, not a reseller. We assemble our hardware in Pikesville, MD — Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro, solar camera poles, guard booths, cooling stations, light trailers. We run the cloud VMS (AI VisionStream) on AWS. We staff our own 24/7 SOC in the US.

That stack matters because the three gaps are connected. You cannot fix response time if your camera vendor, your cloud vendor, and your monitoring vendor are three different companies pointing at each other. You cannot guarantee NDAA compliance if you are reselling whatever the distributor had in stock. You cannot keep pricing predictable if every layer is marked up by someone else.

So we own the layers. One number to call. One company accountable for the outcome.

## What "integrated mobile security infrastructure" actually means

It means the camera, the power source, the chassis, the network, the VMS, and the human watching the screen at 3 AM all answer to the same company. It means a Sky Guard MSU deployed in Arizona and one deployed in Maine pull data into the same platform, get monitored by the same SOC, and run on the same audit log. It means when a customer asks "who is watching this site right now," the answer is a name, not a vendor chain.

## The commitments

These are the things we do not compromise on, regardless of price pressure or growth pressure.

- **Manufacturer, not reseller.** We assemble. We integrate. We program. We own the warranty.
- **Genuine NDAA Section 889.** Every camera, every component — not "NDAA-compatible" marketing language.
- **In-house US SOC.** Not contracted offshore. <60s escalation, 97% false alarm reduction.
- **Your data is yours.** US-only data residency, TLS 1.3 / AES-256, 12-month audit log, no third-party data sale, ever.
- **Camera-agnostic platform.** If a customer has existing cameras that meet our compliance baseline, they integrate. We do not lock customers into our hardware.
