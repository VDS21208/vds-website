## Procurement and contract vehicles

### Sourcewell awarded contract holder
VDS is an awarded contract holder on the Sourcewell cooperative purchasing program. Sourcewell contracts are available to public agencies, education institutions, and non-profits across the US and Canada — over 50,000 participating entities — and let qualifying buyers procure VDS without running their own RFP.

This matters operationally because it shortens procurement cycles from months to weeks for municipal, county, K-12, higher education, and public agency buyers. The Sourcewell contract terms, pricing, and scope are documented and audited by Sourcewell — buyers don't have to re-litigate any of that.

Contract number and current eligible product list are available on request.

## Technology partnerships

### AWS Cloud Partner
VDS is an Amazon Web Services Cloud Partner. AI VisionStream runs on AWS US regions exclusively, and the partnership designation reflects validated technical alignment with AWS architectural best practices, security controls, and data residency commitments.

For enterprise customers running AWS at scale internally, partner status simplifies vendor-to-vendor due diligence — your team can verify our AWS posture through AWS's own partner channels.

## Compliance attestations

### NDAA Section 889 compliance
VDS hardware and operational stack comply with National Defense Authorization Act Section 889(a)(1)(A) and 889(a)(1)(B). We do not use covered telecommunications equipment or services as defined under the statute. Attestation documentation is available for federal contractors, defense suppliers, grant-funded programs, and any prime contractor requiring 889 representations from their vendors.

This is not a marketing claim — it's a documented attestation, refreshed annually and tied to specific hardware bills-of-materials and operational architecture.

### State-level procurement compliance
VDS hardware is also compliant with state-level procurement restrictions on specific Chinese-manufactured camera brands (Hikvision, Dahua) that exist in Florida, Texas, Georgia, and other states. The compliance overlap with NDAA 889 is substantial but not identical — attestation documentation distinguishes both.
