## What it is like to work here

VDS is small enough that you will meet the founders in your first week and large enough that you will not be doing three jobs at once. Headcount is in the 25–50 range. Decisions get made fast. Customer problems get answered fast. The work is operational, not theoretical.

Engineering ships firmware to units in the field — the kind that get vandalized, baked in 105°F sun, and rained on. Operations runs a 24/7 SOC with measurable response time targets. Sales talks to GCs, facility managers, and dealers who will tell you in plain language whether the product solves their problem. Manufacturing assembles units in Pikesville and inspects every chassis before it ships.

What we hire for, in order:
1. Will the person do the work
2. Can the person be trusted with a customer relationship
3. Does the person have the specific skills the role requires

That order is intentional.

## Compensation and benefits

- Competitive base salary, calibrated to role and experience
- Health, dental, and vision insurance (employer-contributing)
- 401(k) with company match after 6 months
- Paid time off, paid holidays, and paid parental leave
- Tools, equipment, and certifications relevant to the role, fully reimbursed
- Continuing education stipend for technical certifications

## Open roles {#open-roles}

### Hardware Assembly Technician
**Location:** Pikesville, MD (on-site)
**Type:** Full-time

Build, test, and inspect VDS mobile surveillance units on the Pikesville assembly line. Solar arrays, battery banks, camera modules, control electronics, cellular uplinks, and chassis. Read a wiring diagram, follow a build sequence, use a torque wrench, document a build sheet.

**What we need:**
- 2+ years assembly, electromechanical, or electrical work
- Comfortable with hand tools, basic soldering, and 12V/24V DC wiring
- Able to read schematics and follow written build procedures
- Detail-oriented; documents and labels work as it goes
- Lift 50 lbs, work in a shop environment with PPE

**What is nice to have:**
- Background in solar installation, RV/marine electrical, or fleet vehicle build-out
- Familiarity with low-voltage IP camera systems

**To apply:** Email resume to careers@visiondetectionsystems.com with subject "Hardware Assembly Technician."

### Administrative Coordinator
**Location:** Pikesville, MD (on-site)
**Type:** Full-time

Keep the operations side of VDS running. Customer billing coordination, vendor invoice processing, shipping coordination with the SOC and dealer channel, executive scheduling support, office logistics. This is the role where, if you do it well, the entire company runs better.

**What we need:**
- 3+ years administrative or operations coordination experience
- Excellent written communication and follow-through
- Comfortable with QuickBooks, Google Workspace, and a ticketing system (we use HubSpot)
- Ability to keep confidential information confidential
- Self-directed; you will not be micromanaged

**What is nice to have:**
- Background in B2B services, security industry, construction services, or fleet operations
- Notary commission

**To apply:** Email resume to careers@visiondetectionsystems.com with subject "Administrative Coordinator."

### Other roles — speculative resumes welcome

We hire continuously across:

- **Security Operations Center operators** — 24/7 shifts, US-staffed only, paid training
- **Firmware and embedded engineering** — Linux on edge devices, C/C++, low-power systems
- **Cloud platform engineering** — AWS, React, Node.js, video pipelines at scale
- **Field service technicians** — regional, US-based, drive a service truck, fix what is in the field
- **Account executives and dealer channel managers** — security industry experience preferred
- **Customer success managers** — own enterprise renewals and expansion

If your background fits one of these and there is no posted role, send a resume with the area of interest in the subject line. We respond within 5 business days.

## How hiring works

1. Resume submission via email
2. 30-minute phone screen with the hiring manager
3. 45–60 minute role-specific interview (technical, operational, or scenario-based)
4. On-site visit to Pikesville for final-round candidates (travel reimbursed for out-of-area candidates)
5. Reference check and offer

We do not run multi-day interview loops. We do not give homework assignments longer than 2 hours. We give a decision within one week of the final interview.
