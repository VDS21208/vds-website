## This is not the steady-state product

Most VDS deployments are 12-month or longer programs — a construction site through completion, an auto dealership year-round, a utility substation indefinitely. Tactical is the opposite use case: short-duration, time-critical, often unpredictable.

We built the tactical practice because customers kept calling us. A retail district had three smash-and-grab incidents in a week and needed cameras up by Friday. A municipality needed surge coverage after a homicide cleared an evidence radius. A utility had to redeploy crews and equipment to a substation after Hurricane Helene took out two of theirs.

The hardware is identical to our steady-state fleet. The operating model is different.

## What tactical deployments cover

### Post-incident surge coverage
A theft, vandalism, assault, or other documented incident has occurred. The customer needs visible deterrent and forensic coverage at the affected site within days, not weeks. Typical duration: 30-90 days, sometimes longer if criminal proceedings extend the evidence window.

### Severe weather and disaster response
Hurricanes, wildfires, ice storms, and tornadoes displace assets, open security gaps, and create staging areas that didn't exist 48 hours earlier. VDS has deployed mobile units for utility mutual-aid staging, FEMA contractor camps, and temporary materials laydown yards after major weather events. Solar-autonomous matters here — grid power may be out for weeks.

### Special events
Festivals, large public gatherings, sporting events, political conventions. Cameras need to be up the day before crowds arrive and gone the day after. Permitting is usually handled by the event host; we plug into the existing security command structure.

### Government and law enforcement temporary operations
Task force operations, surge deployments to high-incident corridors, temporary checkpoints, evidence-recovery perimeters. LE customers get a dedicated channel to our SOC and direct radio integration with on-scene units when operationally appropriate. NDAA Section 889 compliance matters here — many of these contracts require it.

### Construction phase coverage gaps
A long project has a 60-day window where the permanent system is being retrofitted, or a phase hand-off creates a temporary blind spot. Tactical fills it without restructuring the master agreement.

## How tactical billing works

Tactical deployments are quoted at a weekly rate or an event-based fixed fee — not the monthly subscription pricing on our steady-state product pages. Typical structures:

- **Weekly trailer rate** — flat per-week through end of deployment, minimum two weeks
- **Event fixed fee** — agreed up front for a defined start/end window
- **Emergency surge pricing** — applies when deployment is requested inside 24 hours

The 24/7 SOC, AI VisionStream analytics, audio talk-down, and LE dispatch are included on every tactical deployment. Cloud retention defaults to 30 days; longer retention available as an upgrade if evidentiary preservation is a known requirement.

## What we will and won't accept

We accept tactical engagements where the operating profile is clear, the site is accessible to a flatbed delivery, and there is a documented stakeholder on the customer side empowered to authorize SOC escalation protocols.

We decline tactical engagements that are functionally surveillance of identified individuals without legal process, deployments to private property without verified authorization, or anything that would put our SOC operators in a position of making law enforcement judgment calls. The SOC follows escalation trees — it does not improvise.
