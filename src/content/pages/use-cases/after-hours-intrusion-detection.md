## What after-hours intrusion looks like in 2026

The crime window is consistent across construction sites, retail strip centers, auto dealerships, warehouses, and industrial yards: 10pm to 5am. Suspects scout during business hours, identify gaps in lighting and patrol coverage, and return when the site is empty. Tools, copper, equipment, vehicles, electronics — anything portable and valuable is on the table.

The conventional response options each fail differently. Static recorded video provides post-incident footage but no deterrent and no intervention. Guard services run $30 to $50 per hour, or $22,000 to $36,000 per month for 24/7 coverage at one site — and a single guard cannot watch a multi-acre property, gets fatigued, and creates its own liability exposure. Alarm systems with central station monitoring escalate slowly and generate enough false alarms that police response is deprioritized.

After-hours liability is rising in parallel — premises security litigation against property owners has expanded significantly over the past decade.

## How VDS prevents after-hours intrusion

VDS combines automated detection with human intervention. AI VisionStream runs time-of-day rules — after-hours zones arm automatically based on site schedule. The rule set includes person-on-site detection, vehicle loitering, fence-line breach, and perimeter approach. Thermal imaging on Sky Guard MSU detects body heat in low light, through brush, and behind cover. Radar detects movement at the property line before a breach.

When a rule fires, the alert reaches a US SOC operator's console in seconds. The operator pulls live video, classifies the threat, and acts: live audio talk-down through the on-unit speaker, LE dispatch with GPS and direction of travel, evidence preservation. 97% false alarm reduction through AI pre-filtering means the alerts that reach a human are real.

Our Mid-Atlantic GC case study site saw a 73% reduction in theft incidents over 12 months, zero insurance claims, and $340,800 in avoided losses on a $28M project.
