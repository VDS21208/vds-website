## Who this is for

The VDS dealer program is built for security integrators, low-voltage contractors, and managed service providers who already sell to commercial end customers and want to add mobile surveillance and a hosted VMS to the offering. It's not a referral program — dealers transact, fulfill, and own the customer relationship.

If you're looking to refer leads to VDS for a commission, that's our separate referral program. Ask sales.

## Volume tiers

Dealer pricing is structured around trailing-12-month volume. Tiers reset every January 1; new dealers start at Tier 1 and move up based on actual closed volume.

| Tier | Annual Volume | Trailer Discount | Platform Discount | MDF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Authorized** | $0-$100K | 12% off list | 10% off list | — |
| **Tier 2 — Preferred** | $100K-$500K | 18% off list | 15% off list | 1% of revenue |
| **Tier 3 — Premier** | $500K-$2M | 25% off list | 20% off list | 2% of revenue |
| **Tier 4 — Strategic** | $2M+ | Negotiated | Negotiated | 3% of revenue |

Discounts apply to the published list prices on [the main pricing page](/pricing). MDF (Marketing Development Funds) accrues quarterly, paid as co-op against approved marketing spend or as bill credits.

## MAP policy

Minimum Advertised Price is published with the program and enforced. Dealers may discount in private quotes — that's normal commercial behavior — but advertised pricing (website, public ads, public proposals) must hold the line at MAP or above. The reason is structural: published prices on the VDS main pricing page are the floor. Dealers undercutting public pricing in public is what creates the race-to-the-bottom dynamic that destroys margin for everyone.

MAP violations get a written warning on the first incident. Two violations in a 12-month window suspends program eligibility for 90 days. Three suspends indefinitely.

## What dealers get

**Wholesale pricing.** Per the tier table above, applied to any line item on a customer order — trailers, platform subscriptions, SOC monitoring, accessories.

**Dealer portal.** Quote builder, order entry, deal registration, marketing assets, training, and commission/MDF tracking. The portal is self-serve. Most orders never require a sales call.

**Deal registration.** Register a deal with VDS and lock pricing for 90 days, protecting you from another dealer or direct sale undercutting your work-in-flight. Approved registrations get a 5% additional discount on top of tier pricing.

**Technical training and certification.** Free online certification path for sales and field tech roles. Certified Field Tech is the prerequisite for billing customer-facing installs through the dealer channel.

**Marketing assets.** Co-branded brochures, web pages, slide decks, case studies. Customizable with your logo and contact info.

**Marketing Development Funds.** Quarterly accrual at the tier rate, redeemable against approved campaigns (trade shows, paid ads, content production) or as account credit.

**Demo trailer program.** Dealers at Tier 2+ can request a loaner trailer for customer demos. Loan period is 14 days. One loan per quarter at Tier 2, unlimited at Tier 3+.

## What dealers commit to

- Sell only the trailers and platform tiers listed on the current VDS lineup
- Honor MAP on all public pricing and advertising
- Complete the Authorized certification within 90 days of program entry
- Maintain commercial general liability and E&O insurance with VDS named as additional insured
- Use deal registration for any opportunity over $25K
- Submit a forecast and pipeline review quarterly (15-minute call, sales team driven)

## Co-marketing and lead-share

VDS routes inbound leads to dealers based on geography, vertical specialty, and tier. Tier 3 and Tier 4 dealers get first right of refusal on inbound leads in their territory. Tier 1 and Tier 2 dealers get leads on a round-robin basis after Tier 3/4 declines.

Outbound demand-gen co-investment (paid media, trade shows, event sponsorships) is available to Tier 2+ dealers via MDF and proposal-based campaign funding.

## How to apply

The dealer application is short. We're looking for:

- An active business with a security or low-voltage integration history
- General liability insurance ($1M minimum, $2M preferred)
- A named technical contact and a named sales contact
- Brief overview of your geographic and vertical focus

Most applications are approved within 5 business days. We'll schedule a 30-minute kickoff to walk through the portal, deal registration, and certification before your first quote goes out.
