## When purchase beats lease

Lease economics work best for deployments under 30 months. Above that, the math typically flips toward purchase. We've watched customers run the spreadsheet — here's the rule of thumb:

| Deployment horizon | Recommended model |
|---|---|
| <12 months | Lease, no question |
| 12-30 months | Lease, with optional buyout at end of term |
| 30+ months, single site | Purchase |
| Fleet, mobile across multiple sites | Lease (the flexibility outweighs the savings) |
| Capex-only budget cycles | Purchase, regardless of horizon |

The break-even on Sky Guard MSU is roughly 18 months — $2,200 × 17.3 months = $38,000. After 18 months of ownership, every additional month is platform + maintenance only.

## Capex vs opex — how customers actually decide

The choice usually comes down to four factors:

**Budget structure.** Many enterprise security buyers have capex authority but limited opex. Trailers as a capex line are easier to approve than recurring lease payments. Government, education, and federal buyers in particular often prefer purchase for budget cycle reasons.

**Deployment longevity.** Single-site, permanent or near-permanent deployments — a port, a substation, a long-cycle data center build — favor purchase. Roving construction, event security, and disaster response favor lease.

**Refresh strategy.** Owning a trailer means owning the refresh decision. Cameras improve. Solar improves. Cellular standards shift (4G → 5G → whatever's next). Lease customers refresh implicitly when we update the lease pool. Purchase customers refresh when they decide to.

**Tax treatment.** Section 179 expensing and bonus depreciation may make purchase materially more attractive in a given tax year. We're not your CPA — talk to yours.

## Warranty terms

**Standard warranty (included, 12 months):**
- All trailer hardware against manufacturing defect
- Cameras, mast, solar panels, batteries, cellular hardware
- Excludes: customer-caused damage, acts of nature beyond design spec, neglect
- Parts shipped within 48 hours; labor billed at standard rate

**Extended warranty (optional, 3-year):**
- 12% of purchase price, paid up front
- Same coverage as standard, extended to 36 months total
- Adds 50% labor allowance on covered repairs
- Adds one free battery bank replacement at year 3

**Lifetime platform compatibility:**
We commit, in writing, that any trailer purchased from us will remain platform-compatible for a minimum of 7 years from delivery. Older trailers may not get every new feature, but they will not be EOL'd off the platform short of that horizon.

## Post-sale service

Ownership means you own the maintenance. We offer three service tiers for trailer owners:

| Plan | Price | What's covered |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-serve** | $0 | Remote firmware updates, platform support, parts at list |
| **Standard service** | $99/unit/mo | Above + 50% parts discount + 2 service calls/year included |
| **Premium service** | $249/unit/mo | Above + unlimited service calls + 4-hour response SLA + annual on-site PM |

Most purchase customers run Standard service. Premium is typical for fleet owners with mission-critical deployments where downtime cost exceeds the plan price.
