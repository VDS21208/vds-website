## How platform pricing works

One dimension: per camera, per month, at the tier you pick. A 12-camera site on Pro is $69 × 12 = $828/month. A 40-camera campus on Standard is $39 × 40 = $1,560/month. There's no per-seat fee, no per-event fee, no analytics module to unlock at extra cost.

You can mix tiers across a fleet but not within a site — every camera at one physical site runs the same tier. This is a practical limit, not a billing trick; the analytics pipeline runs per-site for a reason.

## Which tier fits

**Standard ($39/cam/mo).** Single-site customers, general surveillance, no analytics requirements beyond person/vehicle motion. This is the right tier for the small construction site, the auto lot, the self-storage facility, the rural property. It's not stripped down — it's a complete VMS with 30-day retention and a real mobile app.

**Pro ($69/cam/mo).** The tier most multi-site customers settle on. Advanced analytics (line-crossing, loitering, PPE, crowd density) turn the platform from a recording system into a detection system. API access enables integration with dispatch, access control, and ticketing. Pro is the default tier on VDS trailer fleets running across 5+ sites.

**Enterprise (custom).** SSO, full audit logging, custom retention beyond 90 days, custom analytic models, and a dedicated CSM. Required for most federal contracts, regulated industries (utilities, healthcare, financial), and customers running 100+ cameras. Pricing is configuration-dependent — expect $50-$80/cam/mo equivalent in most configurations.

## SOC add-on

Every tier supports adding the VDS Security Operations Center for **$399/site/month**. SOC is priced per site, not per camera, so a 30-camera site costs the same to monitor as a 5-camera site.

What you get for $399:
- 24/7 live monitoring of priority alerts
- <60-second escalation on verified events
- Voice-down talk-down where hardware supports
- Direct dispatch coordination with your designated responders (police, internal security, on-call)
- Customer playbook configuration (what counts as priority, who gets called when)
- Weekly event summary report

The SOC is staffed in the US, trained on the VDS platform, and operating under ESA monitoring standards. Adding or removing SOC mid-subscription requires only a 30-day notice.

## Retention windows and why they matter

Retention isn't just a number — it's what determines whether you can answer the questions that come up two months after an incident.

- **30 days (Standard)** — fine for routine surveillance, investigations that surface within a month, and customers who plan to download evidence as soon as something happens
- **60 days (Pro)** — covers most insurance claim windows, employee dispute investigations, and the typical interval before a previously-unnoticed incident gets reported
- **90+ days (Enterprise)** — required for many regulated industries, federal contracts, and long-cycle dispute resolution

Customers can pull video to local archive at any time. Retention applies to cloud-resident video only.

## Billing, terms, and changes

- **Billing cycle:** monthly, invoiced on the 1st
- **Payment:** ACH preferred, credit card accepted up to $10K/month
- **Tier changes:** upgrade anytime (prorated), downgrade at next billing cycle
- **Add/remove cameras:** anytime, prorated
- **Cancellation:** 30-day notice, no early termination fees on month-to-month
- **Annual prepay discount:** 10% off list when paid 12 months in advance
