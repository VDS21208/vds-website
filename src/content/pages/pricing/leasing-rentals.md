## Standard lease terms

VDS trailer leases are designed to be readable in five minutes. The terms below apply to every lease unless you negotiate an enterprise master agreement.

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| **Minimum term** | 3 months |
| **After minimum** | Month-to-month, 30-day notice to cancel |
| **Security deposit** | One month's lease, refundable |
| **Lead time (standard)** | 5-10 business days |
| **Lead time (expedited)** | 48-72 hours, premium applies |
| **Freight** | Billed at cost, both ways |
| **Relocation between sites** | Free, 48-hour notice required |
| **Platform included** | Standard tier ($39/cam/mo equivalent) |
| **SOC monitoring** | Optional, $399/site/mo |

No setup fees. No activation fees. No "platform onboarding" fees. The lease number on your quote is what you pay every month for the trailer itself.

## What's included with every lease

- **Hardware** — trailer, mast, cameras, solar, batteries, cellular hardware
- **Platform access** — Standard tier subscription bundled with the lease price
- **Standard maintenance** — firmware updates, remote diagnostics, parts replacement
- **Cellular data** — dual-carrier LTE/5G, unlimited data on standard plans
- **Cloud storage** — 30-day video retention on AWS US-East/West
- **NDAA compliance documentation** — provided with every unit

## What's not included

- SOC active monitoring ($399/site/month, add or remove anytime after the first 30 days)
- Starlink uplink ($150/month, optional)
- On-site service calls beyond standard maintenance (T&M, only billed when a non-VDS cause requires truck roll)
- Customer-caused damage (covered by deposit + insurance)
- Platform upgrades to Pro or Enterprise

## Short-term and event rentals

For events, temporary jobsites, and incident-response deployments, we lease trailers under three months at a premium. Common use cases: festivals, sporting events, film sets, disaster recovery operations, temporary lay-down yards for major projects.

| Term | Pricing |
|---|---|
| **30 days** | 1.5x standard monthly rate |
| **60 days** | 1.25x standard monthly rate |
| **90+ days** | Standard rate |

Short-term rentals include the same hardware, platform, and freight handling as standard leases. SOC monitoring is highly recommended for events and is available on a per-night basis if a full month doesn't fit.

## Long-term and multi-year fleets

Multi-trailer, multi-year deployments get materially better economics. Common structures:

- **12-month commit:** 5% discount off standard lease rate
- **24-month commit:** 10% discount, includes one free trailer swap mid-term
- **36-month commit:** 15% discount, includes platform Pro tier upgrade
- **Fleet (5+ trailers):** volume matrix, negotiated MSA

Fleet customers also get a dedicated CSM, quarterly site review, and prioritized service scheduling.

## Lead time, in detail

Standard: **5-10 business days** from signed lease to on-site, including SOC onboarding and customer playbook configuration.

Expedited: **48-72 hours** for active incident response. We hold a small inventory of staged trailers in three regional depots (Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, West) for exactly this purpose. Expedited freight and overtime mobilization fees apply.

Multi-unit deployments of 3+ trailers may extend lead time depending on the trailer configuration. We'll quote the exact date with the quote.

## Early termination

If you cancel after the 3-month minimum, you owe nothing beyond the current month. Cancel mid-month and the remainder of that month is billed.

If you cancel during the minimum term, the remaining months of the minimum are payable. There is no separate ETF on top of that.

If you signed a multi-year commit, early termination is governed by your MSA — typically a glide-path payment that reflects unrecovered mobilization cost.
