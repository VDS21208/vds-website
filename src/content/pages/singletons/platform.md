## Software-led, hardware-honest

Most "AI security platforms" are a UI wrapper over a third-party VMS, a third-party AI model, and a third-party monitoring center that bills hourly. AI VisionStream is one stack, built by one company, since 2017.

Camera-agnostic ingest. AWS US-only data residency. Analytics tuned on 7 years of field footage. A SOC in our building, not a contractor's. The mobile trailer in your parking lot runs the same software as the rooftop camera on your headquarters.

## What you get on day one

- **Camera-agnostic ingest** over ONVIF and RTSP. Axis, Hanwha, Bosch, and 40+ others. Bring what you own.
- **Rule-driven analytics**: intrusion zones, virtual tripwires, loitering windows, crowd thresholds, time-of-day rules.
- **License plate recognition** with 95%+ accuracy and sub-3-second alerts on hotlist matches.
- **24/7 US Security Operations Center** with under 60-second escalation, live audio talk-down, and direct LE dispatch with live video.
- **Mobile app** (iOS + Android) for live multi-camera view, alert acknowledgement, and two-way audio.
- **Integrations** with Lenel, Genetec, Brivo, Bold, Stages, ServiceNow, Jira, PagerDuty, and Slack.
- **30, 60, or 90-day cloud storage** with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest.
- **Immutable 12-month audit log** on every user action, every alert, every camera event.

## Why one stack matters

Three vendors means three SLAs, three support queues, and three places to blame when an alert is missed at 2:47 a.m. We own the camera, the network, the cloud, the AI, and the operator. One number to call. One contract. One root-cause analysis.

## Compliance and privacy that holds up under audit

NDAA Section 889 compliant. AWS Cloud Partner. US-based operators. US-only AWS regions. No Dahua. No Hikvision. RBAC and MFA on every account. A 12-month audit trail you can export to your SIEM.

We do not publish a camera feed to the public internet. We do not sell footage. We do not run pattern-of-life databases on anyone driving by.

## Built for sites that move

Construction yards, multi-family developments, auto dealerships, logistics yards, retail rollouts, utility substations. Mobile trailers redeploy in under 30 minutes. Fixed-site installs pull video from the cameras you already paid for. The platform does not care which.
