## What the demo is

A 30-minute video call with a VDS sales engineer and, when relevant, a SOC supervisor or product manager. The agenda:

- **Minutes 0–5:** Your situation. What you are deploying, what you are evaluating against, what the timeline looks like.
- **Minutes 5–15:** Live VMS walkthrough. We log into a live demonstration tenant with anonymized real data. You see camera feeds, alerts, audit logs, RBAC, and reporting in their working state.
- **Minutes 15–22:** SOC workflow. We play through a recorded P1 incident — alert generation, AI pre-filter, operator triage, talk-down, dispatch — exactly as it ran with a real operator on a real customer event.
- **Minutes 22–28:** Hardware review. Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro shown on camera with deployment specs and field photos. We answer questions on solar autonomy, battery life, NDAA, and integration.
- **Minutes 28–30:** Next steps. Quote, technical scoping call, RFP response, or "we will follow up in 90 days" — whichever fits.

You will not see a slide deck unless you specifically ask. You will not be asked qualifying questions you already answered when booking.

Bookings include automatic calendar invitation with a video call link, a 15-minute pre-demo questionnaire (optional), and a reminder 24 hours before the call.

## Who comes on the demo from your side

- **Decision-makers and influencers welcome.** Security director, operations VP, IT, finance, procurement — bring the team if relevant.
- **Technical buyers welcome.** Bring your IT or security architecture lead and we will go deep on the platform. Engineering joins the call by default if you flag "technical depth" in the booking notes.
- **Procurement and compliance welcome.** We will hand off to Trust Center documentation in real time if it is a compliance-led evaluation.

If you want a custom configuration on the demo — for example, integrating one of your existing camera streams into a sample tenant — flag this in the booking notes at least 24 hours in advance. We will set up the demo environment to match.

## What to have ready

Nothing required. Most useful, if you have them:

- A general sense of deployment count and timeline
- Your current site security cost baseline (guard hours, prior losses)
- Any specific compliance or technical requirements (NDAA, SSO, SIEM integration)

If you do not have these yet, the demo will help you scope them.

## After the demo

You will get an email within 1 business day with:

- Recording of the demo (if you opted in during the call)
- Documentation referenced during the call (Trust Center, NDAA, case studies)
- Next-step recommendation — quote, scoping call, RFP response, or "no follow-up needed"

We do not start a multi-touch nurture sequence after the demo. If you do not want follow-up, say so on the call. We track that flag and respect it.

## If the demo time slips

We get it. Reschedule from the original calendar invitation, or email sales directly. We will rebook within 48 hours.
