## How VDS runs webinars

Each session is 45 minutes plus open Q&A. Slides are working slides, not marketing decks. The presenter is the practitioner — our SOC operations lead, deployment engineering manager, or a customer running the program in production. Recordings post within five business days. Registrants get the deck and the recording link by email.

We do not gate archive recordings behind extended forms. Registration captures email and company; everything else is opt-in.
