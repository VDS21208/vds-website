At VDS, "solutions" is not a euphemism for product. It is a library of cost and decision pages built for people who are 30 days or less from buying mobile surveillance.

We sell three things together — solar-autonomous trailers, a camera-agnostic cloud VMS, and a 24/7 US-based Security Operations Center — and the procurement questions are predictable. How much does a trailer cost per month. How does $399/site/mo for SOC monitoring compare to $60K-$110K per FTE for a guard. Should we lease for opex or buy for capex. What does the break-even look like at month 24.

These pages exist because most security vendors won't publish numbers. We will.

## What you'll find here

**Cost pages** — full pricing for each line item, including what's included in the rate, what costs extra, and how to model annual TCO. We publish our own numbers ($1,500-$2,200/mo for trailers, $39-$69/cam/mo for platform, $399/site/mo for SOC) and benchmark against industry rentals ($1,200-$3,400/mo).

**Decision pages** — frameworks for the three buy-side decisions that drive 80% of our discovery calls: remote monitoring vs guards, rent vs buy, and which trailer config matches the site.

**Case studies** — full deployments with measured outcomes. The hero case ($28M Mid-Atlantic GC) cut security spend from $32,400/mo to $4,000/mo, reduced theft 73%, and filed zero insurance claims over 12 months. That's $340,800 saved in year one on a single project.

## The six pages

1. [Mobile Surveillance Trailer Cost](/solutions/mobile-surveillance-trailer-cost) — purchase ($30K-$60K) and rental ($1,200-$3,400/mo) ranges, what's included, annual TCO model.
2. [Remote Video Monitoring Cost](/solutions/remote-video-monitoring-cost) — per-site, per-camera, and per-hour pricing for managed SOC services.
3. [MSU Rental Cost](/solutions/mobile-surveillance-trailer-rental-cost) — short-term vs long-term rental economics, deposit, lead time, redeployment fees.
4. [Remote Monitoring vs Security Guards](/solutions/remote-monitoring-vs-security-guards) — side-by-side cost ($4,800/year vs $60K-$110K/FTE), coverage, evidence, and response-time matrices.
5. [Buy vs Rent Mobile Surveillance Trailer](/solutions/buy-vs-rent-mobile-surveillance-trailer) — break-even math (purchase ~ 24 months of rental at typical configs) and the decision tree.
6. [Case Study Examples](/solutions/case-study-example) — measured outcomes across construction, retail, logistics, and utility deployments.

## Who these pages are for

Construction security directors costing a new project. Retail loss prevention leaders trying to defend a budget request. Logistics ops managers replacing guards at a yard. Utility and municipal procurement officers comparing trailer rentals to a capex purchase.

If you are comparing vendors, the pricing tables here are accurate as of this quarter and updated against published competitor rates. If you are building an internal business case, the TCO models and break-even calculators are the same ones we use on discovery calls.

If you are not 30 days from a decision, our resources library has technical specs, deployment guides, and the full case studies cited above. Start there.
