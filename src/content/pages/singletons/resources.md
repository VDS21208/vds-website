## What lives here

This hub is the documentation layer for everything VDS deploys, monitors, and bills for. We publish field data, not marketing decks. If a number appears on this site, it came from a customer site, a SOC log, or a billing record.

## Case studies

Verified outcomes from named deployments. Each case study includes the threat profile, the hardware configuration, the SOC response model, and the dollar value of avoided loss across the measurement window. Anchor study: a $28M Mid-Atlantic GC that cut theft 73% and saved $340,800 in 12 months with two Swift Deploy units and three Solar Camera Poles.

## What we do not publish

We do not publish customer logos without written permission, theft footage without a signed release, or claim-rate statistics we cannot tie to a billing record. Every dollar figure on this site is a real number from a real deployment.
