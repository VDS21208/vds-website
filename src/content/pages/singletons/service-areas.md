## Three deployment tiers, mapped to drive time from staging

VDS operates a hub-and-spoke logistics model anchored at our Pikesville, Maryland headquarters with regional staging in Texas and additional secondary sites added as deployment density warrants. Trailer dispatch SLA is a function of distance from staging, permitting requirements, and current fleet utilization — not a marketing tier.

### Same-day deployment (under 12 hours from contract)

Available in Maryland, Washington DC, Northern Virginia (Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, Arlington, Alexandria), and Southern Pennsylvania (Lancaster, York, Chester, Delaware, Philadelphia, Bucks, Montgomery counties).

For sites inside this radius, a Sky Guard MSU or Boundary Guard Pro trailer can be on the ground, solar-aligned, cellular-connected, and registered with the 24/7 SOC inside 12 hours of contract signature. We hold standing inventory at the Pikesville depot specifically to support same-day calls.

### 24-72 hour deployment

Available across the rest of the Eastern Seaboard (NY, NJ, CT, MA, RI, NH, VT, ME, DE, WV, NC, SC, GA, FL), the Midwest corridor (OH, IN, MI, KY, TN), and all of Texas (Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, Austin, El Paso metros).

These deployments dispatch via flatbed from the closest staging hub. We commit to a 72-hour ceiling and average 41 hours from PO to operational across this footprint based on 2025 deployment data.

### Standard deployment (5-10 business days)

Available across the remaining states we serve: AL, MS, LA, AR, OK, MO, IL, WI, MN, IA, KS, CO, AZ, NV, UT, NM, plus pilot footprint in CA and WA.

Standard scheduling reflects long-haul transport, permit lead times in some jurisdictions, and pre-deployment site survey scheduling. Quoted in business days, tracked in your customer portal from PO through GPS arrival ping.

## What "deployment" actually means at VDS

A deployment is not a drop-and-leave. Every trailer arrival includes:

- Site survey verification against the pre-deployment design
- Solar panel orientation and tilt set against latitude
- Cellular signal validation (we require >-95 dBm RSRP for primary carrier)
- Camera azimuth and detection-zone calibration
- SOC handshake — analytics live, talk-down audio tested, escalation tree confirmed
- Customer portal account provisioning with RBAC roles assigned

Deployment is not complete until the SOC dashboard shows the unit green and the first test alert routes correctly.

## Don't see your state?

We expand based on demand. If you operate a multi-site program in a state outside the current 30+ footprint, our deployment engineering team will scope what regional staging and dispatch logistics would cost for your portfolio.

Three of our current standard-tier states were added in 2024-2025 because a single enterprise customer asked. We are not interested in symbolic national coverage — we add states when the operational math works.
