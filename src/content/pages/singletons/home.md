## The problem with most "mobile security."

A trailer with a camera on it is not security. It is a recording device. Construction theft costs the industry more than $1B per year and less than 7% of stolen equipment is recovered (FBI). Retail organized crime is up 18% YoY (NRF). Cargo theft tops $15B annually. Footage after the fact does not solve any of that.

Active deterrence with human verification does. So does sub-3-second alerting. So does live audio challenge through a PA mounted to the unit. So does direct law enforcement dispatch with live video already in the officer's hand.
