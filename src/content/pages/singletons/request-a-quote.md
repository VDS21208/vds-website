## How this works

The quote process is two short forms. The first is what you are deploying. The second is who you are and how to reach you. We do not gate the quote behind a 14-question qualification form. We do not require you to answer "what is your budget" before showing you a number.

## Step 1: What you are deploying {#step-1}

[PLACEHOLDER — Step 1 form embed]

The first form asks:

- **Number of sites** — single site, 2–5 sites, 6–25 sites, 26+ sites
- **Sites per location** — how many units per typical site
- **Site type** — construction project, perimeter, lot/yard, energy/infrastructure, mixed
- **Timeline** — within 30 days, 30–90 days, 90+ days, evaluating only
- **Contract preference** — month-to-month, multi-year, operating lease, project-based, undecided
- **Special requirements** — NDAA attestation, federal contract, GovCloud, custom integration

Estimated time: 3 minutes.

## Step 2: Who you are

After step 1, you will be asked for:

- **Name and title**
- **Company name and industry**
- **Business email**
- **Phone (optional)** — used only for the quote conversation, not for sales sequences
- **Preferred response method** — email or phone
- **Anything else we should know** — open text field

Estimated time: 2 minutes.

## What happens after submission

- **Within 2 hours:** Confirmation email with a quote reference number
- **Within 1 business day:** Initial scoping email from a VDS sales rep with clarifying questions if any
- **Within 2 business days:** Quote document delivered by email — includes hardware MSRP, monthly monitoring, available terms, and any deployment-specific fees

The quote is good for 30 days. Pricing on the standard product lines does not change within that window. Custom-configured deployments may require a follow-up engineering review before the quote is finalized.

## What is in a VDS quote

A standard VDS quote document includes:

- **Configuration summary** — units, model, monitoring tier, contract term
- **Monthly recurring cost** — broken out by line item
- **One-time costs** — mobilization, custom configuration, training (if applicable)
- **Contract term and options** — month-to-month, multi-year, lease alternatives where applicable
- **Service level summary** — SOC response, support hours, replacement policy
- **Compliance attestations** — NDAA, AWS, encryption standards
- **Sample MSA section references** — for procurement teams who want to review terms before signing

Quotes are sent in PDF and editable formats for procurement processes that need to extract data.

## If you need a quote faster

Standard turnaround is 2 business days. Faster turnaround is available if you have an active emergency, an immediate-deployment timeline, or a procurement deadline. Note the urgency in the open-text field and we will prioritize. Same-day quotes happen, especially for direct-deployable single-site needs.

## If your situation is complex

Some deployments require a discovery call before we can quote accurately:

- Multi-site enterprise rollouts with bespoke integration requirements
- Federal contracts with specific clause requirements
- White-label or strategic partner configurations
- Custom hardware modifications

If your situation fits one of these, indicate it in step 1 and we will route you to the right team. The discovery call is free and typically takes 30 minutes.

## What we will not do

- We will not put you in a 30-day nurture sequence after a quote request
- We will not call your office five times in a week
- We will not pass your contact information to a third party
- We will not share pricing on competitors with you (we sell our product, not theirs)
