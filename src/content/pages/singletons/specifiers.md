## What this portal is for

VDS appears in capital project security specifications, RFP responses, and federal/state construction documents because architects, engineers, and security consultants put us there. This portal provides the documentation, technical drawings, and reference materials those professionals need to write VDS into a spec accurately — without having to chase a sales rep for every artifact.

If you spec security systems into commercial, institutional, or government construction documents, this is the page bookmark.

## CSI MasterFormat references

VDS mobile surveillance trailers and the AI VisionStream cloud VMS fit into the CSI MasterFormat under:

### Division 28 — Electronic Safety and Security

- **28 23 23 — Video Surveillance Remote Monitoring and Control** — applies to AI VisionStream cloud VMS, the customer portal, SOC monitoring services, and the camera-agnostic ONVIF/RTSP integration layer
- **28 23 29 — Video Surveillance Remote Storage and Retrieval** — applies to AWS-hosted 30/60/90-day cloud retention, video export, and forensic retrieval workflows

For temporary or mobile deployments specifically, we provide language that overlays Division 01 (General Requirements) procurement and Division 28 technical content. Sample spec language is included in the downloadable pack below.

### Related divisions
- Division 26 (Electrical) — typically not relevant for solar-autonomous deployments
- Division 33 (Utilities) — applicable for permanent communication infrastructure if cellular is supplemented by site fiber
- Division 27 (Communications) — applicable for site integration of cellular/satellite uplink

### Sample specification language
Editable Word documents with CSI MasterFormat-formatted specification sections for each product line. Drop into your project specification with appropriate edits for project-specific scope and procurement structure.

### A&E presentation deck
60-90 minute presentation covering mobile surveillance technology, the VDS product line, SOC operations, deployment logistics, and case studies relevant to the audience type (institutional, federal, commercial). Delivered live by VDS engineering leadership.

We deliver these as in-person, remote, or hybrid sessions and offer AIA CES credit for qualifying audiences pending program approval.

## Dedicated specifier support

VDS maintains a dedicated A&E channel staffed by sales engineering, not general sales. Specifier inquiries route to engineers who have written spec language, sat on bid clarifications, and walked construction administration submittals through approval.

The channel responds to:
- Spec language drafting and review for specific projects
- Pre-RFP technical Q&A and substitution submittals
- Bid clarification questions during open-bid windows
- Construction administration submittal preparation
- Site-survey walk-throughs for projects in design

Contact details are provided after the A&E presentation request below to keep the inbox clean from non-specifier inbound.
