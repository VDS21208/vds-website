## Why partner with VDS

The site security market is large, fragmented, and underserved by traditional fixed-camera integrators. Customers want a 24/7 monitored mobile unit they can deploy in 4 hours without trenching. Most integrators cannot build that themselves. We can. You sell the relationship. We deliver the integrated stack.

The economics work because we own the manufacturing, the VMS, and the SOC. There is no extra vendor between you and the customer outcome. When you escalate a customer issue, it goes to one company — ours.

## What partners get

- **Margin** that supports a real sales motion, not lead-gen pennies
- **Dedicated channel team** for deal support, scoping, and configuration
- **Training program** for sales and field staff, in-person in Pikesville or virtually
- **Marketing assets** — spec sheets, decks, customer-facing one-pagers, ROI calculators
- **Lead routing** — qualified leads in your territory get routed to you
- **Co-branding and white-label options** for high-volume integrators
- **Financing programs** for end customers — lease and multi-year options

## Two partner tiers

**Authorized Dealer.** Standard program. Quarterly commitment, MDF available, lead routing, sales and technical training. Best fit for regional integrators and construction services firms doing 12+ deployments per year.

**Strategic Partner.** White-label or co-branded units, dedicated account team, joint go-to-market planning, custom pricing. Best fit for national integrators, modular construction firms, and large GC service organizations doing 100+ deployments per year.

## What we look for in partners

- Existing customer relationships in our target segments (construction, perimeter, lot, energy, public sector adjacent)
- A working install and service capability, or willingness to subcontract VDS field services
- A direct line to your customers' security and operations decision-makers — not a reseller of resellers
- A clean compliance posture in your own operations (we will ask about NDAA, insurance, and licensing)

## Where to start

If you are a fit, the application process below takes about 20 minutes. We review applications within 5 business days. If we approve, onboarding takes about 30 days from contract signature to first deployment.
