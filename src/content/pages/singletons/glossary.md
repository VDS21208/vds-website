## A definitive glossary of mobile surveillance and AI video security

From NDAA Section 889 to ONVIF and LPR — the terms below define the language of modern site security. Use this glossary to navigate VDS documentation, RFP responses, and procurement conversations.

Bookmark this page or [request a quote](/request-a-quote) to talk to a specialist who speaks every term below in plain English.

## A

### ALPR / Automatic License Plate Recognition

Same technology as LPR. Cameras with optical character recognition that read license plates in real time and compare against a hotlist database. VDS uses LPR with 95%+ accuracy and sub-3-second alerts.

### Audio Talk-Down

A live or recorded voice broadcast through a trailer's PA speaker when a threat is detected. Used by SOC operators to challenge intruders before law enforcement arrives. A core component of active deterrence.

### AES-256

Advanced Encryption Standard with a 256-bit key. The encryption used by VDS for all video and metadata stored at rest. Industry standard for federal and financial-sector data.

### AWS / Amazon Web Services

Cloud computing platform hosting AI VisionStream. VDS uses US-only AWS regions for data residency. VDS is an AWS Cloud Partner.

## B

### BIPA / Biometric Information Privacy Act

Illinois law (2008) governing biometric data collection. VDS does not use facial recognition or biometric matching, so VDS deployments are BIPA-friendly in Illinois.

### Boundary Guard Pro

A VDS fence-line surveillance unit covering 500+ linear feet with no blind spots. Multi-camera configuration. Starting at $1,800/mo.

## C

### Camera-Agnostic

The ability for a video management system (VMS) to accept video from cameras made by any manufacturer that supports the ONVIF or RTSP open standards. AI VisionStream is camera-agnostic — use VDS cameras or any ONVIF/RTSP camera you already own.

### Cargo Theft

Theft of goods in transit or in storage. US logistics industry loses over $15 billion per year to cargo theft. Hot spots: ports, distribution centers, truck yards.

### Catalytic Converter Theft

Theft of catalytic converters from vehicles for the precious metals inside (platinum, palladium, rhodium). Rhodium hit $10,400/oz in 2026. State Farm catalytic converter claims peaked at 45,000 in 2022.

### Cellular Failover

Multi-carrier cellular connectivity (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, FirstNet) that automatically switches to the strongest available signal. VDS trailers also support optional Starlink satellite failover for remote sites.

### Chain of Custody

Documented trail proving who has accessed video evidence, when, and what they did with it. VDS provides an immutable 12-month audit log for every event.

### Construction Theft

Theft of materials, equipment, and copper from active construction sites. The FBI estimates US construction theft exceeds $1 billion per year, and less than 7% of stolen equipment is ever recovered.

### Copper Theft

Theft of copper wire, pipe, and grounding from construction sites, substations, and abandoned buildings. Driven by copper commodity prices. A common VDS use case for utilities.

### CCPA / California Consumer Privacy Act

California law (2018) granting consumers data privacy rights. VDS deployments respect CCPA: customer data is not sold and is isolated per customer.

## D

### Device Health Monitoring

Proactive monitoring of trailer battery state, solar input, cellular signal, camera uptime, and tamper sensors. VDS alerts before failure rather than after.

### DVR / Digital Video Recorder

Older on-site recording device using analog cameras. Largely replaced by NVRs and cloud VMS. VDS does not use DVRs.

## E

### E-E-A-T

Google's Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness rating framework. Used in Search Quality Rater Guidelines and AEO models.

### Edge Computing

Running AI analytics on the camera or trailer (the 'edge') rather than uploading raw video to the cloud. Lower bandwidth use, faster alerts. VDS uses edge analytics on Sky Guard MSU and Swift Deploy.

## F

### False Alarm

An alert triggered by a non-threat (wildlife, weather, headlights, shadows). VDS AI VisionStream filters 97% of false alarms before they reach a SOC operator.

### FERPA

Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act. US federal law protecting student education records. VDS deployments on K-12 and higher-ed campuses respect FERPA — exterior-only surveillance, no classroom interior.

### Fisheye Camera

A wide-angle camera with a 360-degree horizontal field of view. Used for area surveillance where a single camera covers a parking lot or open yard.

## G

### GSA Schedule

General Services Administration purchasing contract used by federal agencies. VDS GSA Schedule is pending.

## H

### HIPAA

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. US health data privacy law. VDS deployments at healthcare facilities are exterior-only (parking lots, ER approaches, behavioral health perimeter); no clinical interior monitoring.

## I

### IP66

Ingress Protection rating: 6 = totally dust-tight, 6 = protected against powerful water jets. VDS hardware is rated IP66 for outdoor durability.

### Intrusion Zone

A virtual perimeter drawn on the camera view. Triggers an alert when a person or vehicle crosses into it. Configurable by time of day.

## L

### LPR / License Plate Recognition

Camera-based OCR reading of vehicle license plates. VDS LPR achieves 95%+ accuracy with sub-3-second alerts on hotlist matches. Used in construction, retail, auto dealers, and parking.

### Loitering Detection

AI analytic that alerts when a person or vehicle stays in an area longer than a configured threshold. Useful for parking lots and storefronts.

### LWIR / Long-Wave Infrared

Thermal imaging in the 8-14 micrometer range. Detects heat from bodies and vehicles in total darkness and through smoke or light fog. Standard on the Sky Guard MSU.

## M

### MFA / Multi-Factor Authentication

Login security requiring a second factor beyond a password (typically a code from a phone or hardware token). Required for all AI VisionStream user accounts.

### MSU / Mobile Surveillance Unit

Industry term for a self-contained surveillance unit (typically trailer-mounted, solar-powered) deployable to any site. VDS Sky Guard MSU is the flagship MSU.

## N

### NDAA Section 889

Section 889 of the John S. McCain National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2019. Prohibits federal agencies and contractors from using video surveillance gear from named Chinese manufacturers (Dahua, Hikvision, Hytera, Huawei, ZTE). VDS hardware is NDAA Section 889 compliant.

### NERC CIP-014

North American Electric Reliability Corporation Critical Infrastructure Protection Standard 014 — physical security requirements for high-impact transmission substations. VDS Sky Guard MSU is configured to support NERC CIP-014 documentation requirements.

### NVR / Network Video Recorder

On-site recording device using IP cameras. Largely being replaced by cloud VMS. VDS does not require NVRs — the AI VisionStream cloud VMS is the system of record.

## O

### ONVIF

Open Network Video Interface Forum. The open standard that lets IP cameras from different manufacturers work with the same VMS. VDS AI VisionStream supports any ONVIF camera.

### Open Graph (OG)

Meta tag protocol used by Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, and others to control how a page appears when shared. Every VDS page includes OG title, description, image, and type.

### ORC / Organized Retail Crime

Coordinated theft of retail goods by groups for resale, often involving flash-mob smash-and-grab events. NRF reports 18% YoY growth and 67% of retailers reporting transnational ORC involvement since 2024.

## P

### Pikesville, MD

VDS headquarters and assembly facility. Located in the Baltimore-DC metro area. All VDS hardware is assembled at the Pikesville plant.

### PTZ / Pan-Tilt-Zoom Camera

A motorized camera that can pan, tilt, and zoom remotely. The Swift Deploy trailer uses a PTZ with 30x optical zoom.

## R

### RBAC / Role-Based Access Control

User permission model where access is determined by role (e.g., Admin, Operator, Viewer). VDS AI VisionStream enforces RBAC on every account.

### Remote Video Monitoring (RVM)

Service in which trained operators watch live camera feeds remotely and respond to events. VDS RVM is delivered by a 24/7 in-house US-based SOC.

### Robots.txt

Plain-text file at the root of a website that tells search engine crawlers which URLs to crawl or skip. VDS robots.txt allows all primary pages and points to the XML sitemap.

### RTSP / Real-Time Streaming Protocol

Open protocol for streaming video from IP cameras. VDS AI VisionStream supports RTSP ingest from third-party cameras.
