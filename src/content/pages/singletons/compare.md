## Why we publish honest comparisons

Most security vendor "vs." pages are sales theater. We take a different approach. Every page on this hub names a competitor, cites public incidents with dates and sources, lists the places that competitor genuinely beats VDS, and shows the places we beat them. If a competitor has the best mobile app in the category, we say so. If they had 150,000 cameras hacked in 2021, we say that too.

This hub indexes eleven detailed comparisons plus two architecture pages (rental vs. purchase, solar vs. diesel). Each page ships with a 12-18 row feature matrix, a sourced incident log, a total-cost-of-ownership math block, and a 3-step migration plan if you're switching to VDS.

## Pick your comparison

**Mobile surveillance and trailers**
- VDS vs. LVT — the largest mobile surveillance player with ~5,000 units deployed
- VDS vs. WCCTV — UK-origin construction rental specialist
- VDS vs. Flock Safety — ALPR network with a January 2026 breach exposing 67 cameras

**Cloud VMS and indoor video AI**
- VDS vs. Verkada — strong UX, $2.95M FTC settlement in 2024
- VDS vs. Spot AI — best-designed indoor video AI, no outdoor or SOC

**Managed monitoring services**
- VDS vs. Pro-Vigil — virtual guards, no first-party platform
- VDS vs. Stealth Monitoring / ECAM — 140,000+ cameras monitored, three brand names in 18 months

**Architecture and approach**
- VDS vs. Security Guards — $1,500-$2,200/mo per trailer vs. $60K-$110K/yr per FTE
- VDS vs. Fixed Cameras — wired install lead time, no redeployment
- Rental vs. Purchase — when to expense it, when to capitalize it
- Solar vs. Diesel — energy density, runtime, refueling cost

## How to use this hub

If you're evaluating two or three vendors at once, open them in tabs. Every matrix uses the same column order so you can compare across pages. If you want a single PDF with every matrix combined, hit the download button in the hero. If you want VDS plugged into your specific site list, book a 20-minute architecture call and bring a site address — we'll pre-pull satellite, sun angle, and cell coverage before the call.
