## One platform. Industry-specific configurations.

We do not sell a generic camera package and call it a "solution." Every deployment starts with a site assessment, a threat profile, and a configuration sized to the actual risk — equipment theft on a construction site behaves differently than catalytic converter theft at a dealership or copper theft at a substation.

The underlying stack is the same across every vertical:

- **Solar-autonomous hardware** — Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard Pro, Solar Camera Poles. No trenching. No utility hookup. Operational in 4 hours.
- **AI VisionStream cloud VMS** — intrusion zones, virtual tripwires, loitering and crowd detection, time-of-day rules, license plate recognition at 95%+ accuracy with sub-3-second alerts.
- **24/7 US Security Operations Center** — Pikesville, MD. <60-second escalation. 97% false alarm reduction. Live audio talk-down and direct law enforcement dispatch.

What changes per industry is camera placement, analytics rule sets, escalation thresholds, and which assets we point the lens at.

## Six primary verticals

**Construction** — General contractors, infrastructure projects, equipment yards. Copper, fuel, generators, tools. FBI estimates >$1B in annual theft with <7% recovery. Our Mid-Atlantic GC case study: $340,800 saved in 12 months, 73% theft reduction, $0 insurance claims.

**Utilities & Substations** — Off-grid substations, transmission yards, water treatment. NERC CIP-014 physical security alignment. Copper theft and vandalism are the dominant loss vectors; solar-autonomous deployment is a natural fit where utility hookups don't exist.

**Retail & Shopping Centers** — Multi-store loss prevention programs, parking lots, ORC and flash-mob response. NRF reports 18% YoY shoplifting increase and 67% of retailers citing transnational ORC involvement. LPR captures getaway plates within seconds.

**Auto Dealerships** — Catalytic converter theft, vehicle theft, after-hours liability. Rhodium is back to $10,400/oz in 2026 and converter claims remain elevated. Entry/exit LPR plus lot coverage closes the gap insurance won't.

**Parking Lots & Garages** — Operators (LAZ, ABM, ParkMobile-class) facing vehicle break-in, vandalism, and assault liability. Center-of-lot Sky Guard plus perimeter poles deliver coverage without trenching the asphalt.

**Government & Public Safety** — Federal, state, municipal property — parks, motor pools, public works yards, water districts. NDAA Section 889 compliant. Available via and GSA cooperative purchasing. This is private-property surveillance, not community-wide camera networks.
