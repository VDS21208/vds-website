## Why threat-specific matters

A "security camera" is not a strategy. A copper theft incident at a substation, a cargo theft from a logistics yard, and a catalytic converter cut in a dealer lot each have a different attacker, time window, approach pattern, and response playbook. Configuring one system to handle all three means none get prevented properly.

VDS builds around the specific threat. Sky Guard MSU thermal + radar for substations where attackers move slowly through cover at 2am. Swift Deploy with LPR at dock entrances where every plate in and out needs to be captured. Boundary Guard Pro across 500-foot fence-lines where intruders test perimeters before committing.

Combined with AI VisionStream rule sets tuned for each threat and a 24/7 US Security Operations Center that escalates in under 60 seconds, the result is what generic surveillance cannot deliver: prevention.
