## Why we publish prices

The mobile surveillance industry runs on opacity. Vendors hide trailer rates behind sales gatekeepers, quote month-to-month customers different prices than fleet customers, and resist publishing platform tiers because comparison shopping kills margin. We've watched it for nine years.

Publishing prices is harder for us, easier for you. You can budget a project before scheduling a call. You can compare us against the competitor that won't tell you what their trailer costs. You can sign a delegated-authority PO without three rounds of negotiation. The trade is worth it.

Below is what we charge. Numbers are net of taxes and shipping. Volume, multi-year, dealer, and government pricing are all available — see the section that applies.

## VDS Platform — per camera, per month

The VMS that runs on every VDS trailer (and on third-party cameras you already own). Camera-agnostic, AWS-hosted, NDAA compliant.

| Tier | Price | Video Retention | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $39/cam/mo | 30 days cloud | Single-site, general surveillance |
| **Pro** | $69/cam/mo | 60 days cloud | Multi-site, analytics-driven |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | 90 days cloud, full feature set | Fleet, federal, regulated |

Standard includes: live view, motion alerts, mobile app, basic analytics, 5 user seats.
Pro adds: advanced analytics (intrusion, line-crossing, loitering, PPE), LPR per-lane add-on, API access, 25 user seats.
Enterprise adds: SSO, audit logging, custom retention, dedicated CSM, white-glove onboarding, unlimited seats.

Full tier breakdown: [Subscription Plans →](/pricing/subscription-plans)
