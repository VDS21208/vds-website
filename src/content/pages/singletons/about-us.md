Vision Detection Systems is America's integrated mobile security infrastructure company. We design, assemble, deploy, and monitor the hardware. We operate the cloud VMS that ingests the video. We staff the SOC that responds when something moves on a perimeter at 3 AM. One vendor. One support number. One throat to choke when a unit goes offline at 2:14 AM on a Saturday.

That integration is the point. The site security industry fragmented decades ago into hardware vendors, software vendors, monitoring centers, and integrators who stitched them together with cable and hope. Customers pay for that fragmentation in finger-pointing, in unmonitored cameras, in 18-minute response times that should have been 47 seconds. We rebuilt the stack so the people who manufactured the unit are the same people who answer the radio when the operator hits the talk-down button.

## What we do

**Solar-autonomous mobile surveillance units.** Sky Guard MSU, Swift Deploy, and Boundary Guard Pro — three platforms covering construction sites, perimeters, lots, yards, and remote infrastructure. No trenching. No grid hookup. Deploy in 4 hours.

**Camera-agnostic cloud VMS.** Hosted on AWS GovCloud-adjacent infrastructure. TLS 1.3 in transit. AES-256 at rest. US-only data residency. Integrates with existing IP cameras alongside VDS units — you are not locked into one hardware ecosystem.

**24/7 US-staffed Security Operations Center.** Live operators. Sub-60-second escalation on verified events. 97% false alarm reduction against industry baseline. Audio talk-down. Police dispatch with verified video evidence packets.

## What makes this different

We have 7 years of field data on units running in coastal humidity, desert heat, Mid-Atlantic winters, and active construction zones. We know what fails at month 14 and what fails at month 38, because we have units that have been running that long. We are not a Series A startup chasing a category. We are a hardware company that learned to run a SOC because the alternative was watching our cameras be ignored by third-party monitoring centers that charged $9 per signal and forwarded everything to the customer at 4:00 AM.

## What we have proven

On a $28M Mid-Atlantic GC project in 2024, our deployment saved the customer $340,800 in 12 months — measured against their prior year's loss, vandalism, and guard-staffing costs. That number is on the case study page with the methodology. It is not the average. It is one project. We publish it because the math is real and the customer signed off.

## What is non-negotiable

NDAA Section 889 compliance on every component. AWS-backed infrastructure with formal partner status. RBAC and MFA
