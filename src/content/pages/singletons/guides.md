## Why these guides exist

Most "security buyer guides" are written by marketing teams that have never stood up a unit in a flood plain at 2 a.m. Ours aren't. Every guide here is built from active deployments, SOC incident logs, and procurement documents we've already filed with federal, state, and utility customers.

Use them to write specs, defend budgets, and shorten vendor evaluation from months to weeks.

## The guides

**[NDAA Section 889 Compliance Guide](/guides/ndaa-compliance)** — What Section 889 prohibits, how it flows down to state and utility procurement, and the exact equipment substitutions that pass federal review. Required reading if you take any federal grant dollars or sell into critical infrastructure.

**[Construction Theft Prevention Guide](/guides/construction-theft-prevention)** — FBI puts construction theft at $1B+/year with under 7% recovery. Anchored on a $28M Mid-Atlantic GC that cut theft 73% and saved $340,800 in 12 months. Includes pre-pour, vertical, and finishes-stage threat models.

**[Utility Substation Security Guide](/guides/utility-substation-security)** — NERC CIP-014 framing, threat actor profiles after the December 2022 Moore County attack, and what passes a CIP-014 R5 physical security plan audit.

**[Retail ORC Response Guide](/guides/retail-orc)** — NRF reports 18% YoY shoplifting increase and 67% of retailers see transnational ORC group involvement since 2024. Flash-mob playbook, audio talk-down scripts, and LE coordination.

**[Event Security Playbook](/guides/event-security)** — Festivals, parking lots, vendor zones, and overflow. 3-day to 3-month rental math.

**[Temporary Surveillance Guide](/guides/temporary-surveillance)** — When to rent vs buy. Break-even analysis at 14 months.

**[2026 Mobile Surveillance Pricing Guide](/guides/pricing-guide-2026)** — Sky Guard MSU at $2,200/mo, Swift Deploy at $1,500/mo, Boundary Guard Pro at $1,800/mo. Sourcewell and GSA cooperative pricing included.

**[ROI Calculator](/guides/roi-calculator)** — Enter site value, incident frequency, and insurance deductible. Get a 12-month and 36-month payback projection.

## Who uses these

Security directors at GCs, utility cooperatives, retail loss prevention, municipal procurement officers, event producers, and CFOs running the budget approval. If you're writing a spec or defending a line item, start here.
