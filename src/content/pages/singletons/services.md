Most security vendors sell one piece of the stack. You end up coordinating a hardware integrator, a monitoring center in another country, a VMS license, and a service contractor — four contracts, four invoices, four people pointing at each other when a camera goes dark at 2 a.m.

VDS does all of it. Hardware engineered and assembled in Pikesville, Maryland. A camera-agnostic VMS that integrates with the cameras you already own over ONVIF/RTSP. A US Security Operations Center staffed by people we trained, on payroll, sitting in a room we own. Site survey, install, maintenance, redeployment, and consulting — same team, same accountability.

## The six services

**Rapid Deployment** — Solar-autonomous mobile units on site within 72 hours of approval. No trenching, no permits, no utility coordination. One person stages a trailer in under 20 minutes. Used for active construction sites, lay-down yards, port terminals, utility substations, disaster response, and any location that needs a perimeter live before the power company shows up.

**24/7 Remote Monitoring** — In-house US SOC. AI flags an event, a trained operator verifies it within 60 seconds, talks the subject down over two-way audio, dispatches law enforcement with live video on the way, and writes a full incident report before clocking out. 97% false alarm reduction measured across the deployed fleet.

**Installation & Maintenance** — Licensed field technicians for permanent install, mast-mount, fixed-camera retrofits, and ongoing servicing. Quarterly preventive maintenance, firmware updates, lens cleanings, and redeployment when a site changes phase. We service what we sell and what you already own.

**System Integration** — VDS VMS speaks ONVIF, RTSP, and a published REST API. We integrate with access control (Genetec, Lenel, Avigilon, AMAG), alarm panels, ITSM platforms (ServiceNow, Jira), and ERP systems (SAP, Oracle). If you already have 200 cameras, we add the analytics layer and the monitoring service — no rip-and-replace.

**Managed Security Services** — Turnkey monthly contract: hardware, monitoring, maintenance, reporting, and replacement. One invoice, one SLA, one number to call. Most clients move from a $32,400/month guard contract to a $4,000/month managed service and keep more coverage.

## The case to study before you pick a vendor

A $28M Mid-Atlantic construction project replaced a guard service with VDS Managed Security. Twelve months later: $340,800 saved against the prior security budget, 73% reduction in theft incidents, zero insurance claims filed, monthly cost down from $32,400 to $4,000. Full incident logs, dispatch records, and ROI math available under NDA.

## Why one roof matters

When your perimeter is a single contract, the failure modes shrink. A camera goes offline and our SOC sees it before you do — and dispatches our field tech, not yours. An alarm trips at 3 a.m. and the operator already knows the site layout because our deployment engineer built the camera plan. A new phase opens on the construction project and the same crew redeploys the trailers — they don't bill discovery time.

That's the operating model. Pick the service that fits, or stack them.
