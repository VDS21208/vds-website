## Free excerpt: the four-phase threat model

Construction sites are not single-threat environments. Each phase has a different attacker profile.

**Phase 1 — Pre-pour (excavation, foundation).** Target: copper grounding, fuel, surveyor equipment. Attacker: opportunistic, often local. Median loss: $4,200/incident. Cameras matter less here; lighting and access control matter more.

**Phase 2 — Vertical (structural steel, framing).** Target: power tools, generators, copper pipe. Attacker: more sophisticated — sometimes day labor scouting at night. Median loss: $11,800/incident. This is where mobile surveillance pays back fastest.

**Phase 3 — MEP rough-in.** Target: copper wiring (highest-value phase), HVAC components, switchgear. Attacker: organized, often multi-site. Median loss: $28,400/incident. Audio talk-down at 2 a.m. is the difference between deterrence and a Monday morning insurance call.

**Phase 4 — Finishes.** Target: appliances, fixtures, finish materials, tools. Attacker: mixed. Median loss: $7,600/incident.

Cameras alone don't deter. Cameras with audio talk-down and live monitoring do. Our SOC data: 97% false alarm reduction vs unmonitored systems, and a 60-second median from intrusion detection to verbal interaction with the intruder. That conversation is what ends most events before law enforcement arrives.
