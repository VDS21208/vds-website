## Free excerpt: when rental wins

Rental is the right answer when:

- **Project duration is under 11 months.** Break-even on contract pricing kicks in at month 14. Anything shorter, rent.
- **Site location changes frequently.** Mid-project relocations on a contract still cost; rentals price the move in.
- **You're piloting before committing.** First 90 days as a rental, convert to contract if it works. We credit rental payments toward the first year of contract pricing.
- **It's a single event or seasonal surge.** Festivals, county fairs, Black Friday through holidays.
- **It's emergency response.** Post-storm, post-incident, or temporary deployment while a permanent system is being built.

## Free excerpt: when contract wins

12-month contract is the right answer when:

- **Project or site duration exceeds 14 months.** The math flips.
- **You have multiple simultaneous sites.** Volume tiers start at 5 units and unlock 8-15% reductions.
- **You're funded under a multi-year capital budget.** Annual subscription line is easier to defend than recurring rental procurement.
- **You qualify for cooperative purchasing.** Sourcewell and GSA Schedule contract pricing is annual-structured.

## Free excerpt: the mixed-portfolio strategy

Smart deployments don't pick rental vs contract. They run both. A typical mid-sized GC portfolio:

- **3 to 5 core sites** (large multi-year jobs) on 12-month contracts at standard rate
- **2 to 4 flex units** on 90-day rolling rental, redeployed as projects come online
- **1 to 2 surge units** held in reserve for emergency or short-phase coverage

Total effective cost runs 8-12% above pure contract rate but eliminates idle inventory and stranded contract commitments.
