## Free excerpt: the parking lot is the security perimeter

Most retail loss prevention budgets concentrate on the sales floor. ORC operations stage from the parking lot. Crews park, scout entrances, signal between vehicles, and stage getaway routes 10 to 40 minutes before the inside event. If your camera coverage starts at the door, you've already lost the deterrence window.

Effective deployment covers:
- Vehicle approach lanes (LPR data feeds into investigation later)
- Cart corral and curbside zones (where smaller boost teams congregate)
- Loading dock and rear access (cargo theft staging)
- Adjacent vacant retail (common surveillance staging by professional crews)

## Free excerpt: the flash-mob playbook

Flash-mob retail theft — coordinated groups of 10 to 30 entering a store, grabbing high-value inventory, and exiting in under 90 seconds — became a 2023-2024 phenomenon. The pattern is now well-documented:

**Pre-event signals (5 to 15 min prior):**
- Multiple unfamiliar vehicles arriving in close timing
- Occupants remaining in vehicles, observing entrances
- Communication via phone between vehicles
- Disabling or covering of license plates

**During event:**
- 90 seconds is the typical event window
- Staff intervention is contraindicated (NRF and major retail guidance)
- Audio talk-down from monitored cameras can disrupt the entry timing
- LE dispatch needs vehicle descriptions and direction of travel, not staff witness statements

**Post-event:**
- Evidence chain matters more than apprehension in the moment
- Immutable 12-month video log (VDS standard) supports later ORC prosecutions
- LPR + cellular geofencing data has supported federal ORC indictments

## Free excerpt: a real audio talk-down script

Here's a verbatim SOC script for an unauthorized after-hours rear-dock approach:

> "This is store security monitoring. You are being recorded on camera. The address of this site has been transmitted to the [city] Police Department. Please exit the property immediately. If you are an authorized employee, please present your badge to the camera."

Simple, calm, factual. Compliance rate in our 2024 deployment data: 91% leave within 60 seconds.
