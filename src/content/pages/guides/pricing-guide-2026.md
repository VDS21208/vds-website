## Free excerpt: what's actually included

Most "mobile surveillance" pricing in the market hides the monitoring fee, the data plan, and the maintenance. Our monthly rate includes:

- Hardware (no separate purchase or finance line)
- Solar + battery system, fully serviced
- Cellular data plan (unlimited, on the unit's SIM)
- 24/7 US-based SOC monitoring
- Audio talk-down events
- Law enforcement dispatch coordination
- Cloud VMS (AWS-backed, immutable 12-month audit log)
- Mobile app and web access for all authorized users
- Deployment, relocation, and teardown labor
- Routine maintenance and component replacement

What's not included (transparent add-ons):
- License plate recognition module: $200/mo per unit
- Satellite failover backhaul: $150/mo per unit
- Custom analytics or integrations: quoted per project
