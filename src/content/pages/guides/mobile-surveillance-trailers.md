Most "buyer's guides" in this category are 8-page brochures dressed up as research. This one is not. We wrote it from the install and operations side, from 500+ trailers across 30+ states, including the deployments where we got it wrong the first time.

## What is inside

**Part 1: The category** (pages 3-8)
- Where mobile surveillance trailers actually fit — construction, logistics, events, law enforcement, retail seasonality, vacant property
- Where they do not fit — and what to deploy instead
- A short history of the category: pole cameras, MSU vans, modern solar trailers
- The 6 questions to ask before you start specifying

**Part 2: Hardware** (pages 9-22)
- Solar power: panel sizing for latitude and winter cloud cover, battery chemistry (AGM vs. lithium iron phosphate), depth-of-discharge math
- Mast: telescoping vs. fixed, wind ratings, deployed vs. transit height
- Cameras: image sensor selection, IR vs. white-light illuminator trade-offs, ONVIF compliance, NDAA Section 889 considerations
- Cellular: carrier diversity, antenna selection, signal floor for usable streaming
- Environmental: IP rating, operating temperature, lightning protection
- A pricing reference table — what a real trailer should cost in 2026, broken down by component class

**Part 3: Software** (pages 23-30)
- VMS architecture: on-prem vs. cloud, the operational consequences of each
- AI analytics: what 95%+ accurate license plate recognition actually means, how to read vendor accuracy claims, false-alarm reduction math
- Storage and retention: tier selection by use case and regulatory framework
- Integration: API, webhooks, ITSM/PSIM/BIM hooks
- Security posture: TLS, encryption at rest, RBAC, MFA, audit log retention

**Part 4: Operations** (pages 31-38)
- Monitoring options: self-monitor, third-party SOC, hybrid
- Why a 24/7 SOC matters and what to look for — onshore staffing, average response time, false-alarm reduction rate, audio talk-down capability
- Device health monitoring as a buyer requirement
- Deployment logistics: survey, transport, install, redeploy

**Part 5: The economics** (pages 39-42)
- CapEx vs. OpEx — buy outright, lease, subscription
- 3-year and 5-year TCO templates
- The hidden costs: connectivity, cloud storage, monitoring labor, redeployment, end-of-life disposal
- Sample RFP language you can paste into a procurement document

## Why this guide instead of the others

We sell trailers. The guide does not pretend otherwise. What it does is keep the recommendations vendor-neutral: the battery chemistry math works against any trailer, the AI accuracy questions apply to any VMS, the SOC evaluation rubric applies to any monitoring provider. If a competitor's product is the right call for a use case, the guide will tell you that.

We use this exact framework with prospects in pre-sales engagements. Publishing it costs us a few demos a year but saves the buyers who skip the BS a lot of time.
