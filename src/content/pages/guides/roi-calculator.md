## How this calculator works

This is not a sales calculator that always returns a positive number. It runs your inputs against our actual deployment data — 10,000+ monitored hours across construction, utility, retail, event, and municipal sites — and returns a realistic payback projection. Some site profiles don't pay back inside 36 months. The calculator will tell you when.

## Calculator inputs

**1. Site type** (dropdown)
- Active construction site
- Utility substation or industrial facility
- Retail (single store or multi-store)
- Event venue or temporary site
- Lay-down yard or material storage
- Other / mixed-use

**2. Current annual loss estimate** (dollar input)
- Theft, vandalism, fuel, copper, equipment, inventory
- Include both reported losses and unreported (industry data: 60% of construction losses unreported)

**3. Annual insurance deductible exposure** (dollar input)
- Per-incident deductible × claims filed last 24 months / 2

**4. Number of sites** (numeric input)
- Total simultaneous sites needing coverage

**5. Coverage duration** (slider)
- 3 months / 6 months / 12 months / 24 months / 36 months

**6. Current security spend** (dollar input, optional)
- Guards, existing camera systems, alarm services

## Calculator outputs

**Year 1 net position**
- VDS program cost (units × duration × monthly rate)
- Projected loss reduction (benchmarked to actual deployments by site type)
- Projected insurance premium reduction (typical 10-20% at renewal with documented monitoring)
- Net savings or net cost

**36-month cumulative position**
- Compounded loss avoidance
- Premium reduction stacked across renewals
- Break-even month

**Recommended configuration**
- Suggested unit mix (Mobile Surveillance Unit, Mobile Surveillance Unit, Mobile Surveillance Unit)
- Suggested SOC coverage tier
- or GSA pricing path (if applicable)

## What the calculator does NOT do

- It does not factor reputational damage from theft events
- It does not factor project schedule delay from material theft
- It does not factor employee retention impact of safer sites
- It does not factor litigation exposure from premises liability

These are real costs. They're just hard to model uniformly. Treat the calculator output as the floor of the actual ROI.

The calculator's loss-reduction assumptions are anchored on real deployments. The hero benchmark:

Plug those numbers into the calculator yourself to verify.
