## Free excerpt: the four event windows

Not all event hours carry equal risk. Effective coverage matches the threat curve.

**Window 1 — Load-in (T-72 to T-12 hours).** Vendor equipment, talent gear, AV. Mostly insider/contractor risk. Cameras at loading docks and vendor row entrances.

**Window 2 — Event hours.** Crowd density, pickpocketing, ticket fraud, parking lot theft. Highest visibility need. SOC monitoring active across all zones.

**Window 3 — Dark hours (overnight between event days).** This is the highest per-hour loss window. Vendor inventory left on-site, talent equipment, festival infrastructure. Audio talk-down on rear vendor row stops 90% of overnight intrusions.

**Window 4 — Teardown (T+12 to T+48 hours).** Mirrored to load-in. Cameras stay live until last vendor exits.

## Free excerpt: rental math

Event coverage doesn't fit a monthly subscription cleanly. The guide breaks down four rental profiles:

- **3-day weekend festival:** Sky Guard MSU rental ~$1,800 per unit, includes deployment, monitoring, and teardown.
- **2-week county fair:** ~$3,200 per unit, full SOC coverage all 14 days.
- **6-week summer concert series:** ~$5,400 per unit, weekend-active SOC.
- **3-month seasonal venue:** $2,200/mo standard rate kicks in, lower effective daily cost.

Break-even between rental and full annual subscription: 14 months of continuous deployment. See [Temporary Surveillance Guide](/guides/temporary-surveillance) for the full math.

## Free excerpt: vendor zone theft is mostly preventable

Vendor zone theft — inventory walked off after midnight by people in branded vendor shirts — accounts for the majority of festival losses we see. Two changes cut it dramatically:

1. **Single brightly-lit vehicle exit lane** with LPR. Records every plate leaving the vendor area.
2. **Mobile camera with audio talk-down at vendor row center.** SOC operator addresses unauthorized loading by name where possible.

Festivals that adopted both reported vendor shrink dropping by 60-80% in our 2023-2024 deployment data.
