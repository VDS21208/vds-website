## What it does

Built for live video monitoring and remote guarding. AI pre-filters eliminate 97% of false alarms before they hit your operators.

## Key capabilities

### AI pre-filter
97% false-alarm reduction. Operators see only what matters.

### Audio talk-down
One-click PA broadcast through trailer speakers.

### Direct LE dispatch
Live video sent to responding officers with site address & gate codes.

### Operator queue
Round-robin assignment. SLA tracking. Escalation paths.

### Auto-disposition
AI-assisted incident reporting. Pre-filled forms. Audit logs.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Alarm Monitoring Portal. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.
