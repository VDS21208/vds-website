## What it does

Capture months of activity in minutes. Built for construction documentation, vegetation monitoring, and progress tracking.

## Key capabilities

### Months in minutes
Compress 30/60/90 days of footage into a sharable video.

### Construction documentation
Auto-stamped daily progress for stakeholders and insurance.

### Vegetation & terrain
Track changes over seasons for solar/wind farm sites.

### Auto-export
Scheduled MP4 generation. Email delivery to stakeholders.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Long-Term Time Lapse. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.

## Compliance & security

- AWS US-only · TLS 1.3 · AES-256
- NDAA Section 889 compliant
- SOC 2 documented
- GDPR / CCPA / PIPEDA aware
- Configurable retention
