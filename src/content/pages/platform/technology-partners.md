## What it does

AI VisionStream works with 30+ camera brands and integrates with the industry's leading VMS, alarm, and access platforms.

## Key capabilities

### Camera partners
Axis, Hanwha, Bosch, Pelco, Avigilon, UNV, Vivotek, Tiandy, TVT, Sunell.

### VMS integrations
Genetec Security Center, Milestone XProtect, Avigilon Control Center.

### Alarm & dispatch
Immix, Patriot, Bold, Sureview, Stages.

### Cloud & AI
AWS, NVIDIA, Intel, AWS Partner Network. SOC 2.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Technology Partners. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.

## Compliance & security

- AWS US-only · TLS 1.3 · AES-256
- NDAA Section 889 compliant
- SOC 2 documented
- GDPR / CCPA / PIPEDA aware
- Configurable retention
