## What it does

Automate billing for sub-accounts. Tier pricing, prorated charges, revenue reporting — all white-label ready.

## Key capabilities

### Usage-based billing
Per-camera, per-analytic, per-month. Automatic proration.

### Revenue reporting
Real-time MRR/ARR by sub-account, tier, geography.

### White-label ready
Your brand on every invoice. Stripe & ACH integrations.

### Channel margins
Configure dealer tiers and commissions automatically.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Billing Module. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.
