## What it does

Embed live camera feeds on any website. Perfect for civic engagement, traffic dashboards, and event streaming.

## Key capabilities

### Embeddable iframe
Drop into any CMS. Customizable bezel and branding.

### Civic engagement
Public-facing traffic, weather, beach, downtown cameras.

### Event streaming
Temporary public feeds for races, festivals, concerts.

### Access controls
IP allowlist, time-bounded, region-locked options.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Streaming Widget. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.
