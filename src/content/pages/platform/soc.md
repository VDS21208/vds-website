## What happens in the 60 seconds after an alert

1. **T+0s** — Camera detects an event. Model classifies it. Rule fires.
2. **T+3s** — Alert lands in the SOC console with live video and rule context.
3. **T+15s** — Operator opens the camera feed, reviews 10 seconds of pre-event buffer.
4. **T+30s** — Operator either dismisses (animal, debris) or escalates.
5. **T+45s** — On escalation: operator activates live audio talk-down through the trailer PA, sends mobile push to your team, opens a dispatch line to local LE if pre-authorized.
6. **T+60s** — Customer is on the alert. LE has been notified with live video link if applicable. Incident report has been started.

## Audio talk-down is a real deterrent

A live operator saying "I can see you in the blue jacket near the white Ford. Police have been notified. Leave the property now" stops 8 out of 10 attempted incidents before LE arrives. Recorded loops do not. Verified humans do.

## Direct law enforcement coordination

Where allowed, our operators have direct dial lines to dispatch in the jurisdiction. We send a CAD-compatible incident summary plus a live video link. LE arrives knowing what they are walking into.

## Every event has a report

After resolution, the customer receives a full incident report:

- Timestamps for detection, escalation, and resolution
- Operator actions taken
- Audio transcript if talk-down was used
- LE case number if dispatched
- Linked video clips
- Recommendations for rule tuning

Reports live in the platform and export to PDF, CSV, or your SIEM.
