## What it does

Connect older NVR/DVR fleets to AI VisionStream without ripping out hardware. PoE-powered. Plug and play.

## Key capabilities

### Legacy bridge
Connect existing NVRs, DVRs, analog encoders to the cloud.

### Plug and play
Outbound-only secure tunnel. No firewall changes.

### PoE-powered
Single Cat6 cable. Drop in, click to claim.

### Hybrid setup
Keep on-prem recording. Cloud adds AI + multi-site dashboard.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including VDS Gateway. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.

## Compliance & security

- AWS US-only · TLS 1.3 · AES-256
- NDAA Section 889 compliant
- SOC 2 documented
- GDPR / CCPA / PIPEDA aware
- Configurable retention
