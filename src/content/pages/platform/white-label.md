## What it does

Resell AI VisionStream under your own brand. Custom domains, your logo, your colors, your invoices — built-in.

## Key capabilities

### Full brand swap
Logo, colors, custom domain, branded invoices, your support phone.

### Native mobile apps
iOS + Android apps in your brand on the App Store / Play Store.

### Reseller margins
Tiered wholesale pricing. Set your own customer rates.

### Marketing kit
Co-branded collateral, demo accounts, sales training.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including White Label & Co-branding. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.
