## What it does

Multi-site live view, playback, search — every camera in one tab.

## Key capabilities

### Multi-site live grid
Watch all sites in one tab. Custom tile layouts. Click any tile for full-screen.

### Instant clip search
Filter by event type, time, camera, AI tag. Export clips in under 5 seconds.

### AI event timeline
Auto-tagged events on a scrubbable timeline. Jump to any moment by event type.

### Forensic search
Color, vehicle type, person count. Search hours of footage in seconds.


## How it fits with VDS

VDS deploys **solar-autonomous mobile surveillance trailers** that come pre-integrated with AI VisionStream — including Video Portal. The same feature works on your existing IP cameras (any ONVIF/RTSP, no NDAA-prohibited brands) and across drones, body cams, and IoT devices.
