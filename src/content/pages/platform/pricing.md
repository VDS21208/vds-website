## Three tiers, one platform

All tiers include camera-agnostic ingest, the mobile app, RBAC and MFA, the 12-month audit log, US-only AWS storage, and every integration listed on the [integrations page](/platform/integrations).

### Essential — $39 per camera per month
- 30-day cloud retention
- Up to 8 analytics rules per camera
- Mobile app for unlimited users
- Email and SMS alerts
- Standard integrations (Slack, Teams, PagerDuty, email)
- Business-hours support

### Professional — $69 per camera per month
- 60-day cloud retention
- Unlimited analytics rules per camera
- License plate recognition included
- All ACS integrations (Lenel, Genetec, Brivo)
- ITSM integrations (ServiceNow, Jira)
- 24/7 platform support (not SOC monitoring)
- Quarterly rule tuning review

### Enterprise — Custom
- 90-day cloud retention (extendable)
- Unlimited rules, sites, and users
- Customer-managed KMS keys
- SSO via SAML 2.0 / OIDC
- Custom integrations and webhooks
- Dedicated customer success engineer
- Annual on-site review

## SOC monitoring add-on

24/7 US Security Operations Center — verified operator response on every Tier 3 alert, audio talk-down, direct LE dispatch with live video, full incident report per event.

- **Per site:** $399/site/month for up to 8 cameras
- **Per camera (over 8):** $29/camera/month
- **Includes:** unlimited verified events, monthly performance report, quarterly rule tuning

See the [SOC page](/platform/soc) for the operating model.

## Hardware

The platform runs on any compatible ONVIF/RTSP camera. If you need hardware too — solar-autonomous mobile trailers, fixed-site cameras, network infrastructure — see [hardware pricing](/pricing).
