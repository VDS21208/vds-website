## The auto dealership catalytic converter theft problem in 2026

State Farm catalytic converter claims peaked at 45,000 in 2022 and stayed elevated through 2024. Federal legislation (PART Act) and several state marking laws cooled the wholesale market briefly, but rhodium prices have recovered through 2025 and 2026, and dealer-lot theft has rebounded alongside.

The economics for the thief:
- **Rhodium** at $10,400/oz, palladium around $1,000/oz, platinum around $950/oz
- **Recycler payout** per converter: $50 (low-grade domestic) up to $1,200+ (high-end European, hybrid)
- **Time on target**: 60-90 seconds with a battery-powered reciprocating saw and a portable jack
- **Targets**: Toyota Prius, Ford F-Series, Honda Element, Lexus RX, any hybrid

A dealer lot offers a buffet — 50-300 vehicles in line-of-sight, often no overnight staff, low fence, and predictable lighting. Crews work 2-3 cuts per visit, in and out in 5 minutes.

Traditional dealer security (perimeter chain-link + dome cameras + alarm) records the theft. The customer's vehicle is on the lift Monday morning for an $1,800-$3,400 repair the dealer eats or fights with the insurer over.

## How VDS prevents catalytic converter theft at dealer lots

The defense recognizes the 90-second window:

**Thermal at the perimeter.** Swift Deploy with dual-spectrum night vision detects a human shape on the lot at 2am before the saw comes out of the bag. Thermal works through humidity, light fog, and dealer lot light pollution — standard CCTV does not.

**LPR at every gate.** 95%+ accuracy in under 3 seconds. Every plate in, every plate out, with hot-list lookups. Many theft crews are repeat offenders working a regional route.

**SOC audio talk-down in under 60 seconds.** This is the only intervention that fits inside the 90-second theft window. The SOC operator verifies the intrusion, hits the speaker, dispatches LE with live video. The crew bails before the first cut.

**AI VisionStream behavior classification.** A person walking between vehicles at 2am triggers a verification. A person crouching next to a vehicle triggers an immediate talk-down. The system understands posture and dwell.
