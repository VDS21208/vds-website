## The construction equipment theft problem in 2026

Stolen equipment moves fast. A CAT 299D3 skid steer pulled at 2am Saturday is on a trailer to a chop yard 200 miles away by sunrise. By Monday it is repainted, the VIN plate is gone, and the data link module is wiped. National Equipment Register data: average recovery time on heavy equipment is 11 months when recovered at all, and the recovery rate sits below 7%.

What gets taken:
- **Compact track loaders and mini excavators** — $45,000-$85,000 each, towable behind a 3/4-ton truck
- **Tier 4 generators (20-60 kW)** — $18,000-$40,000, ready to plug in at another site
- **Plate compactors, walk-behind saws, laser levels** — $2,000-$8,000 each, untraceable, cash sale
- **Copper, wire, plumbing rough-in** — covered in detail at [/solutions/construction/copper-theft](/solutions/construction/copper-theft)
- **Fuel** — 200-gallon diesel pulls overnight, $700+ per incident

The thieves rarely climb the fence. They drive through the gate, often after cutting the chain or following the last subcontractor out. **Every theft begins with a license plate at the entry.**

## How VDS prevents equipment theft at construction sites

The defense is layered around the equipment-staging area and the site entry:

**At the equipment yard:** Swift Deploy unit with dual-spectrum night vision and AI VisionStream object classification. The system distinguishes a parked excavator from a moving one. The moment a unit moves after curfew hours, the SOC has live eyes within 8 seconds.

**At the site entry:** LPR camera on every vehicle in and out. 95%+ accuracy, capture in under 3 seconds, plate logged with timestamp and a hot-list lookup. Stolen vehicles, prior unauthorized entries, and out-of-pattern after-hours traffic trigger SOC review immediately.

**At the SOC:** US-based operators, audio talk-down, and direct LE dispatch with live video stream. Average from detection to talk-down: under 60 seconds.
