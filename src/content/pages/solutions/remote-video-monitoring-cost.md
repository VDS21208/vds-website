Remote video monitoring is a managed service where trained operators watch your camera feeds, verify alarms, talk down intruders over speaker, and dispatch police. The cost varies by pricing model, coverage hours, and how the service handles false alarms.

This page breaks down the three pricing models you'll see in quotes, what each one buys, and how the numbers compare to guards.

## The three pricing models

**Per-site flat rate** — A fixed monthly fee per location, regardless of camera count or alert volume. VDS uses this model: $399/site/mo for 24/7 coverage.

**Per-camera** — $20-$40 per camera per month is typical for unmonitored cloud VMS. Add $5-$15 per camera per month for analytics-driven monitoring. A 6-camera site runs $150-$330/mo on this model.

**Per-hour-of-coverage** — $8-$20 per hour for active human monitoring during defined windows (e.g., 6pm-6am only). A 12-hour overnight window runs $2,880-$7,200/mo. Useful for daytime-staffed sites that only need after-hours coverage.

## What "monitoring" actually means

Cheaper monitoring services watch cameras passively or only respond to motion alerts. VDS SOC operators do four things:

1. **Verify** — operators visually confirm the alert is a real intruder, not a deer or shadow.
2. **Voice-down** — live audio warning over the trailer's onboard speaker. Roughly 80% of intrusions end here.
3. **Dispatch** — direct call to local PD with verified visual evidence (police respond faster to verified events).
4. **Document** — full incident video clipped, timestamped, and delivered to the site manager within 15 minutes.

If you're quoted $99/mo for "remote monitoring," ask which of those four they actually do. Often it's #1 only — alert verification with no voice-down or dispatch authority.

## Hidden costs

- Per-incident charges (some vendors charge $25-$75 per dispatched event)
- False-alarm penalties (police-imposed fines for unverified alarms — $50-$300 per fine in many jurisdictions)
- Camera-count overages
- After-hours upgrade fees for "24/7 plans" that are real
