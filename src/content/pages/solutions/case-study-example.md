## Featured: $28M Mid-Atlantic General Contractor

**Vertical:** Construction (commercial mixed-use)
**Duration:** 14 months
**Configuration:** 3x Sky Guard MSU + 24/7 SOC monitoring
**Replaced:** Overnight guard contract ($32,400/mo)

**Outcomes (12 months post-deployment):**
- Security spend: $32,400/mo → $4,000/mo (**88% reduction**)
- Total savings: **$340,800** in year one
- Theft incidents: **73% reduction** vs prior 12 months
- Insurance claims filed: **$0** (down from $47K in prior year)
- Police dispatches: 11 verified events, 100% with usable video evidence
- Project completion: on schedule

This is the case study we cite most often because it spans the full stack — hardware, platform, SOC — and the customer's risk team independently verified the numbers for their insurer.

## Methodology

We publish outcomes using a consistent framework:

1. **Baseline** — 12 months of pre-deployment incident, claim, and cost data, sourced from the customer.
2. **Deployment** — configuration, timeline, and integration steps documented.
3. **Measurement period** — 12 months minimum post-deployment.
4. **Verification** — customer risk team or finance team signs off on the numbers before publication.

We do not publish unverified claims. If a customer can't or won't validate the outcome, the case study doesn't ship.
