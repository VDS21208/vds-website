## What 24/7 Guard Coverage Actually Costs

<p>The sticker price for security guard services doesn't tell the whole story. To staff a single post 24/7, you need 3–4 guards to cover shifts, breaks, sick days, and vacation coverage. At $28/hour, that's $18,144/month minimum before overtime, holidays, and supervisor costs.</p>

<p>Loaded costs make the real number much higher. Security agencies mark up rates 25–75% to cover their overhead and profit margins. Annual turnover exceeds 100% in most markets, creating constant recruitment and training costs. Workers' compensation insurance adds 30% or more to hourly rates. Holiday and weekend premiums increase costs another 10–25%.</p>

<p>The true cost of 24/7 guard coverage runs $18,000–$36,000/month for a single post. Enterprise clients protecting multiple locations often spend $500,000–$2 million annually on guard services alone.</p>

## Where Guards Still Win: An Honest Assessment

<p>Guards are genuinely necessary for physical ID verification at access points, situations requiring physical intervention, and regulatory requirements specifying on-site personnel. Federal facilities, airports, and high-security manufacturing often mandate human presence for compliance reasons.</p>

<p>For everything else — theft deterrence, perimeter monitoring, incident documentation, and after-hours coverage — AI-powered remote monitoring delivers superior results at a fraction of the cost. Video evidence holds up in court. AI doesn't sleep, take breaks, or miss details. SOC operators maintain consistent alertness across all shifts.</p>

<p>The decision matrix is straightforward: use guards where human judgment and physical presence are irreplaceable. Use remote monitoring everywhere else.</p>

## Guards vs. Cameras FAQ



### Is remote video monitoring as effective as security guards?

<p>Remote video monitoring provides superior incident documentation, consistent alertness, and faster verified response times. Guards excel at physical intervention and access control, while VDS excels at detection, deterrence, and evidence collection. The most effective approach combines both technologies strategically.</p>



### How much do security guards cost per month?

<p>24/7 guard coverage costs $18,000–$36,000 per month for a single post, including wages, agency markup, benefits, turnover costs, and overtime. Enterprise clients often spend $500,000–$2 million annually on guard services across multiple locations.</p>



### Can cameras replace security guards?

<p>Cameras can replace guards for perimeter monitoring, theft deterrence, and incident documentation. Guards remain necessary for physical access control, situations requiring intervention, and regulatory compliance. Most clients achieve optimal results with hybrid deployments that reduce guard costs 50–75%.</p>



### What is the response time for remote video monitoring?

<p>VDS provides verified response within 60 seconds of detection. AI VisionStream filters 97% of false triggers before alerts reach SOC operators, ensuring human attention focuses on genuine threats. SOC operators can issue audio challenges and coordinate police response with live video evidence.</p>



### What types of properties are best suited for remote monitoring?

<p>Construction sites, retail parking lots, warehouses, auto dealerships, and outdoor storage facilities benefit most from remote monitoring. Properties with large perimeters, after-hours risk, and limited existing infrastructure see the greatest cost savings and coverage improvements with VDS solutions.</p>
