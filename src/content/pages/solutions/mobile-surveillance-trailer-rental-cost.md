Most VDS trailers ship as rentals. The reason is straightforward: construction sites, event security, retail seasonal needs, and disaster response all have variable durations and locations. Owning a trailer that sits idle 4 months a year is worse economics than renting one for the 8 months you need it.

This page covers rental rates by term length, what's included, deposit and lead time, and how redeployment works.

## Rental rates by term

| Term | Swift Deploy | Sky Guard MSU | Boundary Guard Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month-to-month (1-2 mo) | $1,800/mo | $2,600/mo | $2,200/mo |
| Short-term (3-11 mo) | $1,650/mo | $2,400/mo | $2,000/mo |
| Standard (12 mo) | $1,500/mo | $2,200/mo | $1,800/mo |
| Long-term (24+ mo) | $1,350/mo | $1,980/mo | $1,620/mo |

Quarterly billing is available at the 12-month rate. Monthly billing is standard. There's no penalty for ending a rental at the contracted term — extensions or early returns require 30-day notice.

## Per-week and per-quarter math

For short jobs (event security, disaster response, 60-day projects):
- **Per week:** Sky Guard MSU at month-to-month = $2,600/mo ÷ 4.3 weeks = **~$605/week**
- **Per quarter:** Standard 12-month rate prepaid = $2,200 x 3 = **$6,600/quarter**

We don't list a true "per-week" rate because deployment costs make sub-2-week rentals uneconomical for both parties. Minimum rental is 14 days for emergency response, 30 days standard.

## Lead time

| Configuration | Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Standard SKU (Sky Guard, Swift Deploy, Boundary Guard) | 14 days from PO |

| Custom configuration | 30-45 days |

Standard 14-day lead time is door-to-door from PO to trailer powered on at your site. We keep buffer inventory in regional yards across 12 states for faster turnaround.

## Redeployment between sites

Most of our customers move trailers between sites during a rental. Redeployment fees:
- Same metro area (under 50 miles): $500
- Same state: $800
- Adjacent state: $1,200

There's no per-redeployment charge if you give us 7-day notice and a confirmed new site address. Same-day moves incur a $300 expedite fee on top of the redeployment cost.

## When rental beats purchase

Rent when:
- Site duration is under 24 months
- You need to redeploy across multiple locations
- Capex is unavailable or constrained
- You want predictable opex with bundled service

Buy when:
- Site duration is 36+ months at a fixed location
- You have capex available and prefer ownership
- Your asset depreciation policy favors capitalization

Full break-even math is on [Buy vs Rent](/solutions/buy-vs-rent-mobile-surveillance-trailer). At Sky Guard rates, break-even hits around month 24.
