## The construction insurance problem in 2026

Builder's risk rates climbed 14% on average in 2024 and another 7-9% in 2025 across most US regions, driven by elevated theft frequency, wildfire exposure, and reinsurance pressure. Underwriters are no longer accepting "we have cameras on site" as a meaningful theft control. They want:

- **Monitored detection** — not just recording. Recording is post-loss documentation. Monitored detection is prevention.
- **Documented response times** — average time from detection to verified intervention.
- **Video evidence retention** — minimum 30 days, preferably 90.
- **Loss-frequency data** — claim-free months, incident counts, intervention outcomes.
- **NDAA-compliant hardware** — increasingly a binary requirement on federal and many state contracts.

General contractors who can produce this package at renewal are credited 8-15% in premium reductions on the builder's risk policy. On a $28M project with a 0.6% rate, that is $13,400 to $25,200 in annual premium savings — before considering the loss-ratio improvement in subsequent renewals.

You hand this to your broker at renewal. They hand it to the underwriter. The underwriter credits the rate.
