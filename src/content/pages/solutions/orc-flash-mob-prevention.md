## The retail ORC flash mob problem in 2026

The flash mob pattern stabilized as the dominant ORC modality through 2024-2025: a crew of 6-20 enters in a 30-second window, targets specific merchandise (Apple devices, designer handbags, luxury skincare, OTC pharma), bags it, and exits to 2-3 staged getaway vehicles in the parking lot. Total time on target: 2 to 4 minutes. Total loss per event: $15,000 to $400,000.

What law enforcement and prosecutors need to make these cases:
- **High-quality interior video** — most retailers have this
- **Getaway vehicle plates** — most retailers do not have this, or have low-resolution motion-blurred captures from a soffit camera 80ft away
- **Direction of travel and time-of-exit correlation** — feeds into regional ORC task force pattern matching
- **Chain-of-custody evidence export** — prosecutorially admissible, with metadata

VDS produces the second, third, and fourth bullets. That is where most retail security stops short and where most prosecutions die.

## How VDS prevents and documents ORC flash mobs

The defense recognizes that you will not stop a 12-person flash mob in motion through audio talk-down alone. The win is at evidence and recovery:

**Swift Deploy in the parking lot.** Positioned for full coverage of staged getaway vehicles, with line-of-sight on the storefront. Thermal sees people loading bags into trunks; AI VisionStream classifies the loading behavior pattern.

**LPR captures every plate.** 95%+ accuracy under 3 seconds. Multiple plates per vehicle (front and rear) where geometry allows. State, partial-plate confidence, vehicle make/color metadata.

**24/7 SOC response.** During an active event the SOC operator pushes a live video feed to the local PD dispatch, gives real-time direction-of-travel updates, and stays on the line through the response. We do not engage flash mob crews with audio talk-down during the event — operator safety and de-escalation protocol takes precedence.

**Evidence export pack.** Within 24 hours of the event: chain-of-custody video, LPR captures with confidence scores, SOC narrative timeline, exported for the AG's office or regional ORC task force.
