## The dealer lot vehicle theft problem in 2026

The thief profile has shifted from amateur joyrider to organized export ring. Late-model SUVs (Tahoe, Suburban, Escalade, Wagoneer), pickup trucks (F-250/350, Silverado HD, RAM HD), and high-value imports (Range Rover, GLE, X5) are pulled from dealer lots and storage yards by crews running professional equipment:

- **Key-cloning hardware** — relay attack tools that grab the FOB signal through the showroom window
- **Flatbed/rollback tow trucks** — drive in, load, drive out in under 4 minutes per vehicle
- **CAN-bus injection** — bypass the immobilizer entirely on certain Stellantis and Hyundai platforms
- **Inside information** — keys-in-vehicle policies, predictable shift changes, known camera blind spots

A successful lot pull moves the vehicle to a containerization yard within hours and onto a vessel within days. Recovery rate on export-bound vehicles drops well below 20%.

## How VDS prevents vehicle theft at dealer lots and storage yards

The defense layers detection, identification, and intervention:

**Sky Guard MSU at lot center.** Thermal LWIR sees a person or vehicle moving across the lot at 1,200ft. Radar correlates. 4K PTZ tracks. The SOC operator sees a flatbed pulling up to a row of trucks at 1:47am and intervenes before the load straps are out.

**Solar Camera Poles at the perimeter.** Continuous overlapping coverage along the fence line and rear access points. Battery + solar = no trenching, no power drop, relocates with the lot.

**LPR at every gate.** 95%+ accuracy under 3 seconds. The thieves' truck plate is captured on entry. The plate logs into AI VisionStream with a hot-list lookup — stolen vehicles, prior unauthorized entries, and out-of-pattern after-hours visitors flag instantly.

**SOC audio talk-down + LE dispatch.** Under 60 seconds from detection to verified intervention. Live video stream to dispatch on every escalation.
