## Mobile surveillance in Florida

Florida is a deployment environment with two seasons: build season and storm season. Our units are rated for sustained 80mph winds and we have a documented hurricane response protocol — when a named storm enters the 5-day cone, the SOC works with you to either secure units on site (anchored tie-downs, deployment trailer secured) or relocate them inland to pre-staged yards.

We've executed this protocol four times since 2022. Zero unit losses.
