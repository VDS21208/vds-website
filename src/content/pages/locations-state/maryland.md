## Industries we protect in Maryland

**Construction.** The Baltimore-DC corridor runs hot — Port Covington redevelopment, Howard County data centers, Frederick warehouse builds. We have 90+ units active on Maryland construction sites today, ranging from single-tower jobsites to 12-unit perimeters around 200-acre lay-down yards.

**Utilities.** BGE substations, Pepco service yards, and water treatment facilities across the state. Our Mobile Surveillance Unit fixed-mount cameras handle critical infrastructure where solar-autonomous trailers don't fit the threat model.

**Port of Baltimore and logistics.** Container yards, intermodal facilities, and the warehouses along I-95 between Baltimore and Aberdeen. Cargo theft is a measurable problem and SOC-monitored escalation reduces it.
