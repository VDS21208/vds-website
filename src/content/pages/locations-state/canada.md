## Where VDS stands in Canada today

VDS does not currently deploy units in Canada. We operate 500+ mobile surveillance units across 30+ US states, with a 24/7 SOC in the United States and AWS US-region data residency. A Canadian launch is planned for a future phase, contingent on AWS Canada Central data residency, French-language SOC capacity, and provincial compliance work (PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA).

We'd rather tell you this up front than pretend we can serve you well today and learn the hard way.

## If you're a Canadian buyer right now

These are the operators we'd point you to. Each runs Canadian infrastructure, employs Canadian staff, and handles Canadian compliance natively.

- **Zedcor** — The largest Canadian mobile surveillance operator. Calgary HQ, publicly traded. Strong track record in oil and gas, construction, and remote sites. Coverage across western Canada with eastward expansion.
- **A&B Mobile Security** — Canadian rent-or-purchase mobile surveillance operator. Flexible commercial terms for shorter deployments.
- **SureSite Security** — Canadian remote video monitoring service. Useful if you have cameras already and need eyes-on rather than full hardware.
- **Spotter Security** — Toronto-based mobile and integrated security. Good fit for GTA construction and commercial sites.
- **LotCop** — Specialist in parking-lot mobile surveillance across Canada. Narrower scope, but they know the lot-monitoring problem well.

We're listing competitors on our own page because honest referrals beat a bad fit. If one of these is right for you, take it.

## If you run a US/Canada cross-border program

This is where we can help today. If your portfolio spans both countries — a US retailer with stores in Ontario, a North American construction GC, a utility with assets on both sides of the border — VDS can quote and operate your US footprint while we coordinate Canadian coverage with a vetted Canadian partner.

You get one point of contact, consistent reporting on the US side, and a defensible compliance posture on the Canadian side. Talk to us before you split the contract two ways.

## Compliance and infrastructure work still required

Before VDS deploys a Canadian unit, we need:

- AWS Canada Central data residency configured separately from our US environment
- PIPEDA-aligned data handling, plus provincial regimes (Quebec Law 25 in particular)
- French-language SOC operator coverage for Quebec deployments
- NDAA Section 889 is a US federal standard; the Canadian equivalent for federal procurement is the Policy on Government Security and related supply-chain integrity reviews. We'll address this before any Crown procurement work.

This is engineering and operations work, not marketing copy. We won't claim Canadian operations until that work is finished.
