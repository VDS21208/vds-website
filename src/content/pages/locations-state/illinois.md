## Mobile surveillance in Illinois

Illinois has one of the country's most-publicized organized retail crime environments and an active commercial and infrastructure construction pipeline. Both drive our Chicago metro deployments. ComEd substation work and CTA-adjacent infrastructure projects add a utility and transit layer.

We pre-stage inventory near Chicago O'Hare for metro coverage and use Indianapolis secondary inventory for far-southern Illinois work.

## Industries we protect in Illinois

**Retail and ORC response.** National retailers operating in Chicago metro asked us to deploy around distribution centers, high-loss store parking lots, and last-mile delivery hubs. SOC-monitored voice-down deterrent moves PD response priority on real incidents.

**Construction.** North and west suburban multifamily, downtown commercial, and industrial builds along I-80/I-88. Standard perimeter deployments with copper and equipment theft as the steady-state driver.

**Utilities and transit.** ComEd substations and transmission work, plus CTA-adjacent infrastructure projects where the construction zone needs after-hours monitoring. NDAA 889 compliance matters here for federally-funded transit work.

## Coverage and lead time

- **Chicago metro, north and west suburbs:** 5-7 business days
- **Rockford, Peoria, Champaign-Urbana:** 5-7 business days
- **Southern Illinois (Carbondale, Cairo):** 7-10 business days

O'Hare pre-stage covers most of the metro in 24 hours from yard release.

## An Illinois-area deployment

A national retailer's Chicago metro distribution center was logging an average of nine after-hours intrusion attempts per month, with three escalating to successful entry and goods loss. Two Sky Guard MSUs and one Boundary Guard Pro fixed deployment went in over 6 business days. Eight-month coverage: 71 deterred entries (SOC voice-down triggered), four arrests via SOC-dispatched Cook County Sheriff, zero successful intrusions after week three. Retailer extended to two additional Midwest DCs.

## Illinois compliance and procurement

Illinois has specific privacy considerations for ALPR and biometric data under BIPA (Biometric Information Privacy Act). Our standard deployments do not use facial recognition or biometric identification — analytics are object and behavior based. We document this in the Illinois MSA.

We hold Sourcewell and Omnia Partners cooperative contracts, both widely accepted by Illinois counties, municipalities, and school systems. NDAA Section 889 compliant for federally-funded work, including CTA federal funding.
