## Mobile surveillance in New Jersey

New Jersey is consistently among the top three US states for cargo theft by incident count. Port Newark-Elizabeth is the largest container port on the East Coast and the surrounding warehouse and trucking ecosystem operates 24/7. We have 40+ active units in NJ, concentrated around the port, the Meadowlands, and the I-78 and I-95 logistics corridors.

Pikesville is 180 miles from Newark. Same-week deployment is standard, 24-48 hours for fleet redeploy.

## Industries we protect in New Jersey

**Port and cargo logistics.** Port Newark-Elizabeth-adjacent yards, container drayage operations, and the warehouse cluster along I-78 between Newark and Allentown. Cargo theft is the primary driver — both organized crews and opportunistic incidents. SOC-dispatched response into the Port Authority and Newark PD is documented.

**Construction.** North Jersey commercial and multifamily, Hudson County waterfront redevelopment, and the active industrial construction along I-287. Copper, equipment, and tool theft are the steady-state drivers.

**Utilities.** PSEG substations and transmission projects across the state. We hold the standard vendor qualifications and have multiple active deployments on PSEG-adjacent work.

## A New Jersey-area deployment

A drayage operator running a 22-acre container staging yard adjacent to Port Newark was losing roughly $24,000/month to cargo theft across an average of 11 incidents — primarily after-hours entries cutting trailer seals and pulling specific high-value containers. Three Sky Guard MSUs and one Boundary Guard Pro fixed deployment went in over 4 business days (NJ priority lane). Seven-month coverage: 53 deterred entries, five arrests via SOC-dispatched Port Authority PD, an 89% reduction in successful theft incidents. Customer extended to a Newark-area sister yard.
