## Where VDS stands in Toronto

No VDS units operate in the Greater Toronto Area today. We run 500+ mobile surveillance units across 30+ US states. A Canadian launch — Toronto included — is planned for a future phase. We're not committing to a quarter or year until the back-end work is done (AWS Canada Central, PIPEDA-aligned operations, SOC coverage for Eastern Canada).

## What Toronto needs

The GTA stresses mobile surveillance differently than most North American markets:

- **Construction** — Toronto leads Canada in active construction starts. Highrise sites, infrastructure projects, and long-duration condo builds all need persistent perimeter surveillance with minimal grid dependency.
- **Ports and logistics** — The Port of Toronto and the broader logistics corridor along the 401 generate demand for yard surveillance, cargo monitoring, and after-hours coverage.
- **Financial district and commercial real estate** — Vacant or transitioning commercial properties downtown need monitored deterrence between leases and during renovations.
- **Tech and film** — Production sets, equipment yards, and temporary event venues need rapid-deploy units that don't require trenching or permanent power.

These are exactly the use cases VDS handles in US markets. We're not yet positioned to handle them in Toronto.

## For Toronto buyers today

**Spotter Security** is the local recommendation. Toronto-based, runs mobile and integrated security across the GTA, and knows the local jobsite and commercial property market. For broader Ontario or Western Canada coverage, **Zedcor** is the largest Canadian operator. For monitoring-only (you have cameras, you need eyes), **SureSite** is a fit. For parking-specific work, **LotCop**.

## For cross-border programs

This is where VDS contributes today. If you run a portfolio that spans Buffalo and Toronto, Detroit and Windsor, or any US/Canada mix, we can quote the US sites under our standard pricing (Sky Guard MSU $2,200/mo, Boundary Guard Pro $1,800/mo, Swift Deploy $1,500/mo) and coordinate Toronto coverage through a Canadian partner.

One vendor relationship. Consistent reporting on the US side. Defensible compliance on the Canadian side. Worth a conversation if you'd otherwise sign two separate contracts.
