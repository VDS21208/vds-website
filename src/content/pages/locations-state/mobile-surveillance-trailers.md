## What changes city to city

Three things vary by metro and matter when you're choosing a vendor:

**Lead time.** Same-day in Baltimore. Next-day in Philadelphia, DC, and Richmond. 2–4 days for most other listed metros depending on unit availability and staging hub.

**Local compliance.** New York POST Act, Illinois BIPA, California CCPA + SB 1186, DC federal procurement rules, and city-specific permit requirements all shape how a deployment is configured. We default to the strictest applicable rule and document it in writing.

**Industry mix.** Construction is the largest category in every city. Beyond that, the mix shifts: ports in Baltimore, LA, and Miami. Federal in DC. Refineries in Philadelphia. Film and event production in LA. Logistics in Chicago and Dallas. Retail and hospitality in NYC and Miami.
